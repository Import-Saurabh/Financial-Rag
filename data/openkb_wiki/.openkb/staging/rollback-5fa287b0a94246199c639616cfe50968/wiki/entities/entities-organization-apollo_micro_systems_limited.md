# Apollo Micro Systems Limited

[[entities/entities-organization-apollo_micro_systems_limited]] is an Indian defence and aerospace technology company founded in 1985 and headquartered in Hyderabad. It specializes in delivering mission-critical electronic and electromechanical solutions for missile systems, naval platforms, avionics, space systems, and homeland security.

## Key Facts

- **Business Transformation:** Reported a transformational financial performance in FY 2024-25 with a 51.24% growth in consolidated revenue from operations (₹562.07 crore), a 54% surge in EBITDA (₹132 crore), and an 81.18% increase in net profit.
- **Strategic Acquisitions:** Advanced toward becoming a fully integrated Tier-1 defence OEM through the acquisition of [[entities/entities-organization-idl_explosives_limited]] via its subsidiary [[entities/entities-organization-apollo_defence_industries_private_limited]].
- **Defence Participation:** Involved as a subsystem partner in over 60 Defence Capital Procurement Programmes, aligning closely with national indigenization initiatives like [[concepts/concepts-defense_indigenization]] and [[concepts/concepts-tier_1_defense_oem_integration]].
- **Strategic Partnerships:** Collaborates with major public sector enterprises and research institutions such as [[entities/entities-organization-drdo]], [[entities/entities-organization-bel]], [[entities/entities-organization-grse]], and [[entities/entities-organization-bdl]].
- **Leadership:** Led by Managing Director karunakar reddy baddam, Wholetime Director chandrapati venkata siva prasad, and Chairman raghupathy g theegala.
