# Arunodaya Trust

Arunodaya Trust is a charitable organization partnering with [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] to advance environmental sustainability initiatives. During the financial year 2024-25, the trust received ₹70 Lakhs in corporate social responsibility (CSR) contributions from [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]], as detailed in [[summaries/2025_Financial_Year_2025_from_bse_part2]].

## Key Activities

* **Environmental Sustainability:** Utilizes CSR allocations from [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] to support ecological and environmental projects aligned with [[concepts/corporate-social-responsibility]].
