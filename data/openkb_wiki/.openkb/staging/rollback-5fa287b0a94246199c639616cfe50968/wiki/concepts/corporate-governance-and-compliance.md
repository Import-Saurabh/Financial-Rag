# Corporate Governance and Compliance

Corporate governance and regulatory compliance form a core pillar of operations for [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]], ensuring transparency, accountability, and adherence to statutory mandates under the Companies Act, 2013, and SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015. Detailed insights and statutory findings are documented in [[summaries/2025_Financial_Year_2025_from_bse_part2]].

## Governance Structure and Board Oversight

The company is governed by an eight-member Board of Directors that combines executive, non-executive, and independent directors. This structure ensures objective oversight and strategic guidance. Key board committees—including the Audit Committee, Nomination and Remuneration Committee, Stakeholders Relationship Committee, and Risk Management Committee—actively monitor internal controls, financial reporting, and risk management frameworks.

## Statutory Disclosures and Compliance

As outlined in [[summaries/2025_Financial_Year_2025_from_bse_part2]], the company maintains rigorous internal controls, whistleblower mechanisms, and policies under the Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Act. Secretarial audits and corporate governance certificates—such as those issued by practicing company secretaries—validate full compliance with listing agreements, insider trading regulations, and related-party transaction norms under Form AOC-2.
