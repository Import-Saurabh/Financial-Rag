# Apollo Defence Industries Private Limited

Apollo Defence Industries Private Limited is a corporate entity and subsidiary utilized by [[entities/entities-organization-apollo_micro_systems_limited]] to execute strategic acquisitions and drive vertical integration in the defence sector.

## Key Facts
- **Strategic Role:** Acts as the investment and acquisition vehicle for [[entities/entities-organization-apollo_micro_systems_limited]] to scale operations and expand its product portfolio into complete weapon platforms.
- **Acquisitions:** Spearheaded the strategic acquisition of [[entities/entities-organization-idl_explosives_limited]] to advance toward Tier-1 defence OEM status.
- **Related Party Transactions:** Identified in official notice and statutory reports for material related party transactions and financial arrangements up to ₹300 crores and loan/guarantee approvals up to ₹1,500 crores.
