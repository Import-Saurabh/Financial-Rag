# Defense Indigenization and Self-Reliance

Defense indigenization and self-reliance represent the strategic alignment of domestic manufacturers with national policies such as 'Atmanirbhar Bharat' and 'Make in India'. As detailed in [[summaries/2025_Financial_Year_2025_from_bse_part1]], these initiatives aim to reduce import dependence by prioritizing indigenous technology solutions and boosting domestic manufacturing capabilities within the aerospace and defense sectors.

## Strategic Importance and Industry Tailwinds

The operating environment for Indian defense enterprises is heavily shaped by policy frameworks like Positive Indigenisation Lists and the Defence Procurement Procedure. These regulatory tailwinds encourage public and private sector collaboration to co-develop advanced weapon systems, missile electronics, and naval platforms.

Companies such as [[entities/entities-organization-apollo_micro_systems_limited]] actively participate in numerous Defence Capital Procurement Programmes.
