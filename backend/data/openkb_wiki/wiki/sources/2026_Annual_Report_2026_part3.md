of the nation's long-term commitment to military

₹50,000 crore in exports by 2029, the runway 
ahead for capable Indian private sector players 
is substantial.
	
	
Three structural forces underpin this momentum.

converted self-reliance from a policy preference

a

execution, with orders now flowing to the private 
sector at scale. Third, India's credibility as a 
neutral, technology-capable partner has opened 
genuine co-development and co-production 
opportunities with friendly foreign nations across 
precision munitions, guided systems, C4ISR, and 
unmanned platforms positioning Indian firms 
within global defence supply chains rather than 
merely alongside them.

For a technology-led defence enterprise,

investments in indigenous weapon system 
technologies, with several programmes

Key achievements and focus areas during the

Mini Torpedo The Mini Torpedo is a compact, 
lightweight underwater weapon designed for 
engagement of small and mid-size sub-surface 
threats, 
including 
midget 
submarines 
and 
underwater swimmer delivery vehicles. Developed 
indigenously by Apollo Micro Systems Limited, 
the system integrates in-house acoustic homing, 
guidance electronics, actuation, and safety-and-
arming technologies into a single platform. Its 
reduced form factor enables deployment from 
a variety of launch platforms, including surface 
ships, helicopters, and unmanned systems. The 
programme leverages AMS's proven building 
blocks in signal processing and underwater 
sensing. It represents a critical capability in 
littoral and shallow-water warfare, an area of 
growing operational priority. The Mini Torpedo 
underscores the Company's evolution into a 
complete underwater weapon systems house.
	
	
Vehicle Mounted Counter Drone System The 
Vehicle Mounted Counter Drone System is a 
mobile, integrated solution for the detection, 
tracking, identification, and neutralisation of 
hostile unmanned aerial threats. Mounted on 
a high-mobility vehicle platform, the system 
combines radar, RF detection, and electro-
optical sensors with soft-kill and hard-kill 
countermeasures for layered protection. Its 
on-the-move capability makes it particularly 
suited 
for 
convoy 
protection, 
forward-
deployed formations, and mobile air defence 
gaps. The system architecture is modular and 
scalable, allowing configuration to specific user 
requirements. With drone warfare now central to 
the modern battlefield, this system addresses 
one of the most urgent operational needs of 
the Armed Forces. It reflects AMS's strength 
in sensor fusion, real-time processing, and 
system integration.
	
	
Limpet Mines Limpet Mines are compact 
underwater explosive devices designed to 
be covertly attached to the hulls of ships and 
maritime assets by combat divers and special 
forces. AMS's indigenously developed limpet 
mine integrates advanced safety-and-arming 
mechanisms, programmable time-delay fuzing, 
and anti-removal features to ensure both 
operational effectiveness and handler safety. 
The design emphasises reliability under extreme 
underwater conditions, ease of deployment, and 
secure arming protocols. Backward integration 
within the Apollo Group provides access to 
qualified energetic materials, enabling end-
to-end realisation of the weapon. The product 
addresses the specialised requirements of naval 
special operations and harbour denial missions. 
It further extends AMS's growing portfolio in 
underwater ordnance.
	
	
Moored Mine The Moored Mine is an indigenously 
developed sea mine anchored to the seabed 
and suspended at a pre-determined depth for 
area denial against surface and sub-surface 
targets. At its core is AMS's advanced multi-
influence signal processing system, capable 
of detecting acoustic, magnetic, and pressure 
signatures and discriminating genuine targets 
from countermeasures. The mine incorporates 
programmable target logic, secure arming, 
and 
extended 
underwater 
endurance 
for 
prolonged deployment. Designed for defensive 
mining of harbours, chokepoints, and strategic 
waters, it constitutes a potent sea-denial 
capability. The programme demonstrates the 
Company's mastery of underwater sensing, 
target classification, and fuzing technologies.
