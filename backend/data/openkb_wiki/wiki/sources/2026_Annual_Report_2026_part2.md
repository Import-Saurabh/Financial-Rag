Technology Development Fund (TDF)
The TDF is a Ministry of Defence instrument, developed 
to extend financial and technical support to public and 
private enterprises, with particular emphasis on startups 
and MSMEs, for the development of critical defence 
technologies. The scheme provides grants of up to 
₹50 crore per project. To date, several technologies 
have been successfully developed under TDF, and two 
project systems have been flown in space as part of 
PSLV missions. As of June 2026, 80 projects with a total 
cost of ₹334 crore are under implementation.
Chandrayaan Mission
After the success of Chandrayaan-3, making India the 
first country to achieve a soft landing near the Moon’s 
south pole in 2023, the nation’s lunar ambitions have 
continued to expand with further missions planned in 
the coming years. The Chandrayaan-4 is scheduled 
for 2027 with an objective of collecting lunar samples 
and returning them to Earth. Further ahead, the 
Chandrayaan-5 mission will explore water and other 
volatile materials near the lunar south pole.
GAGAN
GAGAN (GPS Aided GEO Augmented Navigation) is an 
indigenous Satellite-Based Augmentation System co-
developed by ISRO and the Airports Authority of India. 
The platform enhances GPS accuracy and provides 
vital integrity information for safer aircraft navigation. 
Globally certified, GAGAN facilitates precise satellite-
based 
landings 
and 
supports 
operations 
across 
sectors beyond commercial aviation. By strengthening 
sovereign navigation infrastructure, it is driving India’s 
strategic push toward technological self-reliance and 
global prominence in satellite navigation.
National Space Station
The Bharatiya Antariksh Station (BAS), a key pillar of 
Space Vision 2047, is India’s planned five-module space 
station in Low Earth Orbit engineered for long-duration 
human spaceflight and advanced scientific research. In 
September 2024, the Union Cabinet formally approved 
the development and launch of its first module, BAS-01, 
by 2028 as part of the expanded Gaganyaan program. The 
platform is set to enable vital research across life sciences, 
medicine, and pioneering technologies, while supporting 
the nation’s long-term deep-space exploration goals.
Mission DefSpace
Mission DefSpace operates under the iDEX framework to 
promote indigenous development of dual-use technologies 
for India’s defence-space ecosystem. The programme 
has introduced 75 Defence Space Challenges across five 
domains, including launch systems, satellite systems, 
communication and payload systems, ground systems and 
software systems. This has enabled startups, MSMEs and 
research institutions to co-develop technologies aligned 
with the operational requirements of the Defence Space 
Agency and the Indian armed forces. Mission DefSpace 
represents a deliberate effort to mobilise private sector 
capability in a domain that had previously been the 
exclusive preserve of government agencies.
Private Sector Incentivisation

private sector’s contribution to the space ecosystem.

Investment policy, the IN-SPACe Seed Fund Scheme and 
the Pre-incubation Entrepreneurship Programme, alongside 
the pivotal rollout of the ₹1,000 crore Venture Capital Fund 
and the ₹500 crore Technology Adoption Fund.13

Sustained growth in India’s defence budget and capital 
acquisition outlay, with the Government targeting ₹3

Threats
Dependence on government procurement cycles, 
where order finalisation and execution timelines can 
be long and uneven; intensifying competition from 
established DPSUs and new private sector entrants;

developments that may disrupt supply chains or alter 
procurement priorities.
Company Overview
The Next Chapter of Integration
Apollo Micro Systems Limited is a pioneer in defence 
technology, specialising in the design, development and 
manufacture of advanced electronic, electro-mechanical 
and engineering systems for India’s Armed Forces. With 
multi-domain, multi-disciplinary capabilities and robust

electronics for ground, missile and naval defence applications.
