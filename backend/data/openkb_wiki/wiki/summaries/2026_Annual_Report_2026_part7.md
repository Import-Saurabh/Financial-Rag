# Summary: 2026 Annual Report Part 7 — Notes to Standalone Financial Statements

This document is Part 7 of the annual report of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] for the financial year 2025-26. It contains the accounting policies and detailed notes forming part of the standalone financial statements.

## Key Accounting Policies

- **Revenue Recognition**: Revenue from supply of electronics and electro-mechanical systems is recognised using the five-step model under Ind AS 115. Revenue is recognised over time for performance obligations satisfied over time (using input/output method) or at a point in time when control transfers. [[concepts/revenue-recognition]]
- **Financial Instruments**: Covers classification and measurement of financial assets (amortised cost, FVTOCI, FVTPL) and financial liabilities. Includes impairment using expected credit loss (ECL) model. [[concepts/financial-instruments]]
- **Foreign Currency Transactions**: Transactions recorded at exchange rates on transaction date; monetary items translated at closing rate; exchange differences recognised in profit or loss, except for certain equity instruments and cash flow hedges in OCI.
- **Property, Plant and Equipment (PPE)**: Measured at cost less accumulated depreciation and impairment. Depreciation on straight-line basis over useful lives prescribed in Schedule II or technical assessment. Useful lives include buildings 30 years, plant & machinery 15 years, etc. [[concepts/property-plant-and-equipment]]
- **Intangible Assets**: Amortised over 5 years on straight-line basis.
- **Leases**: As lessee, recognises right-of-use (ROU) assets and lease liabilities. Short-term and low-value leases expensed. As lessor, operating and finance lease accounting.
- **Income Taxes**: Current tax and deferred tax using balance sheet approach. MAT credit recognised when convincing evidence exists.
- **Provisions and Contingencies**: Provisions recognised when present obligation probable and reliably estimable. Contingent liabilities disclosed unless remote; contingent assets not recognised.
- **Government Grants**: Recognised when reasonable assurance of compliance and receipt. Revenue grants in other income; asset-related grants over useful life.
- **Earnings Per Share**: Basic and diluted EPS presented.
- **Cash Flow Statement**: Prepared using indirect method.
- **Investment Property**: Measured at cost less depreciation and impairment.
- **Research and Development**: Research expensed; development capitalised if criteria met.
- **Borrowing Costs**: Directly attributable borrowing costs capitalised; others expensed.

- **Other Bank Balances**: ₹6,419.04 lakhs, including restricted balances.
- **Share Capital**: Increased to ₹3,572.92 lakhs (35,72,92,440 shares of ₹1 each) due to preferential issue and warrant conversion.

The Company adopted amendments to Ind AS 1 (Classification of Liabilities as Current or Non-current and Non-current Liabilities with Covenants) from 1 April 2025. New pronouncements effective 1 April 2026 regarding loan covenants are also discussed.
