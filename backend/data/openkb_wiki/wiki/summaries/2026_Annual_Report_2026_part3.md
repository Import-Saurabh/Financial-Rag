# Summary: 2026 Annual Report of Apollo Micro Systems Limited — Part 3

This document is part 3 of the FY2025-26 annual report of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]]. It contains related party transaction (RPT) disclosures, the Directors' Report, CSR annexure, and the Secretarial Audit Report.

## Related Party Transactions (RPT) Disclosures
- The company has two subsidiaries: [[entities/apollo_defence_industries_private_limited]] and [[entities/ananya_sip_rf_technologies_private_limited]], and two step-down subsidiaries: [[entities/apollo_strategic_technologies_private_limited]] and [[entities/idl_explosives]]. [[entities/shauryastra_defence_systems_private_limited]] is also listed.
- All loans to these entities are unsecured, repayable on demand, with interest at 9.90% p.a.
- IDL Explosives Limited has guarantee obligations of Rs. 300 Crores.
- None of the related parties are classified as NPA, wilful defaulters, or undergoing insolvency.
- Debt Service Coverage Ratio is negative for most entities; Debt to Equity Ratio cannot be computed due to omnibus approval.

## Directors' Report FY2025-26

### Financial Results
- Standalone revenue from operations: ₹76,485.32 lakhs (previous year ₹56,206.92 lakhs).
- Consolidated revenue from operations: ₹90,432.38 lakhs.
- Standalone EBITDA: ₹23,122.03 lakhs (+73.50%); consolidated EBITDA: ₹22,443.64 lakhs (+69.91%).
- Standalone PBT: ₹17,462.69 lakhs; consolidated PBT: ₹15,479.76 lakhs.
- Standalone PAT: ₹12,057.89 lakhs (+110.65%); consolidated PAT: ₹10,738.04 lakhs (+90.53%).
- EPS: standalone basic ₹3.57, diluted ₹3.53; consolidated basic ₹3.35, diluted ₹3.31.

### Business Outlook
- Indian defence production reached ₹1.78 lakh crore in FY2025-26; defence exports ₹38,424 crore (+62.66%); defence budget for 2026-27 is ₹7.85 lakh crore.
- Government targets ₹3 lakh crore annual defence production and ₹50,000 crore exports by 2029.
- Key policies: Aatmanirbhar Bharat and the wider push for defence indigenization, DAP 2020, positive indigenisation lists, iDEX, TDF, Make-I/II.
- AMS is transitioning from a subsystem supplier to a prime contractor in precision-guided munitions, underwater weapon systems, embedded guidance and control, and smart energetics.

### Research & Development
- R&D investment: ₹7,253.39 lakhs.
- Key indigenous products: [[entities/mini_torpedo]], [[entities/vehicle_mounted_counter_drone_system]], [[entities/limpet_mines]], [[entities/moored_mine]], and the [[entities/asw_weapon_platform]].
- Awarded a Make-II project by the Indian Army; participated in Tri-Services Make proposals.
- Focus areas: terminal guidance, seekers, safety-and-arming mechanisms, jam-resistant navigation.

### Credit Rating
- Long-term: ACUITE A- Stable; Short-term: ACUITE A2+.

### Share Capital
- Authorised share capital: ₹45,00,00,000 (45 crore equity shares of ₹1).
- Allotted 2,70,42,894 equity shares at ₹114 (premium ₹113) on preferential basis; 2,37,59,986 shares on warrant conversion.
- Paid-up capital as on 31 March 2026: ₹35,72,92,440 (35,72,92,440 equity shares of ₹1).
- 1,43,07,072 convertible warrants pending conversion.

### Dividend
- Recommended dividend of 25% (₹0.25 per equity share), cash outflow ~₹9,28,99,878.
- Unclaimed dividend transferred to IEPF.

### Material Changes
- Ms. Rukhya Parveen resigned as Company Secretary; Mr. G. Seshadri Vasan appointed effective 1 June 2026.
- Share Purchase Agreement to acquire 41.33% of [[entities/premier_explosives_limited]] for ~₹1,550 crore; open offer for 26% at ₹698 per share (~₹975.64 crore).
- Acquisition strengthens defence systems and energetic materials, consistent with [[concepts/defence-acquisitions-and-integration]] and [[concepts/defence-energetics]].

### Other Disclosures
- Loans given to subsidiaries: Ananya SIP RF ₹374.79 lakhs; Apollo Defence Industries ₹23,002.84 lakhs; Apollo Strategic Technologies ₹12.10 lakhs; IDL Explosives ₹21,755.60 lakhs.
- No deposits accepted; no fraud reported; no significant regulatory orders.
- CSR spent ₹74.80 lakhs against obligation of ₹68.06 lakhs.
- Secretarial Audit observations: ROC show cause notice for delay in CRA-4 filing (compounded with ₹55,000 fee); NSE penalty of ₹88,500 for non-submission of audit report with financial results.
- BRSR applicable as company is in top 1,000 listed entities.
- Employees: 347 male, 87 female.

## CSR Annexure A
