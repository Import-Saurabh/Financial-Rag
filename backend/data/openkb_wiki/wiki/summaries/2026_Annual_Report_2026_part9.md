# Apollo Micro Systems Limited – Annual Report 2025-26, Part 9

This document part contains the **Independent Auditor's Report**, the **Consolidated Financial Statements** (Balance Sheet, Statement of Profit and Loss, Statement of Cash Flows, Statement of Changes in Equity), and the **Notes forming part of the Consolidated Financial Statements** (corporate information and material accounting policies) for the year ended 31 March 2026.

## Independent Auditor's Report

**Auditor:** [[entities/s_t_mohite_and_co]], Chartered Accountants (ICAI Firm Registration No. 011410S), signed by [[entities/hima_bindu_sagala]], Partner (Membership No. 231056), dated 18 May 2026, Hyderabad.

**Opinion:** The consolidated financial statements give a true and fair view in conformity with Ind AS, of the consolidated state of affairs as at 31 March 2026, and of the consolidated profit, changes in equity, and cash flows for the year then ended.

### Key Audit Matters (KAMs)

1. **Revenue Recognition** – Application of [[concepts/revenue-recognition]] involves significant judgements on identification of distinct performance obligations, determination of transaction price, and measurement of revenue recognised over time. Procedures included reviewing Ind AS 115 implementation, testing internal controls, reading contracts, and evaluating management estimates.
2. **Capital Work in Progress (CWIP)** – CWIP aggregating ₹19,629.40 lakhs (testing tools/instruments ₹2,341.49 lakhs; civil works ₹17,287.91 lakhs), including ₹7,512.88 lakhs of costs where judgement was applied on capitalisation criteria under [[concepts/property-plant-and-equipment]]. Procedures included testing design and operating effectiveness of controls, substantive testing of additions, and review of physical verification reports.
3. **Inventories** – Inventories of ₹79,528.92 lakhs (47.76% of total current assets, 33.58% of total assets) carry risk of obsolescence because they are customer-specific. Procedures included assessing controls, reviewing management judgements on obsolescence, sample testing of cost accumulation, and comparing cost to net realisable value.
4. **Accounting for Business Combination – Goodwill on Provisional Basis** – The Group acquired 100% of [[entities/idl_explosives]] through a share acquisition, accounted as a business combination under [[concepts/ind-as-103-business-combinations]]. Because IDL's identifiable liabilities exceeded its identifiable assets, goodwill of ₹14,517.66 lakhs was recognised on a provisional basis (book values), pending a Purchase Price Allocation (PPA) exercise within the twelve-month measurement period. Procedures included evaluating the terms of the share agreement, testing acquisition journal entries, and assessing management's future profitability assumptions.

### Other Reporting Matters

- No material misstatement found in Other Information.
- Proper books of account maintained, except for matters under Rule 11(g).
- The consolidated financial statements comply with Ind AS under Section 133 of the Companies Act, 2013.
- No director is disqualified under Section 164(2).
- [[concepts/internal-financial-controls]] over financial reporting were adequate and operating effectively (Annexure A, unmodified opinion).
- No qualifications or adverse remarks in CARO reports.
- The Group had no long-term contracts including derivative contracts with material foreseeable losses.

## Notes forming part of the Consolidated Financial Statements

### 1. Corporate Information
[[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] (the Holding Company) was incorporated on 3 March 1997, converted to a public limited company on 1 April 2017, and is listed on the [[entities/bse_limited]] and the [[entities/national_stock_exchange_of_india]]. The Group supplies electronics and electro-mechanical systems and components, including design, R&D of systems used in missile programmes, underwater missile programmes, avionics, ship-borne and submarine systems.

### 2. Basis of Preparation and Measurement
- Prepared in accordance with Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS) under Section 133 of the Companies Act, 2013, and Division II of Schedule III.
- Prepared on a going concern basis; approved by the Board on 18 May 2026.
- Consolidation procedure includes combining like items, eliminating intragroup transactions, and attributing profit or loss to owners and non-controlling interests.
- **Subsidiaries consolidated:** [[entities/ananya_sip_rf_technologies_private_limited]] (51%), [[entities/apollo_defence_industries_private_limited]] (76%), [[entities/apollo_strategic_technologies_private_limited]] (step-down, 51%), and [[entities/idl_explosives]] (step-down, 100%, acquired during the year).
- Measurement based on historical cost, except certain financial assets/liabilities at fair value, long-term borrowings at amortised cost, and defined benefit plans at fair value.
- Fair value hierarchy uses Level 1, 2, and 3 inputs.

### 3. Material Accounting Policies
- **Revenue:** Recognised on transfer of control using the five-step model of [[concepts/revenue-recognition]]; over-time revenue measured using input/output method.
- **Other income:** Interest, rental, government grants, dividend, export incentives, and other income.
- **Foreign currency:** Transactions at spot rates; monetary items retranslated at closing rates; differences recognised in profit or loss (except certain OCI items).
- **Financial instruments:** Classified at amortised cost, FVTOCI, or FVTPL under Ind AS 109; expected credit loss (ECL) model for impairment, with simplified approach for trade receivables.
- **Cash and cash equivalents:** Short-term, highly liquid investments with original maturities of three months or less.
- **Property, plant and equipment:** Measured at cost less accumulated depreciation and impairment; depreciation on straight-line basis over useful lives (buildings 30 years, plant and machinery 15 years, etc.).
- **Intangible assets:** Amortised over five years.
- **Capital Work in Progress (CWIP):** Costs accumulated until assets are ready for intended use; includes eligible borrowing costs under Ind AS 23.
- **Inventories:** Valued at lower of cost (weighted average) and net realisable value.
- **Impairment of non-financial assets:** Reviewed at each reporting date; recoverable amount is higher of value in use and fair value less costs to sell.
- **Goodwill:** Initially at cost; tested annually for impairment at cash-generating unit level.
- **Employee benefits:** Short-term, defined contribution, defined benefit (projected unit credit method), termination benefits, compensated absences, and share-based payments.
- **Leases:** Right-of-use assets and lease liabilities recognised under Ind AS 116; short-term and low-value lease exemptions applied.
- **Income taxes:** Current tax at enacted rates; deferred tax using balance sheet approach on temporary differences.
