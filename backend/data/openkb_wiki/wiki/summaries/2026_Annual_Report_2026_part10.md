# Apollo Micro Systems Limited – Consolidated Financial Statements Notes (FY 2025-26)

This document is part 10 of the FY 2025-26 annual report of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]]. It contains the notes forming part of the [[concepts/consolidated-financial-statements|consolidated financial statements]], covering significant accounting policies (notes 3.15–3.25) and detailed notes to accounts (notes 4–40). All amounts are in ₹ lakhs unless stated otherwise.

## Accounting Policies (Notes 3.15–3.25)

- **[[concepts/provisions-and-contingent-liabilities|Provisions and contingent liabilities]] (3.15)**: Provisions are recognised when a present legal or constructive obligation from a past event is probable and reliably estimable. Contingent liabilities are disclosed unless outflow is remote; contingent assets are not recognised but disclosed when inflow is probable. Discounting is used when the time value of money is material, with unwinding recognised as finance cost.
- **[[concepts/government-grants|Government grants]] (3.16)**: Recognised when there is reasonable assurance of compliance and receipt. Revenue grants are recognised as other income; grants for additional depreciation are recognised over the asset's useful life. Below-market-rate government loans are treated as grants, measured at fair value with deferred income released over the loan tenure.
- **[[concepts/earnings-per-share|Earnings per share]] (3.17)**: Basic EPS = net profit attributable to equity shareholders / weighted average equity shares. Diluted EPS adjusts for dilutive potential equity shares.
- **Statement of cash flows (3.18)**: Indirect method; cash flows segregated into operating, investing, and financing activities.
- **Investment property (3.19)**: Initially at cost; subsequently at cost less accumulated depreciation and impairment. Depreciation on buildings is on a straight-line basis per Schedule II of the Companies Act, 2013. Derecognition gains/losses are recognised in profit or loss.
- **Research and development (3.20)**: Research expenditure is expensed. Development expenditure is capitalised only if costs are reliably measurable, technical and commercial feasibility is established, future economic benefits are probable, and the Company has sufficient resources.
- **Borrowing costs (3.21)**: Borrowing costs directly attributable to qualifying assets are capitalised; other borrowing costs are expensed.
- **Events after reporting date (3.22)**: Adjusting events are recognised; material non-adjusting events are disclosed.
- **Cash dividend to equity holders (3.23)**: Liability recognised when distribution is authorised and no longer at the Company's discretion; corresponding amount recognised directly in other equity.
- **Changes in material accounting policies (3.24)**: Adopted amendments to Ind AS 1 on classification of liabilities as current/non-current and non-current liabilities with covenants from 1 April 2025, applied retrospectively.
- **New accounting pronouncements (3.25)**: MCA notified amendments; the Company applied relevant amendments effective 1 April 2024. Ind AS 1 amendments on covenant breaches are effective from 1 April 2026 and applied retrospectively.
