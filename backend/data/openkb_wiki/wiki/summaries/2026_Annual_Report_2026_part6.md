# 2026 Annual Report part 6 — BRSR Principles 6–9, Auditor's Report & Standalone Financials

Part 6 of the FY2025-26 annual report of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] covers the tail of the Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report ([[concepts/business-responsibility-and-sustainability-reporting]]), the Independent Auditor's Report, and the audited standalone financial statements.

## BRSR — Principle 6 (Environment)

- **Energy**: total energy consumed 7,97,528.15 kWh (FY 2025-26) vs 7,87,512.89 kWh (FY 2024-25), entirely from non-renewable electricity; zero renewable or fuel consumption. Energy intensity per rupee of turnover improved to 0.0001043 from 0.0001401.
- **Designated Consumers**: the Company has no sites identified as Designated Consumers under the PAT scheme.
- **Water**: total withdrawal and consumption of 3,589.01 kL, all from third-party water; nil discharge; no zero liquid discharge mechanism, as manufacturing produces no significant liquid waste.
- **Air emissions**: NOx, SOx, particulate matter, POP, VOC and HAP all reported as Nil.
- **GHG emissions**: Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions reported as Nil; no GHG-reduction project.
- **Waste**: plastic and e-waste negligible; no other waste streams. The Company states its segment generates no waste or residue.
- No operations in ecologically sensitive areas, no Environmental Impact Assessments undertaken, and full compliance with Indian environmental laws.

## BRSR — Principle 7 (Public policy)

Two affiliations with trade and industry chambers: PHD Chamber of Commerce and Industry, [[entities/society_of_indian_defence_manufacturers]], and ICC — a minor internal inconsistency ("two" stated, three listed). No adverse orders on anti-competitive conduct.

## BRSR — Principle 8 (Inclusive growth)

No Social Impact Assessments or Rehabilitation & Resettlement projects. Grievance redressal runs through a feedback form and departmental email contacts on the website. Inputs sourced directly from MSMEs/small producers: 1.44%; directly from within India: 86.36%. 100% of wage cost is in urban locations.

## BRSR — Principle 9 (Consumers)

Consumer grievances are handled via website contact details. No complaints (data privacy, advertising, cyber-security, delivery, trade practices), no product recalls, and no data breaches. The Company maintains a framework on cyber security and data privacy ([[concepts/corporate-governance-and-compliance]]), though no web-link is disclosed.

## Independent Auditor's Report

[[entities/s_t_mohite_and_co]] (CA. [[entities/hima_bindu_sagala]], ICAI FRN 011410S, UDIN 26231056YFSVJL3298, dated 18 May 2026) issued an **unqualified opinion** on the standalone financial statements, with an adequate internal financial controls opinion in Annexure B ([[concepts/internal-financial-controls]]).

The **[[concepts/key-audit-matters]]** identified were:

1. **Revenue recognition (Ind AS 115)** — judgements on performance obligations, transaction price and revenue timing.
2. **Capital work-in-progress** — ₹17,059.72 lakhs, comprising testing tools/facility expansion (₹2,341.49 lakhs) and civil works (₹14,718.23 lakhs); ₹7,512.88 lakhs specifically judged against Ind AS 16 recognition criteria.
3. **Inventories** — ₹76,000.75 lakhs (50.53% of current assets, 33.13% of total assets) with [[concepts/inventory-obsolescence]] risk; controls over valuation, ageing and provisioning reviewed.
4. **[[concepts/related-party-transactions]]** — loans, property sale/lease and subsidiary investments; arm's-length pricing and Section 177/185/186/188 compliance assessed.
5. **Accounting for business combination** — acquisition of 100% of [[entities/idl_explosives]], which became a wholly-owned step-down subsidiary and was accounted under [[concepts/ind-as-103-business-combinations]] ([[concepts/defence-acquisitions-and-integration]]). Because identifiable liabilities exceeded identifiable assets, provisional goodwill of ₹14,517.66 lakhs was recognised, with purchase price allocation pending within the 12-month measurement period.

## Annexure A — Companies (Auditor's Report) Order highlights

- PPE/right-of-use registers maintained, though the Company plans improvements; physical verification on a three-year cycle; no revaluation; no benami proceedings.
- Inventories physically verified with no material discrepancies; working capital limits above ₹5 crores secured on current assets.
- Loans to subsidiaries/step-down subsidiaries: ₹29,769.14 lakhs granted during the year and ₹31,555.75 lakhs outstanding; loans to others ₹699.55 lakhs. Some interest overdue (₹52.41 lakhs), provision of ₹58.71 lakhs made.
- **Statutory dues disputes**: income-tax disputes across AY 2015-16 to AY 2018-19 totalling ₹834.36 lakhs before the Commissioner of Income-tax (Appeals).
- Share capital activity: 2,37,59,986 warrants converted into equity at ₹113 premium (₹27,086.38 lakhs) and preferential allotment of 2,70,42,894 shares (₹30,828.90 lakhs).
- No fraud, no whistle-blower complaints, no Nidhi/RBI registration, no cash losses, no auditor resignation.
- **[[concepts/audit-trail-rule-11g]]**: the audit trail operated throughout the year and was not tampered with, though the report notes a qualification on books of account under paragraph 2(i)(vi).
- **[[concepts/corporate-social-responsibility]]**: ₹74.80 lakhs spent against a ₹105.43 lakhs obligation, offset by prior-year excess; ₹6.54 lakhs carried forward with no unspent amount requiring transfer.

- The Company is incorporated (3 March 1997), listed on [[entities/bse_limited]] and [[entities/national_stock_exchange_of_india]], headquartered at IDA Mallapur, Hyderabad, and supplies electronics and electro-mechanical systems for missile, underwater, avionics, ship-borne and submarine programmes.

## Key takeaways

- A clean audit opinion masks a year of very large cash absorption and balance-sheet leverage to fund inventories, CWIP and subsidiary loans.
- The IDL Explosives acquisition with provisional goodwill is the most judgement-heavy item and may trigger retrospective adjustments.
- Environmental disclosures are effectively nil across emissions, waste and water discharge, consistent with a design/assembly-oriented defence electronics business.
- Governance and consumer-responsibility indicators are strong (no complaints, recalls or breaches), but disclosure quality is mixed — e.g. the chamber-count inconsistency and absent cyber-security policy link.
