# Apollo Micro Systems Limited – 2026 Annual Report, Part 8: Standalone Financial Statements Notes

## Overview
This document is part 8 of the FY2025-26 annual report of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]], comprising Notes 18–53 to the standalone financial statements. It covers balance sheet liabilities, provisions, tax, revenue and expenses, related-party transactions, leases, employee benefits, segment reporting, CSR, MSME disclosures, financial instruments, risk management, capital management, ratio analysis, and regulatory compliance. Amounts are in ₹ lakhs unless stated.

## Key balance sheet notes
- **Lease liabilities**: closing balance ₹360.58 lakhs (FY25: ₹458.44), split into non-current ₹192.07 and current ₹168.51. Movement: opening ₹458.44, new leases ₹44.29, interest ₹42.26, payments ₹(184.41). See [[concepts/leases]] and Ind AS 116.
- **Provisions**: non-current provisions ₹189.66 lakhs (gratuity ₹160.04, compensated absences ₹29.62); current provisions ₹24.42 lakhs (gratuity ₹13.85, compensated absences ₹10.57).
- **Deferred tax liability (net)**: ₹5,683.16 lakhs (FY25: ₹4,073.81), driven by [[concepts/property-plant-and-equipment]] temporary difference ₹5,721.80; employee benefits deferred tax asset ₹38.64. See [[concepts/deferred-tax]].
- **Trade payables**: ₹35,647.85 lakhs (FY25: ₹27,988.00), including MSME dues ₹83.48 and other dues ₹35,564.37. Ageing shows most not due (₹35,081.27). See msme disclosures and trade payables.
- **Other financial liabilities**: ₹2,108.92 lakhs (FY25: ₹541.15), mainly creditors for capital goods ₹1,797.92.
- **Other current liabilities**: ₹718.61 lakhs (FY25: ₹772.75), including advance from customers/contract liabilities ₹560.16 and statutory deductions ₹158.45.
- **Current tax liabilities**: ₹3,952.60 lakhs (FY25: ₹2,134.56).

- **Other income**: ₹1,733.71 lakhs (FY25: ₹404.83), mainly interest on loans ₹1,363.82 and bank deposits ₹323.22, plus corporate guarantee commission ₹16.67.
- **Cost of materials consumed**: ₹59,246.52 lakhs (FY25: ₹43,567.13). Inventory change: negative ₹(9,520.40) lakhs due to higher WIP and finished goods.
- **Employee benefits expense**: ₹2,688.95 lakhs (FY25: ₹2,183.45), including salaries/wages ₹2,171.32 and managerial remuneration ₹228.00. See [[concepts/employee-benefits]].
- **Depreciation and amortisation**: ₹1,948.88 lakhs (FY25: ₹1,531.94), comprising PPE ₹1,626.10, ROU assets ₹156.88, and intangibles ₹165.90.
- **Finance costs**: ₹3,710.46 lakhs (FY25: ₹3,414.35) after capitalising ₹1,158.96 into qualifying assets.
- **Other expenses**: ₹2,681.93 lakhs (FY25: ₹1,404.97), including foreign exchange loss ₹432.32, contractual commitments ₹408.70, and expected credit losses on receivables/loans/advances (total ₹261.38).

## Key takeaways
The notes show strong revenue and profit growth, improved debt-equity and net profit ratio, increased trade payables and borrowings, growing subsidiary loans, a single-segment domestic business, and continued exposure to interest-rate, credit, and foreign-exchange risks. The company remains compliant with CSR, MSME, lease, tax, and regulatory disclosure requirements.
