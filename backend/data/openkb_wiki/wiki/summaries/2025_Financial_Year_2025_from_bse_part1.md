- **Company Overview & Expertise:** Founded in 1985 and headquartered in Hyderabad, AMS is a provider of mission-critical electronic and electromechanical solutions for India's defence and aerospace sectors, supporting applications in missile systems, naval platforms, avionics, space systems, and homeland security.
