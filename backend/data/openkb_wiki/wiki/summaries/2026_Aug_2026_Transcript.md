# MTAR Technologies — Q1 FY27 Earnings Call Transcript (30 July 2026)

**Source type:** Regulatory disclosure + verbatim earnings call transcript
**Filed:** 6 August 2026 to [[entities/bse_limited]] (Scrip 543270) and [[entities/national_stock_exchange_of_india]] (Symbol: MTARTECH) under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015; signed by [[entities/priyanka-agarwal]], Company Secretary & Compliance Officer, in line with [[concepts/corporate-governance-and-compliance]] and the [[entities/securities_and_exchange_board_of_india]] framework.

- [[entities/srinivas-reddy]] — Managing Director & Promoter

## Headline Message
Management framed [[entities/mtar-technologies]] as being at an **inflection point**: all three core verticals — civil nuclear power, aerospace & defense, and clean energy — are simultaneously entering volume-growth phases, backed by a record order book and a multi-year capacity build-out. Guidance was reiterated (80% revenue growth, 24% ±100 bps EBITDA margin) with an explicit statement of confidence in **beating** it.

## Order Book and Growth Visibility
- Closing order book at end-Q1 FY27: **INR5,143 crore** — already above the INR5,000 crore FY27 closing target set a quarter earlier (see [[summaries/2025_Aug_2025_Transcript]]).
- A further **INR800 crore** of orders was announced on the call day (one participant referenced ~INR3,100 crore; the CFO clarified the incremental amount was INR800 crore on top of the ~INR5,100 crore base).
- Management stressed the book is **short-cycle** — tranches of 2 months to 2 years, not 5–10 year programmes — so continuous inflows matter more than the headline number. See [[concepts/order-book-and-revenue-visibility]] and [[concepts/revenue-recognition]].

## Segment Deep-Dive

### Civil Nuclear Power
- **Highest-ever order inflows** in the segment during the quarter, led by Kaiga 5 & 6 (two reactors).
- Segment order book: INR684 crore today, plus ~INR130–140 crore expected in the current quarter (L1 position) → **~INR800–815 crore**, which management called unprecedented in the company's history.
- Refurbishment of existing reactors: ~INR200+ crore total opportunity; orders expected during the current quarter; execution within 2 years.
- Kaiga 5 & 6 execution: 1 to 3–3.5 years; overall nuclear execution begins in **H2 FY27** and ramps from there.
- **[[entities/pfbr]]** recently achieved criticality — MTAR supplied the majority of critical assemblies for IGCAR/Bhavini, working directly with the government rather than through EPC vendors; additional fast breeder units are being scoped.
- **[[entities/mahi-banswara]]** (4 × 700 MW, NTPC–[[entities/npcil]] JV): tenders floated; MTAR would participate through EPC vendors; opportunity larger than Kaiga 5 & 6 given double the reactor count; orders likely in the **next financial year**.
- Wallet share of 60–70% on NPCIL/Department of Atomic Energy reactor orders, plus a Government of India target of **100 GW nuclear capacity by 2047**.
- Explicit claim that the vertical has moved to a more consistent trajectory with **reduced cyclicality** versus 5–10 years ago. See [[concepts/civil-nuclear-power]].

### Clean Energy
- Record order inflows in the quarter, strengthening an already robust book.
- Fuel cell capacity expansion in **three phases**: Phase 1 commissioned; Phase 2 by September–October 2026; Phase 3 a **multifold** expansion commissioned by March 2027, with volume ramp-up from April 2027.
- Specific capacity numbers withheld under NDA; the call referenced the previously guided 20,000 hot-box capacity as the base.
- Manpower training and automation initiatives are being run **ahead of** capacity coming on stream. See [[concepts/capital-work-in-progress]] and [[concepts/property-plant-and-equipment]] for the asset build-out.
- New "products" revenue reached roughly **INR100 crore** in the quarter (~50% of the clean energy revenue run-rate) and is expected to keep growing, with H2 stronger than H1. Drivers include non-hot-box fuel-cell content, **ball screws** with export demand, and a major MNC aerospace ball-screw contract.
- Traction also building in hydropower and wind energy. Clean energy is expected to be the largest revenue driver by end of FY27. See [[concepts/clean-energy-and-fuel-cells]].

### Aerospace & Defense
- Revenue expected to **double in FY27**, with significant ramp-up from that base spread over 3–4 years.
- Domestic defense pipeline: actuator assemblies for **[[entities/lca-tejas]]** Mark-1A (programme sized at INR140–150 crore for MTAR), wing kits, and electromechanical actuators across programmes — total opportunity exceeding **INR250 crore**. See [[concepts/defense_indigenization]] and [[concepts/defense-program-supply-chains]].
- MNC aerospace: qualified first articles converting to volume, with **10–15x volume increases** versus first-article levels; multiple sub-units being created inside the existing dedicated aerospace facility; NADCAP-approved special processes facility cited as a differentiator for speed of customer-specific capability build.
- FY30 aspiration: aerospace at INR600–700 crore and the products business above INR1,000 crore. See [[concepts/tier_1_defense_oem_integration]].

### Data Center Infrastructure (new vertical)
- MTAR has entered **data center infrastructure solutions**, with the first batch of assemblies in qualification.
- Order in hand: **INR45 crore**, to be executed by February–March 2027.
- The customer's potential requirement is **8× the current order**, on a repeating annual basis (eight infrastructure sets per year), supporting a dedicated facility.
- Business is **export-only**; management dismissed talk of US data-center capex delays as "unwanted noise." See [[concepts/defense-exports-and-global-market-entry]] for the export orientation.

### Oil & Gas
- Dedicated facility operational by **October 2026**; first articles already delivered to one customer, with another customer's first articles in progress.

## Capital Allocation and Balance Sheet
- **Capex:** ~INR500 crore across FY27 and FY28 (70% clean energy, 30% other verticals, with fungibility). Q1 spend ~INR30–35 crore; INR80 crore was capitalised in the quarter from capital work-in-progress. Target asset turnover of 4–5x, and at least 6x on incremental capex.
- **Debt:** INR423.6 crore gross versus INR379 crore of investments (as of July-end) → effective net debt of only INR20–30 crore. Interest cost was INR16 crore for the quarter, expected to trend down QoQ.
- **Working capital:** 59 days versus 172 days for FY26 — inventory days cut from 208 to 145, receivable days from 140 to 82. Targets: below 100 days for FY27, and combined receivables + inventory around 200–220 days. Levers include renegotiated commercial and credit terms, delivery-linked billing, daily/weekly monitoring, and a targeted ~INR70 crore per year of GST refunds. See [[concepts/capital-management]].
- **Funding plan** for capex and incremental working capital: a blend of internal accruals and debt; inorganic working-capital solutions were considered but rejected where they would erode margins.
