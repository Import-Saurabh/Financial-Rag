# Apollo Micro Systems Limited — Annual Report 2025-26, Part 5 (Corporate Governance & BRSR)

This section of the Annual Report 2025-26 of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] covers the tail of the Corporate Governance Report, its annexure certificates, and the full Business Responsibility & Sustainability Report (BRSR) for FY 2025-26.

## Credit Rating
- Domestic rating obtained from [[entities/acuite_rating_and_research_limited]] on a ₹707.50 crore Line of Credit (short term ₹150 cr, long term ₹557.50 cr).
- Long-term: **ACUITE A-** with a **"Stable"** outlook; short-term: **ACUITE A2+** (reaffirmed).
- International rating: Nil. Disclosed to stock exchanges under SEBI (LODR) Regulation 30.
- See [[concepts/credit-rating]].

## Other Disclosures
- **Related party transactions:** No materially significant RPTs; all transactions with related parties were in the ordinary course of business and at arm's length. A Related Party Transaction Policy is in place and published on the website. The company has not transacted with any promoter/promoter group entity holding ≥10%. See [[concepts/related-party-transactions]].
- **Non-compliance (last three years):**
  1. Regulation 33 of SEBI (LODR) — limited review reports filed instead of statutory auditor's reports for FY ended 31 March 2025; penalty of ₹88,500 imposed by [[entities/national_stock_exchange_of_india]].
  2. Section 148(6) of the Companies Act, 2013 — compounding fee for delayed submission of Cost Audit Report (Form CRA-4) for FY 2022-23: ₹25,000 on the company plus ₹10,000 each on the MD and two whole-time directors, imposed by the Regional Director, South East Region, MCA.
- **Whistle Blower Policy and Vigil Mechanism:** protects good-faith reporters of unethical behaviour, fraud or Code of Conduct violations; strict confidentiality; no personnel denied access to the Audit Committee. See [[concepts/whistle-blower-policy]].
- **Mandatory requirements:** full compliance with Regulations 17–27 and Regulation 46(2)(b)–(i). Non-mandatory adoptions: separate Chairman/MD posts, Internal Auditor reporting to the Audit Committee, voluntarily constituted Risk Management Committee. See [[concepts/corporate-governance-and-compliance]].
- **Subsidiaries:** Audit Committee reviews consolidated financials and unlisted subsidiary investments; no material subsidiary.
- Other items: accounting policies per Accounting Standards, Material Events Policy, Independent Director familiarisation programmes, Board/senior-management succession plan, compliance with ICSI Secretarial Standards, quarterly share reconciliation certificates filed with BSE and NSE, and risk management.
- **CEO/CFO certification** furnished under Regulation 17(8).
- Corporate Governance Compliance Certificate obtained from [[entities/mnm_and_associates]] (Sridevi Madati, Partner) — full compliance with Regulations 17–27 and 46(2)(b)–(i).
- **Total fees to statutory auditors:** ₹14.95 lakh (statutory audit ₹10.00 lakh, tax audit ₹3.00 lakh, certification ₹1.95 lakh).
- **Insider trading:** Code of Internal Procedures and Conduct to Regulate, Monitor and Report Trading by Insiders adopted under SEBI (PIT) Regulations, 2015 and revised per the 2020 amendments; Mrs. Rukhya Parveen, Company Secretary, is the Compliance Officer. See [[concepts/insider-trading]].
- **Suspense account:** 1 shareholder holding 50 equity shares as at 1 April 2025 and 31 March 2026; voting rights frozen until claimed.
- **Preferential issue utilisation:** ₹308.29 crore raised via 2,70,42,894 equity shares at ₹1 each plus ₹113 premium; 3,80,67,058 convertible warrants at ₹114 each, with ₹108.49 crore (25%) received on allotment and ₹203.15 crore (75%) received on conversion of 2,37,59,986 warrants by 31 March 2026. Proceeds used for working capital, R&D and general corporate purposes.
- **POSH disclosures:** zero-tolerance policy under the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace Act, 2013; zero complaints filed, disposed or pending.

## BRSR Highlights
### Section A — General Disclosures
- CIN L72200TG1997PLC026556; incorporated 1997; paid-up capital ₹35,72,92,440; shares listed on [[entities/bse_limited]] and [[entities/national_stock_exchange_of_india]]; reporting on a standalone basis.
- Main activity: manufacture of electronic components (NIC 2610), 100% of turnover. Three national plants, two offices (12 states served); no exports.
- Customers are largely government organisations including [[entities/drdo]] (DRDO), DPSUs, shipyards and ordnance factories, plus private firms such as Tata, L&T, Bharat Forge and Solar Industries.
- **Employees:** 434 permanent employees (347 male / 87 female; 2 differently abled). No workers. Women hold 25% of Board seats and 50% of KMP positions.
- **Employee turnover:** 1.67% total in FY 2025-26 versus 11.00% in FY 2024-25 and 7.90% in FY 2023-24.
- **Group structure:** [[entities/ananya_sip_rf_technologies_private_limited]] (51.01% subsidiary), [[entities/apollo_defence_industries_private_limited]] (76.00% subsidiary), [[entities/apollo_strategic_technologies_private_limited]] (51.00% step-down subsidiary) and [[entities/idl_explosives]] (100% step-down via Apollo Defence Industries).
- CSR applicable under Section 135: turnover ₹76,485.31 lakh; net worth ₹1,32,233.34 lakh. See [[concepts/corporate-social-responsibility]].
- **Material issues:** employee retention (risk), design & development (opportunity), sustainable supply chain (opportunity) and data privacy/cyber security (risk).

### Section B — Management & Process
- Policies cover all nine NGRBC principles, are Board-approved, and are published at apollo-micro.com; they do not extend to value chain partners.
- Certifications: ISO 45001:2018, ISO 14001:2015, AS 9100D and DRDO registration.
- [[entities/karunakar_reddy_baddam]], Managing Director (DIN 00790139), is the director responsible for business responsibility and sustainability, and chairs oversight of the BRSR.
- No external agency assessment of policy working; compliance is scrutinised by internal, statutory and secretarial auditors.

### Section C — Principle-wise Performance
- **Principle 1 (Ethics):** training on governance, risk, compliance and NGRBC for Board (4 programmes), KMPs (5) and employees (2), 100% coverage; anti-corruption/anti-bribery framework with integrity pact and whistleblower policy; 198 days of accounts payable (FY25: 181); RPT share of loans & advances 97.83% and investments 100%.
- **Principle 2 (Sustainable & safe goods):** no sustainable sourcing procedure; no product reclamation or Extended Producer Responsibility due to the defence nature of operations. See [[concepts/defense-program-supply-chains]].
- **Principle 3 (Employee well-being):** 100% health and accident insurance coverage; PF 95.62%, Gratuity 94.23%, ESI 27.88% coverage; well-being spend 0.17% of revenue; 100% performance reviews; zero safety incidents, fatalities or LTIFR; all plants assessed for health, safety and working conditions.
- **Principle 4 (Stakeholder responsiveness):** key stakeholders are employees, investors, customers, suppliers/partners, government authorities and the community; community engagement focuses on environmental sustainability, livelihood enhancement and women empowerment.
- **Principle 5 (Human rights):** 100% of employees trained on human rights; all employees paid more than minimum wage; zero complaints of sexual harassment, discrimination, child labour, forced labour or wage issues; HR Head and Whole-Time Directors own human rights issues.
