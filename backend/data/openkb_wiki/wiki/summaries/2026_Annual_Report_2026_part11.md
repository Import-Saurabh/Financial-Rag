# Summary: 2026 Annual Report Part 11 — Consolidated Financial Statement Notes

Part 11 of the 2025-26 Annual Report of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] contains Notes 40–55 to the [[concepts/consolidated-financial-statements]] for the year ended 31 March 2026. All amounts are in ₹ lakhs unless otherwise stated. The notes cover employee benefit plans, MSMED dues, leases, EPS, financial instruments, risk management, capital management, the IDL Explosives acquisition, revenue recognition, ratio analysis, and regulatory disclosures.

## Employee benefits
- **Gratuity**: The plan is 100% funded through funds managed by insurers. Principal actuarial assumptions for FY26 vs FY25: discount rate 0.94% vs 6.75%; expected return 6.33% vs 6.00%; salary escalation 6.33% vs 6.00%.
- **Sensitivity analysis**: +1% discount rate would increase obligation by ₹15.08 lakhs (FY25: ₹10.46 lakhs); -1% would decrease it by ₹12.12 lakhs (FY25: ₹8.68 lakhs). +1% salary increase would increase obligation by ₹11.97 lakhs (FY25: ₹13.99 lakhs); -1% would decrease it by ₹13.83 lakhs (FY25: ₹17.02 lakhs).
- **Expected contributions** for year ending 31 March 2027: ₹24.20 lakhs. Expected benefit payments: 1 year ₹96.09; 2–5 years ₹54.65; 6–10 years ₹136.19; >10 years ₹852.09.
- **Compensated absences**: liability ₹60.32 lakhs (FY25: ₹19.34 lakhs).
- See [[concepts/employee-benefits]].

- **Credit risk**: ageing of trade receivables shows ₹35,296.12 lakhs within credit period; ₹6,966.42 lakhs 91–180 days past due; ₹5,830.92 lakhs >180 days past due.
