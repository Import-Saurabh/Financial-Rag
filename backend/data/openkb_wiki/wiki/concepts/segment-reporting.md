# Segment Reporting

**Segment reporting** is the financial-statement disclosure framework, governed in India by **Ind AS 108 – Operating Segments**, under which an entity reports financial information about its operating segments so that users can evaluate the nature and financial effects of the business activities and economic environments in which it operates.

An **operating segment** is a component of an entity:

- that engages in business activities from which it may earn revenues and incur expenses,
- whose operating results are regularly reviewed by the entity's **Chief Operating Decision Maker (CODM)** for resource allocation and performance assessment, and
- for which discrete financial information is available.

Disclosure is required only for **reportable segments** — generally those meeting quantitative thresholds (e.g. 10% of combined revenue, profit/loss, or assets) or that management designates as reportable. Where an entity operates in a single identifiable business, it presents one reportable segment and no separate segment note is required.

## Key Details from the FY 2025-26 Annual Report (Part 10)

In [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part10]], [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] applies Ind AS 108 as follows:

- **Single reportable segment:** The Group is engaged primarily in the business of manufacturing and sale of electromechanical components and systems and allied components and services. This constitutes a **single reportable segment**, so no separate segment information is disclosed.
- **CODM approach:** The Chief Operating Decision Maker monitors operating results of the Company *as a whole* for decisions about resource allocation and performance assessment. This entity-wide view is what collapses the business into one segment.
- **Revenue disaggregation instead of segments:** Although a single segment exists, revenue from contracts with customers is disaggregated by **product category** and **geography**:
  - *By product/service:* Electronical components/goods — ₹90,432.38 lakhs (FY26) vs ₹56,206.92 lakhs (FY25); Other — nil.
  - *By geography:* Domestic (India) — ₹90,432.38 lakhs (FY26) vs ₹56,206.92 lakhs (FY25); Export (outside India) — nil in both years.
- **Major customers:** No customer individually contributed more than 10% of revenue for the year ended 31 March 2026. In the prior year (FY25), **one customer contributed 20.03%** of revenue — a concentration disclosure relevant to segment/customer reporting.
- **Interaction with revenue recognition:** The disaggregated revenue figures reconcile to total revenue from operations of ₹90,432.38 lakhs, tying segment-style disclosure to the [[concepts/revenue-recognition]] note in the [[concepts/consolidated-financial-statements]].

## Why It Matters

- Provides **transparency** on business lines and geographies when an entity is diversified; where it is not, it confirms concentration risk.
- Feeds directly into **investor analysis** of geographic exposure (here: 100% domestic revenue).
- Connects to related disclosures such as **major customer concentration**, related-party analysis under [[concepts/related-party-transactions]], and the entity's broader financial reporting framework in the [[concepts/consolidated-financial-statements]].
