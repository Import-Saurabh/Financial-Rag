# Deferred Tax

## Definition
Deferred tax is the accounting recognition of future tax consequences arising from **temporary differences** between the carrying amount of an asset or liability in the financial statements and its tax base, as well as from unused tax losses and credits. Under Ind AS 12 (Income Taxes), such differences are measured and recognised as deferred tax assets (DTA) or deferred tax liabilities (DTL), and are presented net where a legally enforceable right of set-off exists for the same taxable entity and authority.

A **deferred tax liability** typically arises when book depreciation is higher than tax depreciation (or income is recognised earlier for books than for tax). A **deferred tax asset** arises from items such as provisions for employee benefits that are deductible for tax only when paid.

### Recognition and measurement
The Group recognises deferred tax assets and liabilities for deductible temporary differences between the tax base and carrying amounts of assets and liabilities, except where the deferred income tax arises from the initial recognition of an asset or liability in a transaction that is not a business combination and affects neither accounting nor taxable profit or loss at the time of the transaction. A DTA is recognised only to the extent that it is probable that taxable profit will be available against which the deductible temporary difference can be utilised; it is re-measured at each reporting date and reduced where the related tax benefit is no longer probable. Deferred tax is measured using substantively enacted tax rates expected to apply when the temporary differences reverse, and assets and liabilities are offset only where they relate to income taxes levied by the same tax authority and the entity intends to settle on a net basis.

**Minimum Alternate Tax (MAT) credit** is recognised only when, and to the extent that, there is convincing evidence that the company will pay normal income tax during the specified period; it is reviewed at each balance sheet date and written down where that evidence no longer holds.

## Key observations

- The dominant driver of the net DTL, on both a standalone and a consolidated basis, is the gap between book depreciation and tax depreciation on [[concepts/property-plant-and-equipment]]. On a consolidated basis this component grew from ₹4,123.89 lakhs to ₹5,461.57 lakhs, reflecting heavy capital expenditure and accelerated tax depreciation; acquisitions added a further ₹405.18 lakhs of deferred tax liability through business combinations, partly offsetting the year's P&L charge.
- The DTA relates mainly to **employee benefits** (gratuity and compensated absences) where provisions are not yet tax-deductible — see [[concepts/employee-benefits]]. The amounts routed through OCI (₹8.90 lakhs consolidated / ₹4.34 lakhs standalone credit) track the actuarial gains and losses on the defined benefit plan, consistent with the requirement that such items be recognised in OCI without recycling.
- Deferred tax expense: consolidated decrease/(increase) in DTA of ₹8.90 lakhs and (decrease)/increase in DTL of ₹1,742.87 lakhs, totalling **₹1,751.77 lakhs** recognised in profit or loss (FY25: ₹707.52 lakhs), with a further ₹(8.90) lakhs recognised in OCI (FY25: ₹5.85 lakhs). Standalone deferred tax expense was ₹1,609.36 lakhs (FY25: ₹706.28 lakhs).
- Total income tax expense (current + deferred + OCI) on a consolidated basis was **₹4,741.72 lakhs** (FY25: ₹2,618.87 lakhs); current tax for the year was ₹2,998.85 lakhs, including a ₹90.73 lakhs prior-year adjustment for an under-provision relating to FY2024-25. The consolidated effective tax rate was **30.63%** (FY25: 31.73%) against the applicable rate of 29.12% — the excess is largely due to depreciation timing differences. The standalone effective rate was 30.95% (FY25: 31.70%).
- As at 31 March 2026 the Group has **no carried-forward tax losses or unabsorbed depreciation** available for set-off against future taxable profits under the Income-tax Act, 1961.
- Separately, the Group is contesting various disallowances by Income Tax authorities, with an associated tax impact of ₹6,356.28 lakhs (FY25: ₹321.44 lakhs) considered more likely than not to be accepted; no provision was made. This is a form of **uncertain tax position**, distinct from deferred tax measurement. The standalone equivalent was ₹2,147.81 lakhs (FY25: ₹321.44 lakhs).
