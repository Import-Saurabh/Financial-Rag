# Capital Management

Capital management is the practice of structuring and adjusting a company's mix of equity and debt to maximise shareholder value while maintaining financial flexibility and compliance with lender covenants. It involves monitoring capital ratios, balancing the cost of borrowing against dilution, and responding to changes in the economic environment and financial covenants.

## Definition
For capital management purposes, **capital** comprises issued share capital and all other equity reserves. The **debt-to-total-equity ratio** treats long-term and short-term borrowings as debt, with total equity being issued share capital plus all other equity reserves. The primary objective is to maximise shareholder value; the company adjusts its capital structure in light of economic conditions and covenant requirements.

## Application at Apollo Micro Systems Limited

The disclosures in [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part11]] show the capital structure of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] as at 31 March 2026 and 31 March 2025:

| Particulars | 31 March 2026 | 31 March 2025 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Total equity attributable to equity shareholders | ₹1,30,822.03 lakhs | ₹60,756.89 lakhs |
| As a percentage of total capital | 71.1% | 64.7% |
| Long-term borrowings including current maturities | ₹16,312.56 lakhs | ₹8,713.87 lakhs |
| Short-term borrowings | ₹36,928.65 lakhs | ₹24,362.43 lakhs |
| Total borrowings | ₹53,241.21 lakhs | ₹33,076.30 lakhs |
| As a percentage of total capital | 28.9% | 35.3% |
| Total capital (equity and borrowings) | ₹1,84,063.25 lakhs | ₹93,833.19 lakhs |

### Key observations
- Equity grew sharply year on year, lifting the equity share of total capital from 64.7% to 71.1% and reducing the debt-to-equity ratio to 0.81 from 1.14 — a change the company attributes to an increase in equity during the year.
- Both short-term and long-term borrowings increased in absolute terms, so the improvement in the capital mix reflects faster equity growth rather than deleveraging.
- Capital management interacts closely with [[concepts/financial-ratios]], since the debt-equity ratio, debt service coverage ratio, and return on capital employed are all derived from the capital structure.

## Why it matters
- **Shareholder value**: an optimal mix lowers the weighted average cost of capital and supports valuation.
- **Covenant compliance**: lenders impose financial covenants that constrain leverage.
- **Risk profile**: the debt component interacts with [[concepts/financial-risk-management]], particularly interest rate and liquidity risk.
- **Funding capacity**: retained equity and borrowing headroom determine the ability to fund growth, including through [[concepts/ind-as-103-business-combinations]].
