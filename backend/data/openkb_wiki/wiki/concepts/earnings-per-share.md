# Earnings Per Share (EPS)

## Definition
Earnings Per Share (EPS) is the portion of a company's profit attributable to each outstanding equity share. It is computed by dividing the net profit attributable to equity shareholders for the period by the weighted average number of equity shares outstanding during the year. EPS is a core profitability metric used by investors to assess value on a per-share basis and is disclosed for both basic and diluted scenarios under applicable accounting standards. In group reporting, EPS is computed on the basis of profit attributable to the owners of the holding company, excluding amounts attributable to non-controlling interests.

## Basic vs. Diluted EPS
- **Basic EPS** divides net profit attributable to equity shareholders by the weighted average number of equity shares outstanding during the year.
- **Diluted EPS** additionally considers potential equity shares (e.g., pending share warrants, convertible instruments). Potential shares are treated as dilutive only if their conversion would decrease the net profit per share.

## Accounting Policy
Note 3.17 of the consolidated financial statements sets out the policy applied by [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]]:
- The Company presents basic and diluted EPS data for its ordinary shares.
- Basic EPS is computed by dividing the net profit attributable to equity shareholders for the period by the weighted average number of equity shares outstanding during the year.
- Diluted EPS divides the net profit attributable to equity shareholders relating to the dilutive potential equity shares by the weighted average number of shares used for basic EPS plus the weighted average number of shares that could have been issued on conversion of all dilutive potential equity shares.
- Potential equity shares are deemed dilutive only if their conversion to equity shares would decrease the net profit per share.

The same policy is restated in Note 43 of the consolidated financial statements, which also presents the year's basic and diluted EPS computation.

## Disclosure in the Source Documents
[[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] presents EPS for both standalone and consolidated results for FY2025-26 and FY2024-25.

### Standalone ([[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part8]])
Presented in Note 41 to the standalone financial statements.

#### Profit attributable to equity shareholders
- FY2025-26: ₹12,057.89 lakhs
- FY2024-25: ₹5,724.16 lakhs

#### Weighted average shares
- Basic, FY2025-26: 33,74,56,014 shares
- Add: pending share warrants to be allotted: 38,02,411 shares
- Diluted, FY2025-26: 34,12,58,425 shares
- FY2024-25 (basic and diluted): 30,36,60,303 shares

#### EPS (par value ₹1)
- Basic: ₹3.57 (FY2025-26) vs ₹1.89 (FY2024-25)
- Diluted: ₹3.53 (FY2025-26) vs ₹1.89 (FY2024-25)

The gap between basic and diluted EPS in FY2025-26 reflects the dilutive effect of pending share warrants.

### Consolidated ([[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part9]], [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part11]])
Presented in Note 43 to the consolidated financial statements.

#### Profit attributable to owners of the Company
- FY2025-26: ₹11,292.04 lakhs
- FY2024-25: ₹5,639.65 lakhs

Non-controlling interests contributed a loss of ₹554.00 lakhs in FY2025-26, so consolidated profit for the year was ₹10,738.04 lakhs. Note 51 of the consolidated financial statements confirms this consolidated net profit figure and reports total consolidated comprehensive income of ₹10,762.14 lakhs for FY2025-26 (including other comprehensive income of ₹24.11 lakhs), versus ₹5,621.53 lakhs in FY2024-25.

#### Equity share capital
- As at 31 March 2026: 35,72,92,440 equity shares of ₹1 each (₹3,572.92 lakhs)
- As at 31 March 2025: 30,64,89,560 equity shares of ₹1 each (₹3,064.90 lakhs)

The increase of 5,08,02,880 shares during the year reflects new share issuances, including shares issued to fund the acquisition of the [[entities/idl_explosives]] step-down subsidiary.

#### EPS (par value ₹1)
- Basic: ₹3.35 (FY2025-26) vs ₹1.86 (FY2024-25)
- Diluted: ₹3.31 (FY2025-26) vs ₹1.86 (FY2024-25)

Consolidated EPS is lower than standalone EPS because group profit attributable to owners is reduced by the losses of the non-controlling interests, and the consolidated share base reflects the full equity share capital of the holding company.

Note 51 also disaggregates the consolidated result by entity: the holding company contributed ₹1,32,233.32 lakhs of net assets (101.08% of the consolidated total) and ₹12,057.87 lakhs of profit (112.29%), which is reduced by losses at the subsidiaries to arrive at the consolidated profit of ₹10,738.04 lakhs.

### Share Capital Movements ([[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part10]])
Note 13 of the consolidated financial statements reconciles the number of shares outstanding during FY2025-26:
- Outstanding at the beginning of the year: 30,64,89,560 equity shares of ₹1 each (₹3,064.90 lakhs)
- Issued during the year: 2,70,42,894 shares (₹270.43 lakhs)
- Conversion of warrants: 2,37,59,986 shares (₹237.60 lakhs)
- Outstanding at the end of the year: 35,72,92,440 shares (₹3,572.92 lakhs)

The Company issued 3,80,67,058 fully convertible warrants in FY2025-26. During the year 2,37,59,986 warrants were converted into an equal number of equity shares, while the remaining 1,43,07,072 warrants remained outstanding for conversion as at 31 March 2026. These outstanding warrants are the primary source of potential dilutive equity shares.

Both the fresh issue (2,70,42,894 shares at ₹1 each at a premium of ₹113) and the warrant conversion were made on a preferential basis, raising securities premium of ₹57,407.25 lakhs. Profit for the year recognised in retained earnings was ₹11,292.04 lakhs, matching the consolidated profit attributable to owners of the Company. A final dividend of ₹0.25 per share was declared and paid for FY2024-25 (₹0.05 per share for FY2023-24).

## Interpretation
The near doubling of EPS from ₹1.89 to ₹3.57 on a basic standalone basis (and from ₹1.86 to ₹3.35 on a consolidated basis) reflects the strong growth in net profit, which more than offset the increase in the weighted average share count. Dilution from pending warrants slightly reduces per-share earnings — to ₹3.53 on a standalone basis and ₹3.31 on a consolidated basis. The share capital reconciliation in the consolidated notes shows that the expanded share base came from a fresh preferential issue and warrant conversion, leaving 1,43,07,072 warrants still outstanding and therefore still dilutive in future periods. EPS is closely tied to the broader profitability picture captured in [[concepts/financial-ratios]], including the net profit ratio and return on equity, and is underpinned by revenue growth described in [[concepts/revenue-recognition]]. Consolidated EPS is further shaped by the group structure, including the acquisition of a step-down subsidiary and the resulting non-controlling interest.
