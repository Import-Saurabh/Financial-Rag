# Goodwill

Goodwill is an intangible asset that arises in a business combination when the consideration transferred by the acquirer exceeds the fair value of the identifiable net assets acquired. Under [[concepts/ind-as-103-business-combinations]], goodwill is recognised at the acquisition date and is not amortised; instead it is carried at cost less accumulated impairment losses and tested for impairment at least annually.

## Accounting Policy (as applied by Apollo Micro Systems Limited)

Per the material accounting policies in the consolidated financial statements:

- Goodwill is **initially recognised as an asset at cost** and subsequently measured at cost less any accumulated impairment losses.
- For impairment testing, goodwill is **allocated to each of the Group's cash-generating units (CGUs)** or groups of CGUs expected to benefit from the synergies of the combination.
- CGUs to which goodwill has been allocated are **tested for impairment annually**, or more frequently when there is an indication that the unit's value may be impaired.
- The **recoverable amount** of the CGU is the higher of fair value less costs to sell and value in use. Future cash flow projections consider economic uncertainties, discount rates, growth rates used for terminal value, and sensitivity analysis.
- If the recoverable amount is less than the carrying value, the impairment loss is **allocated first to reduce goodwill** and then to the other assets of the unit on a pro-rata basis.

Goodwill is presented as a non-current asset within the [[concepts/consolidated-financial-statements]]. Note 5 discloses the movement of the gross carrying value, accumulated amortisation and net carrying value:

Goodwill is not amortised, so the accumulated amortisation balance remains nil across both years, and no impairment loss was recognised in either year. The step increase during FY2025-26 is driven almost entirely by the acquisition of [[entities/idl_explosives]].

## Origin of the Pre-Existing ₹1.89 Lakhs Goodwill

The ₹1.89 lakhs goodwill carried into FY2025-26 arose in **FY2020-21**, when the holding Company acquired **51% control** of [[entities/ananya_sip_rf_technologies_private_limited]] through the purchase of equity shares. The Group accounted for that business combination based on the fair value of the identified assets, liabilities and contingent liabilities as at the date of acquisition, and recognised goodwill of ₹1.89 lakhs.

## Goodwill on the IDL Explosives Acquisition (Note 47)

### The transaction

[[entities/apollo_defence_industries_private_limited]] ("ADIPL"), a subsidiary of the Company, entered into a Share Purchase Agreement dated **2 May 2025** to acquire **100% of the equity share capital** of [[entities/idl_explosives]] ("IDL"). The transaction was completed on **15 November 2025** (the "acquisition date"), on which date ADIPL obtained control of IDL, making IDL a wholly owned step-down subsidiary of the Company.

IDL is engaged in the manufacture of industrial explosives, accessories and allied products and qualifies as a business as defined in Ind AS 103. The acquisition was undertaken by the Group to expand its defence and explosives manufacturing capability and to obtain access to IDL's manufacturing licences and infrastructure. This is the strategic context captured under [[concepts/defence-acquisitions-and-integration]].

The total purchase consideration for the acquisition of IDL was **₹10,700.00 lakhs**, satisfied by cash paid on completion.

At the acquisition date, IDL's **identifiable liabilities exceeded its identifiable assets** — the negative net assets are driven principally by the large borrowings balance of ₹20,721.03 lakhs — while the consideration transferred was positive. This resulted in recognition of **goodwill of ₹14,517.67 lakhs**, being the excess of consideration transferred over the identifiable net assets acquired, in accordance with paragraph 32 of Ind AS 103. (The Note 5 movement table presents this amount as ₹14,517.66 lakhs, a rounding difference.)

### Basis of the goodwill

The goodwill arising on the acquisition is attributable to the **assembled workforce, manufacturing know-how and statutory licences** held by IDL, and the **synergies expected from integration** with the Group's defence manufacturing business, none of which qualify for separate recognition as an identifiable intangible asset. The goodwill so recognised is **not expected to be deductible for income tax purposes**.

### Measurement period and provisional accounting

As at 31 March 2026, the initial accounting for the business combination is **incomplete** and the amounts disclosed are accordingly **provisional**. The Company is in the process of:

- finalising the **independent valuation of property, plant and equipment and identifiable intangible assets**; and
- completing the assessment of **provisions and contingent liabilities** relating to statutory, regulatory and environmental matters specific to an explosives manufacturing licence holder.

In accordance with paragraphs 45–50 of Ind AS 103, the Company will **retrospectively adjust** the provisional amounts recognised as at the acquisition date, with a corresponding adjustment to goodwill, to reflect new information obtained about facts and circumstances that existed at the acquisition date. The measurement period will not exceed one year from the acquisition date, i.e. it will conclude **no later than 15 November 2026**. The goodwill recognised is therefore itself provisional and remains subject to adjustment within that period. Management obtained a written representation that **no impairment indicators exist** for the provisional goodwill as at the reporting date.

### Contribution to Group results

IDL contributed revenue of **₹13,764.39 lakhs** and profit/(loss) after tax of **₹(262.82) lakhs** to the Group for the period from the acquisition date (15 November 2025) to 31 March 2026, as included in the consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss for the year. Had the acquisition been completed on 1 April 2025 (the first day of the financial year), the Group's revenue for the year ended 31 March 2026 would have been **₹1,14,647.77 lakhs** and the Group's profit for the year would have been **₹7,936.79 lakhs**. These pro forma figures inform the assessment of whether the goodwill carrying amount is supported by expected future performance.

### Acquisition-related costs

Acquisition-related costs of **₹2.73 lakhs**, comprising legal, due diligence and advisory fees incurred in connection with the acquisition, have been recognised as an expense and included in the Statement of Profit and Loss for the year ended 31 March 2026.

## Why Goodwill Was a Key Audit Matter

The auditor identified the IDL goodwill as a [[concepts/key-audit-matters]] because:

1. Determining the fair value of consideration transferred involves significant estimation and judgement.
2. The quantum of goodwill is material, and any subsequent fair valuation during the measurement period could result in a **significant retrospective adjustment to goodwill**, with consequential impact on the opening balance sheet and comparatives.
3. The purchase price allocation remained incomplete as at the reporting date.

Audit procedures included evaluating the terms of the share agreement, confirming the legal acquirer is also the accounting acquirer, agreeing provisional goodwill to the acquisition-date balance sheet, testing acquisition journal entries, evaluating disclosures under Ind AS 103, assessing management's future profitability assumptions (revenue growth, margins, synergies) against industry data, and obtaining written representations on impairment indicators.
