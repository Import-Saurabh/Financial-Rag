# MSMED Supplier Disclosures

## Overview
MSMED Supplier Disclosures are the disclosures required under Section 22 of the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development (MSMED) Act, 2006. They require companies to report amounts due to suppliers registered as micro, small, or medium enterprises, along with related interest and payment delays. The disclosures help enforce timely payment and transparency in supplier relationships.

## Regulatory basis
- Section 22 of the MSMED Act, 2006 mandates disclosure in the annual accounts of any amount due to micro and small enterprises that remains unpaid beyond the appointed day.
- Section 16 of the MSMED Act prescribes interest on delayed payments beyond the appointed day (45 days, or as agreed).
- The disclosures are typically presented as a note to the financial statements, alongside other statutory disclosures.
