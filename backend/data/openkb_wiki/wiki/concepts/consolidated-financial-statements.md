# Consolidated Financial Statements

**Consolidated Financial Statements (CFS)** present the assets, liabilities, equity, income, expenses and cash flows of a parent (Holding Company) and its subsidiaries as those of a single economic entity. They are prepared by combining like items line-by-line, eliminating intra-group transactions, and attributing profit or loss and other comprehensive income between owners of the parent and non-controlling interests.

## Regulatory Framework

In India, CFS are prepared under:

- **Section 133 of the Companies Act, 2013** read with the Companies (Indian Accounting Standards) Rules, 2015
- **Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS)**, notably Ind AS 110 (Consolidated Financial Statements) and Ind AS 112 (Disclosure of Interests in Other Entities)
- **Division II of Schedule III** to the Companies Act, 2013, which prescribes presentation and minimum disclosure requirements, including the general instructions on identification of related parties

CFS are typically prepared on a going concern basis and are subject to statutory audit, with the auditor's report issued to the members of the Holding Company.

## Core Statements

A complete set of consolidated financial statements comprises:

1. **Consolidated Balance Sheet** – consolidated state of affairs as at the reporting date
2. **Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss** (including Other Comprehensive Income)
3. **Consolidated Statement of Changes in Equity** – movement in share capital, reserves, retained earnings and non-controlling interests
4. **Consolidated Statement of Cash Flows** – prepared under the Indirect Method of Ind AS 7, with cash flows segregated into operating, investing and financing activities
5. **Notes**, including material accounting policies and other explanatory information

## Consolidation Procedure

The Group's accounting policy describes the standard mechanics:

- Combine like items of assets, liabilities, equity, income, expenses and cash flows of the parent with those of its subsidiaries, using the acquisition-date values for subsidiary income and expenses.
- Offset (eliminate) the carrying amount of the parent's investment in each subsidiary against the parent's portion of equity in that subsidiary.
- Eliminate in full intra-group assets, liabilities, equity, income, expenses and cash flows, including profits or losses embedded in inventory and fixed assets.
- Attribute profit or loss and each component of OCI to owners of the Holding Company and to **non-controlling interests (NCI)**, even where this results in a deficit balance.
- Where necessary, adjust subsidiary financial statements to align accounting policies with the Group's policies.
- A change in ownership interest without loss of control is accounted for as an equity transaction.
- Deferred tax assets and liabilities are offset where they relate to income taxes levied by the same tax authority and the entity intends to settle on a net basis.

## Material Accounting Policies

The notes to the consolidated financial statements set out the policies applied across the Group, including:

- **[[concepts/provisions-and-contingent-liabilities]]** – provisions are recognised where a past event creates a probable, reliably estimable obligation; contingent liabilities are disclosed unless outflow is remote, and contingent assets are not recognised but disclosed where inflow is probable. Where the time value of money is material, provisions are discounted and the unwinding is recognised as a finance cost.
- **[[concepts/government-grants]]** – recognised only where there is reasonable assurance of compliance with conditions and receipt; revenue grants are recognised as other income, grants compensating additional depreciation over the asset's useful life, and below-market-rate government loans are measured at fair value with deferred income released over the loan tenure.
- **[[concepts/earnings-per-share]]** – basic EPS divides net profit attributable to equity shareholders by the weighted average number of equity shares outstanding; diluted EPS adjusts for dilutive potential equity shares.
- **Investment property** – initially at cost and subsequently at cost less accumulated depreciation and impairment, with depreciation on buildings under the straight-line method per Schedule II of the Companies Act, 2013; gains or losses on derecognition are recognised in profit or loss.
- **Research and development** – research expenditure is expensed as incurred; development expenditure is capitalised only where costs are reliably measurable, technical and commercial feasibility is established, future economic benefits are probable, and the Company has sufficient resources to complete and use or sell the asset.
- **Borrowing costs** – borrowing costs directly attributable to qualifying assets that take a substantial period to be ready for intended use or sale are capitalised; all other borrowing costs are expensed.
- **Events after the reporting date** – adjusting events are recognised, while material non-adjusting events are disclosed.
- **Cash dividend to equity holders** – a liability is recognised when the distribution is authorised and no longer at the Company's discretion, with a corresponding amount recognised directly in other equity.
- **Changes in material accounting policies and new pronouncements** – the Group adopted the amendments to Ind AS 1 on the classification of liabilities as current or non-current and non-current liabilities with covenants, applied retrospectively; the amendment on covenant breaches effective 1 April 2026 is applied retrospectively under Ind AS 8.

## Notes to the Consolidated Financial Statements

### Non-current assets

- **[[concepts/property-plant-and-equipment]]** – gross block rose to ₹44,546.43 lakhs from ₹24,564.14 lakhs, aided by additions of ₹11,246.57 lakhs and ₹8,770.22 lakhs from business combinations; net carrying amount was ₹31,525.89 lakhs (2025: ₹17,227.13 lakhs). Titles to TSIIC-allotted land and a flat purchased from Ambience Projects and Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd are pending registration. Assets are pledged to banks and NBFCs and no impairment was recorded.
- **[[concepts/capital-work-in-progress]]** – net CWIP was ₹19,629.40 lakhs (2025: ₹7,228.24 lakhs), with ₹2,130.91 lakhs committed under agreements to purchase property, plant and equipment. Capitalised interest of ₹1,158.96 lakhs was recorded at an average rate of 7.38%.
- **[[concepts/leases]]** – right-of-use assets carried at ₹1,113.97 lakhs (2025: ₹461.72 lakhs), with depreciation of ₹241.37 lakhs and lease interest of ₹97.11 lakhs. Lease liabilities totalled ₹1,084.68 lakhs, split between non-current ₹696.98 lakhs and current ₹387.70 lakhs.
- **Other intangible assets** – software carried at ₹580.57 lakhs after an amortisation charge of ₹168.90 lakhs.
- **[[concepts/goodwill]]** – grew to ₹14,519.55 lakhs, almost entirely goodwill of ₹14,517.66 lakhs arising on business combinations during the year; no impairment was recognised.

### Working capital

- **Inventories** – ₹79,528.92 lakhs (2025: ₹60,063.61 lakhs), of which ₹59,646.69 lakhs is pledged as security for borrowings; no write-down to net realisable value was made.
- **[[concepts/financial-instruments]]** – trade receivables were ₹48,093.46 lakhs net of an expected credit loss allowance of ₹732.60 lakhs, computed using a provision matrix based on ageing and forward-looking information; the allowance rose sharply from ₹10.31 lakhs, with ₹138.85 lakhs of ECL on trade receivables charged to profit or loss.
- **Cash and other balances** – cash and cash equivalents were ₹8,950.32 lakhs; other bank balances of ₹6,661.11 lakhs include margin money and restricted share-warrant proceeds, while other current assets of ₹22,735.28 lakhs include material advances of ₹16,916.37 lakhs net of ECL.

Promoter holding includes Baddam Karunakar Reddy (47.28%) and [[entities/baddam_chanakya_reddy]] and [[entities/baddam_kanishka_reddy]] (2.35% each).
- **Other equity** – totalled ₹1,27,736.57 lakhs, with securities premium of ₹89,486.57 lakhs, retained earnings of ₹34,108.36 lakhs and money received against share warrants of ₹4,077.52 lakhs.
- **Borrowings** – non-current borrowings rose to ₹11,925.97 lakhs and current borrowings to ₹41,315.24 lakhs. Facilities are held with [[entities/state_bank_of_india]], [[entities/hdfc_bank]], [[entities/bandhan_bank]], [[entities/indian_overseas_bank]] and others, secured by hypothecation of assets, equitable mortgages and personal guarantees of two Directors. The non-convertible debentures issued to [[entities/revx_capital_fund_ii]] were fully repaid during the year. There were no defaults.
- **Trade payables** – ₹38,201.43 lakhs, including MSME dues of ₹652.92 lakhs, with ageing disclosed for MSME and other payables.

### Provisions, taxes and commitments

- **Tax expense** – current tax of ₹2,998.85 lakhs and deferred tax of ₹1,751.77 lakhs gave a total income tax expense of ₹4,741.72 lakhs. The effective tax rate was 30.63% (2025: 31.73%), with a ₹90.73 lakhs prior-year under-provision adjustment.

Management considers the likelihood of material outflow to be remote.

## Other Significant Elements

- **Non-controlling interest** moved from a positive ₹66.53 lakhs to a deficit of ₹(487.46) lakhs, mainly due to losses attributable to NCI of ₹(554.00) lakhs during the year.
- **[[concepts/earnings-per-share]]** is computed on consolidated profit and disclosed in both basic and diluted form.
- **[[concepts/deferred-tax]]** is recognised on temporary differences on a consolidated basis.
- **[[concepts/leases]]** are consolidated via right-of-use assets and lease liabilities under Ind AS 116.
- **[[concepts/financial-instruments]]** are consolidated and measured at amortised cost, FVTOCI or FVTPL, with expected credit losses applied to receivables.
- **[[concepts/employee-benefits]]** including defined benefit remeasurements flow through OCI at the consolidated level.
- **[[concepts/internal-financial-controls]]** over financial reporting are audited and reported on in Annexure A of the auditor's report.
- **[[concepts/audit-trail-rule-11g]]** compliance is reported as operating throughout the year.
- Other disclosures cover [[concepts/corporate-governance-and-compliance]] matters, pending litigations, dividend compliance under Section 123, and managerial remuneration under Section 197 read with Schedule V.

## Related Concepts

- [[concepts/consolidated-financial-statements]] is closely tied to [[concepts/ind-as-103-business-combinations]] for acquisitions and to [[concepts/goodwill]] for any excess of consideration over net identifiable assets acquired.
- It is distinguished from standalone financial statements, which present only the Holding Company's own financial position and performance.
