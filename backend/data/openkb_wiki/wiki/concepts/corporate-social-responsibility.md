# Corporate Social Responsibility

Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) forms an integral part of the corporate governance and community engagement framework for [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] as detailed in [[summaries/2025_Financial_Year_2025_from_bse_part2]]. Pursuant to Rule 8 of the Companies (Corporate Social Responsibility Policy) Rules, 2014, the company outlines its commitment to sustainable development, societal welfare, and environmental stewardship.

## CSR Policy and Governance

The company maintains a dedicated CSR policy and committee responsible for overseeing initiatives, ensuring statutory compliance, and monitoring fund allocations. The governance structure aligns with the National Guidelines on Responsible Business Conduct (NGRBC) principles concerning inclusive growth and community development.

## Financial Computations and Obligations

For the financial year 2024–25, the statutory CSR obligation was calculated based on the average net profit of the preceding three financial years:

* **Average Net Profit:** ₹3,153.23 Lakhs
* **Total CSR Obligation (2%):** ₹63.06 Lakhs
* **Actual CSR Expenditure:** ₹100.00 Lakhs
* **Excess Amount for Set-off:** ₹36.94 Lakhs (available for set-off in succeeding financial years)

The company confirmed that no capital assets were created or acquired directly through CSR funds during the review period.

## Key Project Allocations

The total expenditure of ₹100.00 Lakhs was directed toward impactful societal and environmental projects executed through trusted partner organizations:

* **Environmental Sustainability:** Contributions amounting to ₹70 Lakhs were allocated toward environmental initiatives via [[entities/arunodaya_trust]].
* **Education Support:** Contributions amounting to ₹30 Lakhs were directed toward supporting and educating dropout students via [[entities/sarada_educational_society]].

Related governance practices and regulatory compliances are further detailed under [[concepts/corporate-social-responsibility]] and [[concepts/corporate-governance-and-compliance]].
