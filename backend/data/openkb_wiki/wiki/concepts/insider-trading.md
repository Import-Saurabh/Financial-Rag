# Insider Trading Compliance

Insider trading compliance refers to the framework of internal codes, procedures and disclosures that a listed company adopts to prevent designated persons and other insiders from trading in its securities while in possession of Unpublished Price Sensitive Information (UPSI). In India the governing regime is the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015, which requires every listed entity to adopt a code of conduct and to appoint a Compliance Officer to administer it.

## Key Elements of a Framework

- **A written code of conduct** setting out procedures to be followed and disclosures to be made when trading in the company's securities.
- **A Compliance Officer** responsible for implementation and monitoring across the organisation.
- **Trading windows and pre-clearance** mechanisms that restrict dealings around results announcements and other price-sensitive events.
- **Designated persons and connected persons** who are subject to disclosure obligations regarding their holdings and dealings.
- **Structural safeguards** — Chinese walls, restricted access to UPSI, and maintenance of a structured digital database of information sharing.
- **Alignment with the company's wider ethics infrastructure**, including the [[concepts/whistle-blower-policy]] for reporting violations of the code.

## Compliance in the Source Document

The [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part5]] section of the annual report of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] records that the Company has adopted the **Code of Internal Procedures and Conduct to Regulate, Monitor and Report Trading by Insiders** under the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015. The Code lays down guidelines for procedures to be followed and disclosures to be made while trading in the securities of the Company, and has been revised in line with the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) (Amendment) Regulations, 2020.

The Code is published on the Company's website under the "Investors" section. The Company further reports that **[[entities/rukhya_parveen]]**, Company Secretary, has been appointed as the **Compliance Officer** for ensuring compliance with, and effective implementation of, the Regulations and the Code across the Company.

## Relationship to Wider Governance

Insider trading compliance sits within the broader [[concepts/corporate-governance-and-compliance]] architecture that [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] maintains as a company listed on [[entities/bse_limited]] and the [[entities/national_stock_exchange_of_india]]. The annual report confirms full compliance with the mandatory corporate governance requirements of Regulations 17 to 27 and Regulation 46(2)(b) to (i) of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 — the same disclosure regime administered by the [[entities/securities_and_exchange_board_of_india]] that underpins the insider trading rules. The Company also maintains related policies, including a Whistle Blower Policy and Vigil Mechanism, that provide channels for reporting conduct that breaches the Code.

## Why It Matters

Weak insider trading controls expose a listed entity to regulatory penalties, reputational damage and loss of investor confidence, and can undermine the fairness and integrity of the market for its shares. A robust code — reinforced by periodic revision to track amendments to the SEBI regulations, website disclosure, and a named Compliance Officer — is the standard mechanism through which companies demonstrate adherence to market-conduct norms.
