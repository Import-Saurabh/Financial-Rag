# Tier-1 Defense OEM Integration

Tier-1 defense OEM integration refers to the strategic position of a company that supplies complete subsystems, critical electronics, and electromechanical assemblies directly to defense original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) or to government agencies such as [[entities/drdo]]. Unlike lower-tier component suppliers, a tier-1 integrator is responsible for designing, developing, and delivering mission-critical systems that are directly integrated into larger weapon platforms.

## Characteristics

- **Development-cum-production partnerships**: Tier-1 suppliers often co-develop products with the OEM, sharing design and production responsibilities. A key example is Apollo Micro Systems and [[entities/bharat_dynamics_limited]] as development-cum-production partners on the MIGM program.
- **Multiple subsystem supply**: They provide several critical subsystems for a single platform. For QRSAM, Apollo supplies the onboard computer, navigation system, power distribution units, and actuation system.
- **High value-addition and technology ownership**: Tier-1 integrators own the design and manufacturing know-how for their subsystems, enabling them to contribute a significant share of the platform's electronics content. Apollo claims to contribute around 63% of the electronics and electromechanical work for DRDO missile programs.
- **Direct relationship with the end customer**: In India, tier-1 suppliers often work directly with DRDO and defence PSUs, bypassing traditional OEM structures. Apollo's work on Project Kusha and torpedo programs (EHWT, ALWT, and Varunastra) illustrates this.

## Strategic Importance

Tier-1 integration is a key element of [[concepts/defense_indigenization]], as it builds domestic capability in mission-critical electronics and reduces reliance on foreign suppliers. It also creates high barriers to entry due to the need for proven reliability, security clearances, and long qualification cycles. For [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]], this position drives revenue visibility (order book of around INR 735 crore) and margin expansion through operating leverage and product mix.
