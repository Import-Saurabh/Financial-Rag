# Leases (Ind AS 116)

Ind AS 116 requires a lessee to recognise a right-of-use (ROU) asset and a lease liability for most leases. The lease liability is measured at the present value of remaining lease payments, discounted using the incremental borrowing rate unless the implicit rate is readily determinable. The ROU asset initially includes the lease liability, initial direct costs, prepayments, estimated restoration costs, and lease incentives. Subsequently, the ROU asset is depreciated, and the lease liability is accreted for interest and reduced by lease payments.

A lessor classifies leases as finance or operating leases. For operating leases, lease income is recognised on a straight-line basis over the lease term, and the underlying asset remains on the lessor’s balance sheet.

## Application at Apollo Micro Systems Limited

The Company has lease contracts for various items of property, plant and equipment used in its operations, including plant and equipment, buildings, and computer equipment. The standalone financial statements for FY2025-26 in [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part8]] and the consolidated financial statements in [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part10]] and [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part11]] disclose the following lease-related amounts for [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]].

### Standalone (Note 16 in [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part8]])

- **Lease liabilities:** closing balance ₹360.58 lakhs as at 31 March 2026, split into non-current ₹192.07 lakhs and current ₹168.51 lakhs. The prior-year closing balance was ₹458.44 lakhs, split into non-current ₹269.13 lakhs and current ₹189.31 lakhs.
- **Movement in lease liabilities:** opening balance ₹458.44 lakhs; recognition of new lease liabilities during the year ₹44.29 lakhs; interest on lease liabilities ₹42.26 lakhs; payment of principal portion ₹(184.41) lakhs; closing balance ₹360.58 lakhs.
- **ROU assets:** included within property, plant and equipment. Closing carrying amount ₹349.12 lakhs as at 31 March 2026 (FY2025: ₹461.72 lakhs). Additions during the year were ₹44.29 lakhs, and depreciation was ₹156.89 lakhs.
- **Amounts recognised in profit and loss:** depreciation of ROU assets ₹156.89 lakhs; interest expense on lease liabilities ₹42.26 lakhs; total ₹199.15 lakhs (FY2025: ₹142.77 lakhs).
- **Short-term and low-value leases:** short-term lease expense was nil in FY2025-26 (FY2025: ₹18.29 lakhs), and low-value lease expense was nil.
- **Cash outflows for leases:** total cash outflow was ₹184.41 lakhs during the year (FY2025: ₹145.96 lakhs), including short-term and low-value leases.
- **Maturity profile of lease liabilities:** before 3 months ₹37.87 lakhs; 3–6 months ₹37.76 lakhs; 6–12 months ₹63.58 lakhs; 1–3 years ₹187.63 lakhs; 3–5 years ₹33.74 lakhs; above 5 years nil; total ₹360.58 lakhs. The prior-year total was ₹458.44 lakhs.
- **Lessors:** the company’s lease contracts include arrangements with [[entities/origa_markets_private_limited]], with interest rates of 10.00% over 36 instalments and 5.91% over 60 instalments.
- **Lessor accounting:** the company also acts as lessor for buildings under operating leases. Lease income was ₹4.95 lakhs (FY2025: ₹0.80 lakhs). Annual undiscounted lease payments receivable are ₹3.6 lakhs each year for up to 1 year through 4–5 years, totalling ₹18.00 lakhs. Leases are renewed only on mutual consent and at prevailing market prices, and sub-leasing is generally restricted.
- **Transition:** the company applied the practical expedient not to apply the recognition requirements of Ind AS 116 to short-term leases and applied a materiality threshold for recognising lease-related assets and liabilities. Lease liabilities were recorded at the present value of lease payments discounted at the incremental borrowing rate, and ROU assets were measured at carrying amount as if the standard had been applied since the commencement date.

## Significance in the financial statements

Lease liabilities form part of total debt in [[concepts/capital-management]] and affect [[concepts/financial-ratios]], including the debt-equity ratio of 0.73 in FY2025-26 (FY2025: 1.07) reported in [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part10]]. ROU assets are disclosed within [[concepts/property-plant-and-equipment]]. Lease liabilities are financial liabilities measured at amortised cost under [[concepts/financial-instruments]], and the maturity profile of borrowings and trade payables feeds into the liquidity disclosures under [[concepts/financial-risk-management]]. Interest on lease liabilities forms part of finance costs, while ROU depreciation affects profitability and [[concepts/earnings-per-share]]. Disclosures also support [[concepts/related-party-transactions]] where lease parties are related parties and may give rise to temporary differences relevant to [[concepts/deferred-tax]].
