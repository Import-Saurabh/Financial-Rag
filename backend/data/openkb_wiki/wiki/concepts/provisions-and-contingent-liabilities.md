# Provisions and Contingent Liabilities

## Definition

A **provision** is a liability of uncertain timing or amount, recognised when an entity has a present legal or constructive obligation arising from a past event, it is probable that an outflow of economic benefits will be required to settle it, and the amount can be estimated reliably. A **contingent liability** is a possible obligation whose existence depends on uncertain future events, or a present obligation that is not recognised because an outflow is not probable or cannot be measured reliably. A **contingent asset** is a possible asset arising from past events whose existence will be confirmed only by uncertain future events.

## Recognition and Measurement

### Provisions

- Recognised only when the three recognition criteria (present obligation, probable outflow, reliable estimate) are met.
- Where the time value of money is material, provisions are measured by discounting expected future cash flows at a pre-tax rate reflecting current market assessments of the time value of money and liability-specific risks.
- The unwinding of the discount is recognised as a **finance cost** (not as part of the provision charge) in the period in which it arises.

### Contingent Liabilities

- **Disclosed** when a possible obligation exists, or a present obligation exists but the outflow is not probable.
- **No disclosure and no provision** is made where the likelihood of outflow is **remote**.
- Contingent liabilities are reassessed continually; if an outflow becomes probable, a provision is recognised in the period the change occurs.

### Contingent Assets

- **Not recognised** in the financial statements.
- **Disclosed** where an inflow of economic benefits is probable.
- Where the inflow becomes **virtually certain**, the asset and related income are recognised in the period the change occurs.

## Application in Apollo Micro Systems Limited (FY 2025-26)

The accounting policy is set out in **Note 3.15** of the consolidated financial statements of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]], and the resulting disclosures appear in **Note 34** of [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part10]].

### Contingent liabilities disclosed (Note 34)

| Item | 31 March 2026 (₹ lakhs) | 31 March 2025 (₹ lakhs) |
|---|---|---|
| Claims against the Company not acknowledged as debts | 6,356.28 | 321.44 |
| Outstanding guarantees and counter-guarantees to banks | 4,832.63 | 2,304.44 |

- The **claims** relate to pending litigations and proceedings with Tax / Statutory / Government Authorities. The Company states it has made adequate provisions wherever required and does not expect the outcome to have a material impact on its financial position. The associated uncertain tax positions (disallowances considered more likely than not to be accepted by tax authorities) are discussed in the tax note alongside [[concepts/deferred-tax]].
- The **guarantees and counter-guarantees** are issued on behalf of customers in favour of banks in the ordinary course of business. Management considers the likelihood of any material obligation arising to be remote, so no provision is recognised; the disclosed amount represents the maximum potential exposure.
- Future cash outflows for these items are determinable only on receipt of judgments/decisions pending with various forums and authorities.

### Commitments (distinguished from provisions)

Note 34 also discloses **commitments**, which are not provisions because they are future executory obligations:

- Estimated contracts on capital account remaining to be executed and not provided for: **₹2,130.91 lakhs** (31 March 2026) relating mainly to construction of a new factory building at Shamshabad.
- The prior-year commitment was ₹561.55 lakhs.

These link directly to the asset-side disclosures in [[concepts/capital-work-in-progress]] and [[concepts/property-plant-and-equipment]].

### Provision balances recognised elsewhere

Not all provisions are contingent. Recognised provisions include:

- **Employee benefit provisions** (gratuity and compensated absences), split between non-current and current, as detailed under [[concepts/employee-benefits]].
- **Current tax liabilities and earlier-year tax adjustments**, discussed under [[concepts/deferred-tax]].
- **Expected credit loss allowances** on trade receivables, loans and advances, which are valuation allowances rather than provisions in the Ind AS 37 sense but follow a similar probability-weighted logic.

## Distinguishing Provisions from Related Items

| Item | Recognition | Measurement basis |
|---|---|---|
| Provision | Recognised | Best estimate, discounted if material |
| Contingent liability | Not recognised (disclosed unless remote) | Disclosed amount |
| Contingent asset | Not recognised (disclosed if probable) | Disclosed amount |
| Commitment | Not recognised | Contractual amount not yet provided for |
| Financial liability (see [[concepts/financial-instruments]]) | Recognised | Amortised cost or fair value |

## Why This Matters

- Provisions and contingencies are a key area of **management judgement**, making them a frequent [[concepts/key-audit-matters|key audit matter]] and a focus of [[concepts/internal-financial-controls]].
- The distinction between probable, possible and remote drives whether an amount hits the balance sheet as a provision, appears only in the notes as a contingency, or is omitted entirely.
- Disclosure of guarantees, litigation and capital commitments is an important transparency mechanism in [[concepts/consolidated-financial-statements]].
