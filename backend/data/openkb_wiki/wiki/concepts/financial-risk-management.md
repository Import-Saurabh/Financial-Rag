# Financial Risk Management

**Financial risk management** is the set of objectives, policies and quantitative analyses by which an enterprise identifies, measures and mitigates the risks arising from its financial assets and liabilities. For the Apollo Micro Systems group, the principal financial liabilities are loans and borrowings, trade payables and other payables — used to finance and support operations — while the principal financial assets are trade and other receivables, cash and cash equivalents, and refundable deposits arising directly from operations.

Senior management oversees the management of these risks, and the Board of Directors reviews and agrees the policies for managing each risk.

## Risk categories

The group discloses three broad categories of financial risk:

### Market risk
Market risk is the risk that the fair value of future cash flows of a financial instrument will fluctuate because of changes in market prices. It comprises:
- **Interest rate risk** — arising primarily from long-term debt obligations with floating interest rates.
- **Foreign currency risk** — arising from operating activities where revenue or expense is denominated in a foreign currency.

Financial instruments affected by market risk include loans and borrowings and refundable deposits.

### Credit risk
Credit risk is the risk that a counterparty will not meet its obligations under a financial instrument or customer contract, leading to a financial loss. It arises principally from operating activities (primarily trade receivables) and from investing activities (deposits with banks and financial institutions). Credit risk is controlled by analysing credit limits and the creditworthiness of customers on a continuous basis, after obtaining necessary approvals for credit. The group establishes an allowance for credit loss based on past and recent collection trends, and credit risk on cash and cash equivalents is limited because the group transacts with banks and financial institutions carrying high credit ratings.

### Liquidity risk
Liquidity risk is the risk that the group cannot meet its obligations as they fall due. The group's objective is to maintain a balance between continuity of funding and flexibility through the use of bank deposits and loans. The maturity profile of financial liabilities is disclosed on a contractual undiscounted basis.

## Link to capital structure

Financial risk management is closely tied to [[concepts/capital-management]]. As at 31 March 2026, total equity attributable to shareholders was ₹1,30,822.03 lakhs (71.1% of total capital) against total borrowings of ₹53,241.21 lakhs (28.9%), giving the group a debt-equity ratio of 0.81 (FY25: 1.14). The interest rate sensitivity above is driven by the floating-rate portion of these borrowings; the group manages interest rate risk by keeping a balanced portfolio of fixed and variable rate loans and does not enter into interest rate swaps.

## Relationship to other disclosures

- The fair values and carrying amounts of the instruments exposed to these risks are set out under [[concepts/financial-instruments]], measured using the Level 1/2/3 fair value hierarchy.
- Credit exposure is assessed partly through the credit quality of counterparty institutions, reflected in the group's [[concepts/credit-rating]] disclosures.
- Lease obligations, a further source of financing-related risk, are disclosed under [[concepts/leases]] (Ind AS 116).
- The sensitivity and ratio disclosures form part of the [[concepts/consolidated-financial-statements]] of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] and are presented alongside [[concepts/financial-ratios]] in the notes to the accounts.
