## Rukhya Parveen

Rukhya Parveen is the **Company Secretary & Compliance Officer** of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] and a member of its Key Managerial Personnel (KMP). She is designated as the Compliance Officer pursuant to the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

### Role and Responsibilities
- Serves as Secretary to all Board Committees of the Company, including the Audit Committee, Nomination & Remuneration Committee, Stakeholders Relationship Committee, Corporate Social Responsibility Committee, Risk Management Committee, Executive Committee of Directors & CFO, and Securities Allotment Committee.
- As Compliance Officer, she is responsible for the Company's compliance with SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 and related disclosure obligations.
- Participates in Board and Committee meetings as the designated secretary.
- Holds Company Secretary membership no. **A65112** and is based in Hyderabad.

### Insider Trading Compliance
- Appointed as the **Compliance Officer** under the Company's Code of Internal Procedures and Conduct to Regulate, Monitor and Report Trading by Insiders, framed under the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015.
- In this capacity she is responsible for ensuring compliance with, and the effective implementation of, the Regulations and the Code across the Company.
- The Code, revised in line with the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) (Amendment) Regulations, 2020, lays down guidelines for the procedures and disclosures required when trading in the Company's securities. See [[concepts/insider-trading]].

### Remuneration (FY 2025-26)
- Remuneration paid: **₹11.55 Lakhs** for FY 2025-26, disclosed as a related party transaction (short-term employee benefits) in the related party disclosures (FY 2024-25: ₹11.08 Lakhs). See [[concepts/related-party-transactions]].
- She is identified among the Company's related parties in the consolidated financial statements as **Company Secretary (Key Management Personnel)**, and her compensation is included in the disclosure of compensation paid to key management personnel.
- Her remuneration forms part of the aggregate KMP remuneration of ₹263.55 Lakhs for FY 2025-26 (FY 2024-25: ₹263.01 Lakhs).
- Remuneration payable (outstanding) at 31 March 2026: **₹1.19 Lakhs** (31 March 2025: ₹0.89 Lakhs).
- Ratio of her remuneration to the Median Remuneration of Employees (MRE): **4.09**.
- Percentage increase/(decrease) in remuneration: **Nil** for FY 2025-26.

### Financial Statement Authorization
- She signed the standalone financial statements of the Company in her capacity as Company Secretary and Compliance Officer, in the Board meeting held on **18 May 2026**, alongside Managing Director [[entities/karunakar_reddy_baddam]], Director [[entities/krishna_sai_kumar_addepalli]], and CFO [[entities/sudarshan_chiluveru]].
- She also signed the **Consolidated Financial Statements** for the year ended 31 March 2026 as Company Secretary, in the same meeting held at Hyderabad on 18 May 2026, alongside the same set of KMP and directors (Membership No: A65112). See [[concepts/consolidated-financial-statements]].
- The consolidated financial statements were approved by the Board of Directors and authorised for issue on 18 May 2026 (Note 55 of the consolidated financial statements), and were audited by [[entities/s_t_mohite_and_co]], Chartered Accountants, with CA Hima Bindu Sagala signing as Partner on the audit report. The Board confirmed that no material subsequent events had occurred after the reporting date up to the date of approval that required adjustment to or disclosure in the financial statements.
- The group financial statements (Notes 1 to 55) carry the signatures of the auditors and of the KMP and directors as per the report of even date.

### Governance Context
Her role supports the Company's [[concepts/corporate-governance-and-compliance]] framework, which emphasizes transparency and accountability as core tenets. She works alongside fellow KMP colleagues such as CFO [[entities/sudarshan_chiluveru]] and Managing Director [[entities/karunakar_reddy_baddam]]. Her compliance remit sits alongside the Company's broader integrity mechanisms, including the whistle-blower policy and vigil mechanism, which guarantee employees and Board members confidential channels for reporting unethical behaviour, suspected fraud or Code of Conduct violations. Her approvals of the financial statements also sit within the Company's [[concepts/internal-financial-controls]] and [[concepts/audit-trail-rule-11g]] framework; the consolidated financial statements confirm that the Company used Tally accounting software with an audit trail (edit log) facility that operated throughout the year for all relevant transactions, with no instances of tampering and prior-year audit trails preserved as per statutory record-retention requirements.
