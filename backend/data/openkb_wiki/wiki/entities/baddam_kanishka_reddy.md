# Baddam Kanishka Reddy

Baddam Kanishka Reddy is a promoter shareholder of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]], the defence electronics company whose consolidated financial statements are summarized in [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part10]].

## Shareholding

According to the promoter shareholding details disclosed in the FY 2025-26 annual report (Note 13 – Share capital in the consolidated financial statements):

- As at 31 March 2026, Baddam Kanishka Reddy held **84,00,600 equity shares** of ₹1 each, representing **2.35%** of the Company's total shares.
- At the beginning of the year (1 April 2025), she held **no shares**; the entire 84,00,600 shares were acquired during the financial year 2025-26 as a change in promoter holdings.
- At the FY 2024-25 reporting date, neither she nor [[entities/baddam_chanakya_reddy]] appeared in the promoter schedule; only [[entities/karunakar_reddy_baddam]] and [[entities/srilakshmi_reddy_vangeti]] were listed.

## Context

Baddam Kanishka Reddy appears alongside other promoter group members in the promoter shareholding schedule, including [[entities/karunakar_reddy_baddam]] (the largest promoter holder, at 47.28% as at 31 March 2026, down from 55.12% a year earlier) and [[entities/srilakshmi_reddy_vangeti]]. Baddam Kanishka Reddy acquired the same number of shares (84,00,600) during the year as fellow promoter [[entities/baddam_chanakya_reddy]], suggesting a common promoter-group transaction. Together, the promoter holdings totaled 18,57,24,200 shares at year end.

The share capital expansion during FY 2025-26 resulted from a preferential issue of 2,70,42,894 equity shares at ₹1 each at a premium of ₹113 and the conversion of 2,37,59,986 warrants, which increased total issued capital to 35,72,92,440 equity shares of ₹1 each from 30,64,89,560 shares at the start of the year.
