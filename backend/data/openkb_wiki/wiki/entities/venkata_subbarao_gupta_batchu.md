# Venkata Subbarao Gupta Batchu

Sri Venkata Subbarao Gupta Batchu is a Director of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] and is identified as Key Management Personnel (KMP) in the Company's consolidated financial statements for the year ended 31 March 2026.

## Role and Classification

- Listed among the Company's Directors / Key Management Personnel in the [[concepts/related-party-transactions|related party disclosures]] (Note 35) of the consolidated financial statements.
- Identified as a related party under Ind AS 24 – Related Party Disclosures, as per the terms of Division II of Schedule III to the Companies Act, 2013.

## Transactions and Balances (FY 2025-26)

- No remuneration or other transaction with him is separately disclosed in the compensation table for the year.
- **Loan payable**: A loan payable to Sri Venkata Subbarao Gupta Batchu of ₹3.21 lakhs is outstanding as at 31 March 2026, unchanged from ₹3.21 lakhs as at 31 March 2025.
- Outstanding balances with related parties at year-end are unsecured and interest-free, and transactions are made on terms equivalent to those prevailing in arm's length transactions.

## Context

- The term loans and working capital facilities of the Company are further secured by the personal guarantees of two Directors and others in their individual capacities; the directors providing such guarantees are not individually named in this note.
- He shares the surname Batchu with fellow KMP [[entities/satyanarayana_batchu]], also a Director of the Company.
