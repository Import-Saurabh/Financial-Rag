# Vehicle Mounted Counter Drone System

## Overview

The Vehicle Mounted Counter Drone System is a mobile, integrated solution developed by [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] for the detection, tracking, identification, and neutralisation of hostile unmanned aerial threats. It is part of the company's [[concepts/counter-drone-systems]] portfolio and was highlighted among the key research and development achievements in FY 2025-26.

## Key Features

- **Mobility**: Mounted on a high-mobility vehicle platform, the system delivers on-the-move capability, making it suited for convoy protection, forward-deployed formations, and mobile air defence gaps.
- **Sensor suite**: Combines radar, RF detection, and electro-optical sensors to build a layered picture of the airspace.
- **Countermeasures**: Integrates both soft-kill and hard-kill countermeasures for layered protection against hostile drones.
- **Modular architecture**: The system architecture is modular and scalable, allowing configuration to specific user requirements.

## Strategic Context

With drone warfare now central to the modern battlefield, the system addresses one of the most urgent operational needs of the Armed Forces. It reflects the company's strengths in sensor fusion, real-time processing, and system integration, and supports the transition of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] from a subsystem supplier toward a complete weapon platform developer.

## Related Products

The system sits alongside other indigenous platforms under development at the company, including the [[entities/mini_torpedo]], [[entities/limpet_mines]], [[entities/moored_mine]], and the [[entities/asw_weapon_platform]], which together reflect a broadening portfolio in [[concepts/underwater-warfare]] and air-defence domains.
