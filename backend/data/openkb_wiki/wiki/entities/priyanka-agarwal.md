# Priyanka Agarwal

**Role:** Company Secretary & Compliance Officer, [[entities/mtar-technologies]]

## What This Entity Is

Priyanka Agarwal is the Company Secretary and Compliance Officer of MTAR Technologies Limited, the officer responsible for the company's statutory filings and secretarial compliance with Indian securities-market regulations. In the knowledge base she appears as the signatory of the company's exchange disclosures — the formal interface between MTAR and the stock exchanges.

Her filing is the mechanism by which the management commentary delivered on the call — by [[entities/srinivas-reddy]] (Managing Director), [[entities/gunneswara-rao]] (CFO) and [[entities/srilekha-jasthi]] (Head, Strategy & IR) — becomes a formal, publicly archived record.
