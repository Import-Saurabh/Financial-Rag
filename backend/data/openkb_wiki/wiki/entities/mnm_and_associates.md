MNM & Associates is a firm of Practising Company Secretaries (Firm Registration No. P2017TL059600) engaged by [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] to provide secretarial and corporate governance certification services. Its partner, [[entities/sridevi_madati]], signs the firm's certifications for the company.

## Role in the FY 2025-26 Annual Report

MNM & Associates appears in the statutory reports section of the company's Annual Report 2025-26, where it issued two certificates relating to [[concepts/corporate-governance-and-compliance]]:

### Certificate on Corporate Governance (Annexure-I)

- Examined the company's compliance with the conditions of Corporate Governance for the year ended 31 March 2026 as stipulated in Chapter IV of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.
- Certified that the company complied with those conditions.
- The certificate notes that compliance is the responsibility of management, that the examination was limited to procedures and implementation, and that it is neither an audit nor an expression of opinion on the financial statements.
- It further states that such compliance is not an assurance of future viability or of the efficiency and effectiveness of management.
- Dated 8 September 2026, signed at Hyderabad by [[entities/sridevi_madati]], Partner (M.No. F6476, COP 11694, UDIN: F006476H001388351).

### Certificate on Non-disqualification of Directors

- Confirmed that none of the directors of the company is debarred or disqualified by the [[entities/securities_and_exchange_board_of_india]] or the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, or any such authority, from being appointed or continuing as a director.
- The certificate is uploaded on the company's website in the "Investor" section.

## Context

MNM & Associates forms part of the external assurance layer around the company's listed-entity obligations, alongside the statutory auditors and the Chief Financial Officer certification issued by [[entities/sudarshan_chiluveru]]. Its work supports the governance and disclosure items described in [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part5]], including the compliance status table covering Regulations 17 to 27 and Regulation 46(2)(b) to (i) of the SEBI Listing Regulations.
