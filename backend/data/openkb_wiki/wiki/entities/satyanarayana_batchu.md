# Satyanarayana Batchu

Satyanarayana Batchu is a **Director** of Apollo Micro Systems Limited and is identified as **Key Management Personnel (KMP)** in the Company's related party disclosures under Ind AS 24 for the year ended 31 March 2026.

## Role and Classification

- Listed in the consolidated financial statements (Note 35, Related party disclosures) as Sri. Satyanarayana Batchu, Director (Key Management Personnel).
- Identified as a related party of the Group alongside other directors, KMP and subsidiaries.
- Likely among the "two Directors" whose personal guarantees secure the Company's bank and NBFC borrowings, as disclosed in the borrowing notes for FY 2025-26.

## Key Facts from the FY 2025-26 Annual Report

- No remuneration or transactions specific to him are separately disclosed in the compensation table for key management personnel for FY 2025-26 or FY 2024-25, which covers only the Managing Director and executive directors/CFO/Company Secretary.
- Related party transactions across the Group are stated to be on terms equivalent to arm's length transactions, with outstanding balances unsecured and interest-free.
