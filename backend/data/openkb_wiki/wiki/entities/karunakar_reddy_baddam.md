# Karunakar Reddy Baddam

Karunakar Reddy Baddam is the Founder and Managing Director of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]], an Indian multidisciplinary defence technology company headquartered in Hyderabad. His DIN is 00790139, and he is a signatory of the company's regulatory correspondence with the stock exchanges, including the covering letter dated 08 September 2026 that submitted the Annual Report 2025-26 and the notice of the 29th AGM to [[entities/bse_limited]] and [[entities/national_stock_exchange_of_india]]. He is also the signatory of several statutory annexures to the FY26 report, including Form AOC-1, Form AOC-2, the remuneration disclosures under Section 197(12), the Business Responsibility & Sustainability Report statement as the Director responsible for it, and the Declaration as to adherence to the Code of Conduct for the financial year ended 31 March 2026. He additionally signed the standalone financial statements for FY 2025-26, as well as the consolidated financial statements for the year ended 31 March 2026, both approved for issue by the Board of Directors at their meeting held on 18 May 2026 in Hyderabad, signing as Managing Director; he appears as a signatory to "Note 1 to 55" of the consolidated financial statements, which carry his DIN 00790139.

## Role and Profile

The 2025-26 Annual Report describes him as bringing **over 41 years of rich business experience** to the role. Under his leadership, Apollo Micro Systems transformed from a design-focused firm into a comprehensive solutions provider now delivering both subsystems and complete system solutions.

He has been instrumental in pioneering innovative solutions for defence and space programmes, leveraging cutting-edge ideas and miniaturisation techniques. His pursuit of excellence and commitment to developing import substitution strategies are cited as pivotal to the company's success. The report credits him with providing the guidance, direction and motivation that propelled the company to new heights, and with an entrepreneurial spirit that has fostered sustainable growth.

## Reflections in the FY26 Annual Report

In the Managing Director's Message, titled "Building Capabilities. Scaling Platforms. Delivering for India and Beyond," he framed FY 2025-26 as "a defining year for Apollo Micro Systems — a year of decisive execution and strategic transformation." He opened the letter with the observation that "some years are more than milestones; they are defining moments. They separate those who wait for certainty from those who have the courage to build through it."

Key points from his message:

- **Financial performance** — Revenue grew 61% year-on-year to ₹904 crore from ₹562 crore, while PAT rose 91% to ₹107 crore from ₹56 crore, described as representing the growing scale of capabilities and deepening trust as a technology partner to India's defence and strategic sectors.
- **R&D investment** — ₹72 crore, or 8% of revenue, invested in R&D, among the highest in the industry, with zero attrition in the R&D team. Outputs cited include the Mini Torpedo, advanced sensor suites for underwater autonomous vehicles, and FOG-based INS systems (see [[concepts/inertial-navigation-systems]] and [[concepts/underwater-warfare]]).
- **Licences** — He highlighted the grant of a **Lifetime Arms Manufacturing Licence by DPIIT**, authorising manufacture, assembly, integration and proof-testing of complete strategic weapon systems including Missiles, ATGMs, Torpedoes, Underwater Mines, Aerial Bombs, Rockets, Loitering Munitions, Chaffs, Flares and Decoys, alongside licensed Unmanned Aerial Systems, Inertial Navigation Systems and Radar Equipment (see [[concepts/defence-manufacturing-licensing]]). He described the combination of electronics, integration and energetics under one roof as "the architecture of a complete weapon-system OEM, and very few companies globally possess it" (see [[concepts/defence-energetics]] and [[concepts/tier_1_defense_oem_integration]]).
- **IDL Explosives** — He noted that [[entities/idl_explosives]], the step-down subsidiary under [[entities/apollo_defence_industries_private_limited]], received an Industrial Licence for HMX and TNT, marking Apollo's entry into specialised defence energetics. Elsewhere, the Group's broader DPIIT and industrial licences cover UAVs, INS systems, radars, torpedoes, underwater mines, ATGMs, decoys, chaffs and flares, aerial bombs, rockets, missiles and loitering munitions.
- **Capacity expansion** — Reference to additional land allotted by [[entities/tsiic]] Hardware Park Phase-II, taking the footprint there to approximately 10.68 acres, with a ₹300 crore investment for advanced weapon platforms including Grad Rockets, ASW Rockets, Anti-Tank Mines and Artillery Munitions; and an MoU with the Government of Telangana for a greenfield Defence and High-Energetic Explosives project with proposed investment of approximately ₹1,500 crore.
- **Future warfare** — Positioning Apollo across UAVs, counter-drone systems including Directed Energy Weapons, autonomous underwater platforms, missiles, torpedoes, electronic warfare, seekers, avionics and artillery systems, shifting focus from individual products to complete integrated platforms (see [[concepts/counter-drone-systems]] and [[concepts/directed-energy-weapons]]).
- **Global ambition** — Growth rests on two complementary engines: deepening position in India's defence procurement ecosystem and building a durable presence in global markets. The receipt of the first export order was described as "an early but meaningful signal of what Apollo can become," to be grown "patiently — order by order, delivery by delivery" (see [[concepts/defense-exports-and-global-market-entry]]).
- **Execution priorities** — Execute customer programmes, commission manufacturing capacity, deepen technology, develop talent and deploy capital prudently, "turning licences into products, products into platforms, platforms into programmes and programmes into enduring capabilities."
- **Acknowledgements** — Thanks to employees, the Armed Forces, [[entities/drdo]], government institutions, technology partners and shareholders, with the intent to build "a stronger Apollo, and through it, a more self-reliant, technologically advanced and secure India" (see [[concepts/defense_indigenization]]).
