## Overview

CA. Hima Bindu Sagala is a partner of [[entities/s_t_mohite_and_co]], the Chartered Accountants firm that serves as statutory auditor of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]]. She signed the Independent Auditor's Report on the standalone financial statements of the Company for the year ended 31 March 2026, issued from Hyderabad on 18 May 2026. She also signed the Independent Auditor's Report on the **consolidated financial statements** of the Company and its subsidiaries for the same year, together with **Annexure A** on internal financial controls with reference to those consolidated statements. She is the signing partner on the accompanying Notes 1 to 53 forming part of the standalone financial statements and Notes 1 to 55 forming part of the consolidated financial statements, both approved for issue by the Board of Directors on the same date (18 May 2026).

- **Place and date of signature**: Hyderabad, 18 May 2026.

## Audit opinions signed

- An **unqualified (clean) opinion** that the standalone financial statements give a true and fair view of the Company's state of affairs as at 31 March 2026, in conformity with Indian Accounting Standards.
- An **unqualified (clean) opinion** that the [[concepts/consolidated-financial-statements]] give a true and fair view of the consolidated state of affairs of the Group (the Holding Company, its two subsidiaries and two step-down subsidiaries) as at 31 March 2026, and of its consolidated profit, changes in equity and cash flows for the year then ended, in conformity with Ind AS.
- **Annexure A** under the Companies (Auditor's Report) Order, 2020, covering property, plant and equipment, inventories, loans and investments, statutory dues, share capital and CSR.
- **Annexure B** on the adequacy and operating effectiveness of [[concepts/internal-financial-controls]] with reference to the standalone financial statements, which she reported as adequate and effective.
- **Annexure A to the consolidated report** expressing an unmodified opinion on the adequacy and operating effectiveness of internal financial controls with reference to the consolidated financial statements.

## Matters addressed in her reports

Her standalone report identified five [[concepts/key-audit-matters]]: [[concepts/related-party-transactions]]; revenue recognition under Ind AS 115 ([[concepts/revenue-recognition]]); [[concepts/capital-work-in-progress]] of ₹17,059.72 lakhs; inventories of ₹76,000.75 lakhs and their [[concepts/inventory-obsolescence]] risk; and the business combination accounting for [[entities/idl_explosives]], where provisional goodwill of ₹14,517.66 lakhs was recognised under [[concepts/ind-as-103-business-combinations]].

Her consolidated report addressed four [[concepts/key-audit-matters]]: revenue recognition under Ind AS 115 ([[concepts/revenue-recognition]]); [[concepts/capital-work-in-progress]] aggregating ₹19,629.40 lakhs (testing tools and instruments of ₹2,341.49 lakhs and civil works of ₹17,287.91 lakhs, including ₹7,512.88 lakhs judged against Ind AS 16 recognition criteria); inventories of ₹79,528.92 lakhs representing 47.76% of total current assets and 33.58% of total assets, with customer-specific obsolescence risk ([[concepts/inventory-obsolescence]]); and the accounting for the [[concepts/ind-as-103-business-combinations]] acquisition of [[entities/idl_explosives]], which became a wholly-owned step-down subsidiary, resulting in provisional [[concepts/goodwill]] of ₹14,517.66 lakhs recognised on book values pending a Purchase Price Allocation within the twelve-month measurement period.

Notable other findings in the standalone report included income-tax disputes of ₹834.36 lakhs pending before the Commissioner of Income-tax (Appeals), non-compliance noted in the maintenance of books of account in respect of the audit trail under Rule 11(g) ([[concepts/audit-trail-rule-11g]]), and CSR expenditure of ₹74.80 lakhs against an obligation of ₹105.43 lakhs.

Her consolidated report noted the Group's revenue from operations of ₹90,432.38 lakhs, profit for the year of ₹10,738.04 lakhs, total assets of ₹2,36,812.03 lakhs, and the issue of 5,08,02,880 equity shares along with share warrants during the year. Under Rule 11 of the Companies (Audit and Auditors) Rules, 2014 she reported disclosure of the impact of pending litigations (Note 34), no long-term contracts including derivatives with material foreseeable losses, no delay in transferring amounts to the Investor Education and Protection Fund, and no material misstatements in the Rule 11(e) representations regarding ultimate beneficiaries and funding parties. She recorded that no qualifications or adverse remarks arose in the CARO reports of the Company and its subsidiaries.
