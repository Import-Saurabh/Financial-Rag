# Apollo Defence Industries Private Limited

Apollo Defence Industries Private Limited (ADIPL, CIN: U26109TS2023PTC177370) is an aerospace and defence subsidiary of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]], incorporated in India and featured in the company's statutory disclosures and financial statements such as Form AOC-1. ADIPL holds a direct or indirect shareholding of 76% attributable to the listed entity and serves as a key vehicle for the Group's expansion into defence manufacturing and energetics.

## Overview and Financial Highlights
As detailed in the annual report summaries (see [[summaries/2025_Financial_Year_2025_from_bse_part2]] and [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part4]]), the company's salient financial features are reported under Form AOC-1. For FY 2025-26, ADIPL reported share capital of ₹1.00 lakh, negative reserves and surplus of ₹1,558.34 lakhs, total assets of ₹11,011.26 lakhs, total liabilities of ₹12,568.50 lakhs, investments of ₹10,703.24 lakhs, and turnover of ₹182.68 lakhs, resulting in a net loss after tax of ₹1,547.70 lakhs. This reflects its early-stage investment and integration phase. The company's contribution to the overall performance of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] is stated as 76%, consistent with its shareholding.

In the Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting (BRSR) disclosure for FY 2025-26 (see [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part5]]), ADIPL is listed among the Group's holding, subsidiary and associate companies as a **Subsidiary** of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] with **76.00%** of shares held by the listed entity. The BRSR also records that ADIPL does not participate in the Business Responsibility initiatives of the listed entity — a position shared by all of the Group's subsidiary and step-down subsidiary entities. See [[concepts/business-responsibility-and-sustainability-reporting]].

Alongside it are [[entities/ananya_sip_rf_technologies_private_limited]] (Subsidiary, 51%), and the step-down subsidiaries [[entities/apollo_strategic_technologies_private_limited]] (Step-down subsidiary, 51%) and [[entities/idl_explosives]] (Step-down subsidiary, 100%). ## Acquisition of IDL Explosives Limited
During FY 2025-26, ADIPL acquired a 100% equity stake in [[entities/idl_explosives]] for a total consideration of ₹107 crore (₹10,700.00 lakhs) in an all-cash transaction. Per the goodwill note (Note 47 of [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part11]]), ADIPL entered into a Share Purchase Agreement dated **02 May 2025** to acquire 100% of the equity share capital of IDL Explosives Limited, with the transaction completed on **15 November 2025** (the "acquisition date"), on which date ADIPL obtained control of IDL, thereby making IDL a wholly owned step-down subsidiary of the Company. This acquisition was described in the FY26 annual report as central to Apollo's strategic transformation, extending the Group's participation across the defence value chain from weapon-system electronics into explosives and energetics (see [[concepts/defence-energetics]] and [[concepts/defence-acquisitions-and-integration]]).

IDL is engaged in the manufacture of industrial explosives, accessories and allied products and qualifies as a business as defined in Ind AS 103. The acquisition was undertaken to expand the Group's defence and explosives manufacturing capability and to obtain access to IDL's manufacturing licences and infrastructure. IDL operates manufacturing units across India's key mining belts, with long-standing relationships with Coal India subsidiaries and institutional customers, and a growing dealer and export franchise. Through IDL, the Group secured an industrial licence for the manufacture of high explosives including HMX and TNT, marking its entry into specialised defence energetics and enabling backward integration into critical raw materials and compounds.

### Consideration and Provisional Purchase Price Allocation
Per Note 47, the total purchase consideration was ₹10,700.00 lakhs, paid in cash on completion. The acquisition resulted in a **net cash outflow of ₹8,965.22 lakhs**, after deducting cash and cash equivalents acquired of ₹1,734.78 lakhs. The amounts recognised in respect of identifiable assets acquired and liabilities assumed, measured at their **provisional** fair values as at the acquisition date, were:

- Property, plant and equipment: ₹4,888.12 lakhs
- Capital work-in-progress: ₹2,309.28 lakhs
- Intangible assets: ₹24.01 lakhs
- Inventories: ₹3,116.69 lakhs
- Trade receivables: ₹3,786.10 lakhs
- Cash and cash equivalents: ₹2,174.57 lakhs
- Other financial and non-financial assets: ₹4,790.91 lakhs
- Trade payables and other financial liabilities: ₹3,768.49 lakhs
- Borrowings: ₹20,721.03 lakhs
- Provisions: ₹417.83 lakhs
- **Total identifiable net assets acquired: ₹(3,817.67) lakhs**
- **Goodwill arising on acquisition: ₹14,517.67 lakhs**

The goodwill of ₹14,517.67 lakhs is attributable to the assembled work force, manufacturing know-how and statutory licences held by IDL, and synergies expected from integration with the Group's defence manufacturing business, none of which qualify for separate recognition as an identifiable intangible asset. The goodwill so recognised is **not expected to be deductible for income tax purposes**.

As at 31 March 2026, the initial accounting for the business combination is incomplete and the amounts disclosed are accordingly **provisional**, because the Company is finalising the independent valuation of property, plant and equipment and identifiable intangible assets and the assessment of provisions/contingent liabilities relating to statutory, regulatory and environmental matters specific to an explosives manufacturing licence holder. In accordance with Ind AS 103.45–50, the Company will retrospectively adjust the provisional amounts recognised as at the acquisition date, with a corresponding adjustment to goodwill. The measurement period will not exceed one year from the acquisition date, concluding no later than **15 November 2026**. See [[concepts/ind-as-103-business-combinations]] and [[concepts/goodwill]].

Acquisition-related costs of **₹2.73 lakhs**, comprising legal, due diligence and advisory fees, were recognised as an expense in the Statement of Profit and Loss for the year ended 31 March 2026.

### Contribution to Group Results
Per Note 47, IDL contributed revenue of **₹13,764.39 lakhs** and a profit/(loss) after tax of **₹(262.82) lakhs** to the Group for the period from the acquisition date (15 November 2025) to 31 March 2026, as included in the consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss. Had the acquisition been completed on 1 April 2025 (the first day of the financial year), the Group's revenue for the year ended 31 March 2026 would have been **₹1,14,647.77 lakhs** and the Group's profit for the year would have been **₹7,936.79 lakhs**. The step-down subsidiary disclosures separately reported IDL turnover of ₹37,979.77 lakhs and a net loss after tax of ₹3,064.15 lakhs for the period following acquisition.

## Step-Down Subsidiaries
ADIPL is the holding company for two step-down subsidiaries of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]]:

- **[[entities/apollo_strategic_technologies_private_limited]]** (CIN: U26109TS2024PTC191803): ADIPL holds a 51% stake. The company became a step-down subsidiary on 10 December 2024 and is yet to commence business operations.
- **[[entities/idl_explosives]]** (CIN: U28132TG2010PLC070529): ADIPL holds 100% equity. It became a step-down subsidiary on 15 November 2025.

The FY 2025-26 BRSR confirms both holdings, listing [[entities/apollo_strategic_technologies_private_limited]] as a **Step down Subsidiary** with 51.00% held and [[entities/idl_explosives]] as a **Step down Subsidiary** with 100.00% held through ADIPL; neither participates in the listed entity's Business Responsibility initiatives (see [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part5]]). There are no associate or joint venture companies of the Group.

In the standalone financial statements of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] for FY 2025-26, ADIPL is listed among the identified related parties as a **Subsidiary company** (see [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part8]]).

## Investment, Loans and Guarantees in the Standalone Financial Statements
In the standalone financial statements of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]], investments in subsidiaries are carried at cost less accumulated impairment (see [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part7]]). The parent holds 7,600 equity shares of ₹10 each in ADIPL, with a carrying value of ₹0.76 lakhs as at 31 March 2026, unchanged from the prior year, and no impairment has been recorded against this investment. In addition, a deemed investment of ₹60.00 lakhs had been recognised in the previous year in respect of a corporate financial guarantee provided to ADIPL; this deemed investment was extinguished during FY 2025-26 upon repayment of the underlying borrowing, so that no deemed investment was outstanding as at the reporting date.

During FY 2025-26, [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] extended loans to ADIPL, of which ₹12,170.24 lakhs of principal and ₹262.47 lakhs of accrued interest — a combined ₹12,432.70 lakhs — remained outstanding as on 31 March 2026 (see [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part3]], [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part7]] and [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part8]]). The loan is unsecured, carries interest at 9.90% p.a. payable quarterly, and has no stipulated repayment period. Net loans and advances to ADIPL during the year amounted to ₹12,069.24 lakhs (FY 2024-25: ₹101.00 lakhs) and interest received during the year was ₹669.82 lakhs (FY 2024-25: ₹0.30 lakhs). ADIPL is one of the principal subsidiaries to which the parent's intercorporate funding is directed, alongside [[entities/ananya_sip_rf_technologies_private_limited]], [[entities/apollo_strategic_technologies_private_limited]], and [[entities/idl_explosives]].

Per Section 186(4) disclosures, the parent holds 7,600 equity shares of ₹10 each in ADIPL (carrying value ₹0.76 lakhs), a loan without repayment period of ₹12,432.70 lakhs as at 31 March 2026 (FY 2024-25: ₹101.00 lakhs), and a corporate finance guarantee that stood at ₹nil as at 31 March 2026 (FY 2024-25: ₹5,000.00 lakhs) (see [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part8]]).

## Related Party Transactions and Guarantees
ADIPL engages in arm's length transactions with [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]], including loans, advances, job work charges, and rent transactions. In FY 2025-26, gross transactions with the parent totalled ₹12,756.33 lakhs, with ₹333.81 lakhs transacted in the current financial year up to the quarter preceding the approval sought. At the Group level, the FY 2025-26 BRSR reports that loans and advances given to related parties represented 97.83% of total loans and advances, and investments in related parties represented 100% of total investments made (see [[concepts/related-party-transactions]]).

The FY 2025-26 related party disclosures in the standalone financial statements record the following transactions between the parent and ADIPL (see [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part8]]):

- Loans and advances (net): ₹12,069.24 lakhs (FY 2024-25: ₹101.00 lakhs)
- Interest received: ₹669.82 lakhs (FY 2024-25: ₹0.30 lakhs)
- Rent received: ₹0.60 lakhs (FY 2024-25: ₹0.60 lakhs)
- Guarantee commission received: ₹16.67 lakhs (FY 2024-25: nil)
- Deferred guarantee commission income recognition (net): ₹(120.00) lakhs (FY 2024-25: ₹120.00 lakhs)
- Corporate finance guarantee (net): ₹(5,000.00) lakhs (FY 2024-25: ₹5,000.00 lakhs)

Outstanding balances as at 31 March 2026 comprised the loan of ₹12,170.24 lakhs and interest of ₹262.47 lakhs. The deferred guarantee commission income of ₹120.00 lakhs and the corporate finance guarantee of ₹5,000.00 lakhs that were outstanding at the prior year-end were reversed to nil during the year, and the rent balance of ₹0.15 lakhs outstanding at the prior year-end was also cleared. The guarantee commission of ₹16.67 lakhs received during the year is separately reported within the parent's other income under "Corporate guarantee commission."

Apollo Micro Systems received rent of ₹0.60 lakhs from ADIPL during the year, approved by the Board on 23 July 2025. Under the material related party transaction approvals sought at the 29th AGM (see [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part2]]), the company proposed intercorporate loans and/or corporate guarantees to ADIPL of up to ₹150 crores during FY 2027—representing 16.59% of the listed entity's consolidated turnover—to be advanced as and when required. These facilities carry a proposed interest rate of 9.90%, are unsecured, have a one-year tenure, are sourced from internal accruals, and are intended to meet day-to-day working capital requirements and acquisitions. The FY26 RPT disclosures confirm that funds to ADIPL are applied toward day-to-day working capital needs and investment in projects and for acquisitions, that repayment terms are as mutually agreed between the parties, and that the facilities are unsecured.

Because these transactions are undertaken under omnibus approval with the final transaction amount not yet determined, ADIPL's Debt to Equity Ratio before and after the transaction cannot be computed with reference to a specific figure; its Debt Service Coverage Ratio before the transaction is reported as (0.13). ADIPL has not been classified as a non-performing asset by any banker, has not been declared a wilful defaulter, and is not undergoing any insolvency resolution or liquidation process.

A corporate financial guarantee extended in favour of ADIPL, previously ₹5,000.00 lakhs (₹50 crore), was fully extinguished during FY 2025-26. In the parent's contingent liabilities and commitments note, the outstanding corporate financial guarantee in favour of ADIPL stood at ₹nil as at 31 March 2026 (₹5,000.00 lakhs as at 31 March 2025). The parent also recognises a corporate guarantee commission earned from ADIPL, recorded at ₹16.67 lakhs for FY 2025-26 (see [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part8]]).

Interested directors and key managerial personnel include [[entities/karunakar_reddy_baddam]] (14% shareholding) and [[entities/krishna_sai_kumar_addepalli]] (10% shareholding), with [[entities/sudarshan_chiluveru]], CFO, holding 3%.
