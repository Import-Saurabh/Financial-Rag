# Apollo Defence Industries Private Limited

Apollo Defence Industries Private Limited is a subsidiary of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]], operating within the defense sector and featured in the company's statutory disclosures and financial statements such as Form AOC-1.

## Overview and Financial Highlights
As detailed in the annual report summaries (see [[summaries/2025_Financial_Year_2025_from_bse_part2]]), the company's salient financial features—including share capital, reserves, assets, liabilities, turnover, and profit/loss—are reported under Form AOC-1.

## Related Party Transactions and Guarantees
The company engages in arm's length transactions with [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]], including loans, advances, job work charges, and rent transactions. Additionally, corporate guarantees such as a ₹50 crore corporate guarantee have been extended in support of its business operations and debenture issuances.
