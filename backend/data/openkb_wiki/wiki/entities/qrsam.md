# QRSAM (Quick Reaction Surface-to-Air Missile)

The Quick Reaction Surface-to-Air Missile (QRSAM) is an Indian short-range air defense missile system developed by [[entities/drdo]] and production-partnered with [[entities/bharat_dynamics_limited]]. It is designed to engage low-flying aircraft, helicopters, and drones in rapid succession, offering a mobile, quick-reaction air defense capability for the Indian Armed Forces.

## Apollo's Role in QRSAM

In the Q1 FY26 earnings call transcript ([[summaries/2025_Aug_2025_Transcript]]), management confirmed that [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] supplies four critical subsystems for the QRSAM program:

- Onboard computer
- Navigation system
- Power distribution units
- Actuation system

These subsystems place Apollo in a key position within the [[concepts/defense-program-supply-chains]] for QRSAM. Management indicated that orders for these subsystems are expected before December, after [[entities/bharat_dynamics_limited]] receives its own order from the government.

## Strategic Context

The QRSAM program forms part of India's broader [[concepts/defense_indigenization]] push under the Make in India initiative. Apollo's involvement in QRSAM — alongside other missile programs such as [[entities/migm]], [[entities/project_kusha]], and [[entities/brahmos]] — underscores the company's claim of contributing roughly 63% of the electronics and electromechanical work for DRDO missile programs. The expected order flow from QRSAM is a significant component of Apollo's near-term order book growth.
