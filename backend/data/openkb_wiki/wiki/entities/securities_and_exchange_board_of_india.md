# Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)

SEBI is the statutory regulator for India's securities markets, established to protect investor interests and regulate the issue, listing and trading of securities. In the disclosures of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]], SEBI is the primary regulator governing the Company's listed-entity obligations.

## Role in This Document

[[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part5]] records SEBI's regulatory footprint across the Corporate Governance Report and the Business Responsibility & Sustainability Reporting (BRSR) annexure:

- **SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015** — the Company's corporate governance, credit-rating disclosure, related party transaction and share reconciliation obligations all flow from these regulations. The Company intimated its credit rating from [[entities/acuite_rating_and_research_limited]] to the stock exchanges pursuant to Regulation 30.
- **Regulations 17 to 27 and 46(2)(b) to (i)** — the mandatory corporate governance framework the Company states it has fully complied with, as certified by [[entities/mnm_and_associates]].
- **Regulation 34(2)(f)** — the basis for the BRSR disclosures in Annexure-K, structured around the National Guidelines on Responsible Business Conduct (NGRBC) principles.
- **SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015** — under which the Company adopted and revised its Code of Internal Procedures and Conduct; see [[concepts/insider-trading]]. The Company Secretary, [[entities/rukhya_parveen]], serves as Compliance Officer.
- **Regulation 17(8)** — requiring the CFO certification furnished by [[entities/sudarshan_chiluveru]].
- **Regulation 40(9) and Depositories Regulations** — annual share transfer certificates and quarterly reconciliation of NSDL/CDSL holdings, filed with [[entities/bse_limited]] and [[entities/national_stock_exchange_of_india]].
- **Regulation 34(3) and Part F of Schedule V** — the framework for disclosure of equity shares held in the suspense account.
- **Regulation 32(7A)** — disclosure of utilisation of funds raised through preferential allotment.

## Non-Compliance and Penalties

SEBI-related non-compliance in the three years under review is limited to one instance: the Company by oversight submitted Limited Review Reports instead of Statutory Auditor's Reports for FY ended 31 March 2025, drawing a ₹88,500 penalty from the National Stock Exchange. There were no penalties or strictures imposed by SEBI itself, and no other capital-markets compliance failures were reported.
