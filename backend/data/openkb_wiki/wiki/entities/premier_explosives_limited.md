# Premier Explosives Limited

**Premier Explosives Limited** is an Indian company operating in the explosives and energetic materials domain, and the target of a major acquisition by [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] announced in 2026. The company is referred to in the deal disclosures as the "Target Company" of the share purchase agreement.

## Acquisition by Apollo Micro Systems

A Share Purchase Agreement (SPA) was executed on **9 July 2026** among:

- [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] (the acquirer)
- Premier Explosives Limited (the target)
- Mrs. Shonika Prasad and Mrs. Kailash Gupta, in their capacity as authorised trustees of the **AKS Family Trust** (the promoter)

Under the SPA, Apollo Micro Systems agreed to acquire **2,22,21,735 equity shares** of face value ₹2 each held by the promoter, representing **41.33% of the paid-up equity share capital**, for an aggregate cash consideration of approximately **₹1,550 crore**.

## Open Offer and Regulatory Process

The acquisition triggered Regulations 3(1) and 4 of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 (SEBI SAST Regulations). The consequent sequence of filings was:

1. Public Announcement (PA) on **10 July 2026**
2. Detailed Public Statement (DPS) on **16 July 2026**
3. Draft Letter of Offer (DLOF) filed with SEBI on **23 July 2026**

An open offer was made to the public shareholders of Premier Explosives to acquire up to **1,39,77,911 equity shares**, representing **26% of the equity share capital**, at an offer price of **₹698 per share**, aggregating to **₹975,65,81,878** payable in cash.

Consummation of the transaction is subject to receipt of applicable governmental and statutory/regulatory approvals, including approval from the **Competition Commission of India**, along with fulfilment of other conditions precedent under the SPA.

## Strategic Rationale

The transaction combines complementary capabilities in defence systems and energetic materials, enabling:

- Operational efficiencies
- Enhanced research and development
- Broader participation in defence and space-related programmes

Apollo Micro Systems framed the acquisition as strengthening India's defence manufacturing capability and deepening sovereign expertise in energetic materials and space programmes. It sits within the broader themes of [[concepts/defence-acquisitions-and-integration]], [[concepts/defence-energetics]], and [[concepts/defense_indigenization]], and complements the group's earlier acquisition of [[entities/idl_explosives]].
