## Overview

Bandhan Bank is an Indian private-sector bank that appears in the standalone financial statements of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] as a secured lender. During FY 2025-26 the bank extended both long-term and working capital facilities to the Company.

## Facilities Extended

### Non-current borrowings (term loan)

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Interest rate | 10.00% p.a. |
| Tenure | 60 instalments |
| Outstanding as at 31 March 2026 | ₹3,720.00 lakhs |
| Outstanding as at 31 March 2025 | Nil |

This term loan is secured by hypothecation of the specific assets created out of the loans financed, and additionally by a **second charge over all the fixed assets** of the Company (a feature shared with the Bajaj Finance Limited facility).

### Current borrowings (working capital)

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Interest rate | 9.75% |
| Nature | Overdraft |
| Outstanding as at 31 March 2026 | ₹2,991.76 lakhs |
| Outstanding as at 31 March 2025 | Nil |

The working capital limits are repayable on demand and secured by hypothecation of inventories, book debts and all other current assets, ranking pari passu with facilities from [[entities/state_bank_of_india]], [[entities/hdfc_bank]] and Non-Banking Financial Companies. They are also secured by an equitable mortgage of specific properties of the Company, its promoters and third parties, and by a **lien over fixed deposits** of the Company — a security shared with [[entities/state_bank_of_india]] and Bajaj Finance Limited.

### Other security

The above referred loans are further secured by the personal guarantees of two Directors and others in their individual capacities.

## Compliance and Utilisation

The Company states that there is **no default** as at 31 March 2026 and 31 March 2025 in repayment of loans or interest payments to Bandhan Bank, and that funds raised from these borrowings were utilised for the purposes for which they were borrowed.

## Context

The Bandhan Bank facilities contributed to the significant increase in Apollo Micro Systems' total borrowings during FY 2025-26, alongside growth in facilities from [[entities/state_bank_of_india]] and [[entities/hdfc_bank]]. The reporting of these balances forms part of the [[concepts/financial-instruments]] disclosures and the asset-charge disclosures relating to [[concepts/property-plant-and-equipment]] in [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part7]].
