# BSE Limited

BSE Limited is an Indian stock exchange and one of the two exchanges on which the equity shares of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] are listed, alongside the [[entities/national_stock_exchange_of_india]].

## Role in the Apollo Micro Systems Disclosure

- **Listing venue:** Apollo Micro Systems Limited's shares are listed on BSE Limited and the National Stock Exchange of India Limited, as disclosed in the BRSR section of the Annual Report.
- **Regulatory filings:** The company intimates the stock exchanges where its shares are listed of material disclosures, including credit rating actions, under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.
- **Share reconciliation:** Under SEBI (Depositories and Participants) Regulations, 2018, certificates reconciling total shares held with NSDL, CDSL and in physical form against the issued/paid-up capital are submitted to BSE and NSE within 30 days of the end of each quarter.
- **Share transfer certification:** Yearly certificates from a Company Secretary-in-Practice confirming due compliance with share transfer formalities are issued under Regulation 40(9) of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.
- **Compliance oversight:** The company states it complied with the requirements of the stock exchanges (NSE and BSE), [[entities/securities_and_exchange_board_of_india]] and other statutory authorities on capital-market matters during the year. The only exchange-imposed penalty in the period was levied by NSE (₹88,500 regarding Regulation 33 filings for FY ended 31 March 2025), not BSE.
