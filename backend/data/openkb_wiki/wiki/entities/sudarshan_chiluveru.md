# Sudarshan Chiluveru

**Sudarshan Chiluveru** (also rendered as Chiluveru Sudarshan) is the Chief Financial Officer (CFO) of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] and a Key Managerial Personnel (KMP) of the company. He is a member of the senior management team and of the Executive Committee of Directors & CFO.

## Role and Responsibilities
- Serves as Chief Financial Officer (CFO) and KMP, as disclosed in the Corporate Governance Report (Annexure H) of the company's 2025-26 annual report and in the related party disclosures of the standalone and consolidated financial statements.
- Member of the Executive Committee of Directors & CFO, which handles routine financial matters under delegated authority from the Board.
- The Executive Committee met four times during FY 2025-26: 28 June 2025, 16 October 2025, 21 November 2025, and 2 February 2026; he attended all four meetings.
- The senior management team listed in the report also includes [[entities/rukhya_parveen]] (Company Secretary & Compliance Officer), Commodore BP Singh VSM (Retd) (President - Strategic Projects), KC Satyanarayana (Head of Special Products Group), Venkateswara Rao Aluri (President Marketing), and Supriya Kondap (Associate Vice President - Projects). There was no change in senior management since the close of the previous financial year.
- As the officer responsible for the company's financial reporting, he is accountable for the accounting policies and estimates underlying both the standalone and the [[concepts/consolidated-financial-statements]], including areas flagged by the auditors as [[concepts/key-audit-matters]] such as [[concepts/revenue-recognition]], [[concepts/capital-work-in-progress]], [[concepts/inventory-obsolescence]], and [[concepts/goodwill]].
- In the consolidated related party disclosures (Note 35), he is identified as "Sri. Chiluveru Sudarshan — Chief Financial Officer (Key Management Personnel)".

## CFO Certification (FY 2025-26)
Under Regulation 17(8) of the SEBI Listing Regulations, he furnished the **CFO Certification** (Annexure-J of the annual report) to the Board for the year ended 31 March 2026, signed at Hyderabad on 8 September 2026. In it he certified, to the best of his knowledge and belief, that:
- the financial statements and cash flow statement for the year ended 31 March 2026 do not contain any materially untrue statement, omit any material fact, or contain statements that might be misleading, and that they present a true and fair view of the company's affairs in compliance with existing accounting standards, applicable laws and regulations;
- no transactions entered into by the company during the year were fraudulent, illegal, or in violation of the company's code of conduct;
- he is responsible for establishing and maintaining internal controls over financial reporting, has evaluated their effectiveness, and has disclosed any deficiencies in design or operation to the auditors and the Audit Committee along with remedial steps;
- he has indicated to the auditors and the Audit Committee any significant changes in internal control over financial reporting, significant changes in accounting policies (disclosed in the notes to the financial statements), and any instances of significant fraud of which he became aware, including the involvement of management or employees with a significant role in internal controls.

This certification forms part of the Corporate Governance and statutory disclosure set recorded in [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part5]], and the underlying [[concepts/internal-financial-controls]] framework is independently reported on by the statutory auditors in Annexure A to their report.
