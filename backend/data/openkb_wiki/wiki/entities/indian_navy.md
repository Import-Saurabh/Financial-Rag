# Indian Navy

The **Indian Navy** is the naval branch of the Indian Armed Forces and one of the principal customers and collaborative partners of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]]. In the FY 2025-26 Annual Report it appears as both an end-user of indigenously developed naval systems and a formal partner in technology co-development.

## Role in the Document

The Indian Navy is referenced across the corporate overview and Management Discussion and Analysis of [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part1]] in connection with [[concepts/underwater-warfare]] and the Company's naval product portfolio.

## Key Facts

### Naval Platforms and Weapons

- Apollo is the approved production agency for the Multi-Influence Ground Mine (MIGM), named **Vighna**, developed under the Development cum Production Partner (DcPP) route with [[entities/drdo]]. See [[entities/migm]].
- Combat trials of naval mines for the Indian Navy have been completed successfully, positioning India among a small group of nations — previously only the United States, Russia, China and Italy — with this undersea-warfare capability.
- The Company supplies electronics and mission-critical subsystems for sensitive naval programmes including nuclear submarines, torpedoes, underwater mines and decoys.
- At the time of the report, the Indian Navy held a large number of torpedoes imported from Western and Eastern origin countries, presenting a significant indigenisation opportunity for homing systems — an area where Apollo already has a strong presence.
- Apollo is developing anti-submarine warfare (ASW) rockets and torpedo homing systems aimed at countering increasingly sophisticated underwater threats.

### R&D and Indigenous Development

- In FY26 Apollo's R&D developed an indigenously designed **Safety and Detonation Device (SDD)** for the Indian Navy, built against specific problem statements issued under the Atmanirbhar Bharat initiative and formally handed over to the Navy.
- Underwater electronics work included torpedo homing systems and autonomous maritime platforms, requiring qualification through naval armament inspection and sea-trial regimes.

### Strategic Partnerships

- A **tripartite alliance** was formed between the Indian Navy, [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] and [[entities/iit_madras]], signed at Swavalamban 2025.
- Apollo holds an MoU partnership with [[entities/bharat_dynamics_limited]] for the joint development of naval platforms.
- The Company's lifetime DPIIT arms manufacturing licence covers Torpedoes and Underwater Mines, supporting its naval manufacturing ambitions.

## Indigenisation Context

The Navy's programmes sit within India's broader [[concepts/defense_indigenization]] and [[concepts/defence-manufacturing-licensing]] framework, under which the Ministry of Defence reserves roughly 75% of the capital acquisition budget for domestic industry and maintains Positive Indigenisation Lists placing munitions and sub-assemblies under import embargo.
