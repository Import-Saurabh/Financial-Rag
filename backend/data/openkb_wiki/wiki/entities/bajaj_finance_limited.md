## Overview

Bajaj Finance Limited is a non-banking financial company (NBFC) that provides secured lending facilities to [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]], as disclosed in the notes to the consolidated financial statements for FY 2025-26.

## Facilities Extended

### Non-current term loan
- Interest rate: 9.85% to 9.55% per annum
- Tenure: 36 instalments
- Outstanding as at 31 March 2026: ₹1,166.67 lakhs
- Outstanding as at 31 March 2025: ₹294.60 lakhs
- Classified within secured borrowings from others (non-current borrowings)

### Working capital overdraft
- Interest rate: 9.75% per annum
- Nature: Overdraft
- Part of the Company's repayable-on-demand working capital facilities

## Security and Ranking

- The term loan is further secured by a second charge over all the fixed assets of the Company (alongside Bandhan Bank).
- The working capital limits are secured by a lien over fixed deposits of the Company, in addition to the standard hypothecation of inventories, book debts and other current assets, and the personal guarantees of two Directors.
- The Company reported no default in repayment of loans or interest as at 31 March 2026 and 31 March 2025.

## Context

Bajaj Finance Limited is one of several lenders in the Company's borrowing mix, alongside [[entities/state_bank_of_india]], [[entities/hdfc_bank]], [[entities/bandhan_bank]], [[entities/indian_overseas_bank]] and [[entities/revx_capital_fund_ii]]. Its exposure relates to the Group's asset creation and working capital funding, reflected in the movements in [[concepts/property-plant-and-equipment]] and [[concepts/financial-instruments]].
