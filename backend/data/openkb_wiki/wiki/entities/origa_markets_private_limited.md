# Origa Markets Private Limited

**Entity type:** Organization (lessor)

Origa Markets Private Limited is a counterparty identified as a **lessor** in the lease disclosures of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]]. The entity appears in the lease notes to both the standalone and consolidated financial statements for the year ended 31 March 2026, and is the only lessor to be named twice in the lessor listing — indicating it is a significant counterparty in the company's lease portfolio.

## Role in the FY2025-26 disclosures

Under Ind AS 116, the company discloses the terms of its lease arrangements with Origa Markets Private Limited. Two lease contracts are attributed to this lessor:

| Lessor | Interest rate (p.a.) | Tenure |
|---|---|---|
| Origa Markets Private Limited | 10.00% | 36 instalments |
| Origa Markets Private Limited | 5.25% | 60 instalments |

The 10.00% / 36-instalment contract is disclosed consistently across the standalone and consolidated notes. The second contract is shown at 5.91% in the standalone lease note ([[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part8]]) and at 5.25% in the consolidated lease note ([[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part11]]); the difference reflects the different reporting entity scope of the two sets of statements.

These arrangements form part of the company's lessee obligations, which are reflected in the reported [[concepts/leases]] balances.

### Standalone basis (Note 40)

- **Closing lease liability:** ₹360.58 lakhs as at 31 March 2026 (FY2024-25: ₹458.44 lakhs)
- **Split:** non-current ₹192.07 lakhs and current ₹168.51 lakhs
- **Movement during the year:** opening ₹458.44 lakhs, new right-of-use liabilities recognised ₹44.29 lakhs, interest on lease liabilities ₹42.26 lakhs, payments of principal ₹(184.41) lakhs
- **Right-of-use asset carrying amount:** ₹349.12 lakhs as at 31 March 2026 (FY2024-25: ₹461.72 lakhs), with depreciation of ₹156.89 lakhs for the year

### Consolidated basis (Note 42)

The consolidated lease note reports materially larger balances, reflecting leases held across the group (including step-down subsidiaries):

- **Right-of-use assets:** opening ₹461.72 lakhs, additions ₹893.61 lakhs, amortisation ₹241.37 lakhs, closing ₹1,113.96 lakhs
- **Lease liabilities:** opening ₹458.44 lakhs, recognised ₹779.41 lakhs, finance cost ₹97.11 lakhs, payments ₹(250.28) lakhs, closing ₹1,084.68 lakhs (current ₹387.70 lakhs; non-current ₹696.98 lakhs)
- **P&L impact:** depreciation of right-of-use assets ₹241.37 lakhs; interest on lease liabilities ₹97.11 lakhs; short-term lease expense ₹41.39 lakhs
- **Maturity profile:** before 3 months ₹92.67 lakhs; 3–6 months ₹92.56 lakhs; 6–12 months ₹173.18 lakhs; 1–3 years ₹626.02 lakhs; 3–5 years ₹100.25 lakhs

The company notes that leases are generally renewed only on mutual consent, at prevailing market prices, and sub-leasing is restricted. The lease liability is measured at the present value of lease payments discounted at the company's incremental borrowing rate.

## Context

Origa Markets Private Limited is mentioned only in this narrow capacity within the annual report — as one of the lessors from whom [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] leases assets (plant and equipment, buildings, and computer equipment) for use in its operations. The other named lessor in the consolidated disclosure is Cholamandalam Finance (10.00%, 20 quarterly instalments). No ownership, governance, or other commercial relationship between Origa Markets Private Limited and the company is disclosed.
