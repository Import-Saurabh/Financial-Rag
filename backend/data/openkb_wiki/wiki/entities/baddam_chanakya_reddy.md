# Baddam Chanakya Reddy

Baddam Chanakya Reddy is a member of the promoter group of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] (Apollo Micro Systems Limited).

## Shareholding

According to the promoter shareholding details in the consolidated financial statements for the year ended 31 March 2026 ([[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part7]], [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part10]]):

- **Shares at beginning of the year (31 March 2025):** Nil
- **Change during the year:** 84,00,600 equity shares acquired
- **Shares at end of the year (31 March 2026):** 84,00,600 equity shares
- **Percentage of total shares:** 2.35%
- **Percentage change during the year:** Not applicable (new holding, no prior-year position)

The entire holding was acquired during FY 2025-26, and the position did not appear in the promoter shareholding table as at 31 March 2025. The acquisition parallels that of [[entities/baddam_kanishka_reddy]], who likewise acquired 84,00,600 shares (2.35%) during the year.

## Context

The stake was acquired in a year in which the Company expanded its share capital substantially — issuing 2,70,42,894 equity shares of ₹1 each at a premium of ₹113 on a preferential basis, and converting 2,37,59,986 warrants into an equal number of equity shares. Total issued, subscribed and fully paid-up capital rose to 35,72,92,440 equity shares of ₹1 each (₹3,572.92 lakhs).
