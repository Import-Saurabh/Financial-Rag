# Apollo Employees Foundation

**Apollo Employees Foundation** ("the Trust") is the employee welfare trust established by [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] for the purpose of administering the **Apollo Employees Stock Options Scheme – 2018 (ESOS-2018)**.

## Role and Purpose

The Trust functions as the vehicle through which ESOS-2018 is implemented. Under the scheme, options granted to employees are serviced via shares acquired by the Trust, typically through secondary market acquisition. The Trust is governed by trustees and reports its share transactions and loan status in the Company's annual report as part of SEBI (Share Based Employee Benefits) disclosure requirements.

## Context within ESOS-2018

ESOS-2018 was approved by shareholders on 26th December, 2018 through a postal ballot process, covering up to 10,00,000 (Ten Lakh) options. However, during the financial year ended 31st March, 2026:

- No options were granted, vested, exercised, forfeited, or outstanding.
- No shares arose from exercise of options.
- No money was realised by exercise of options.
- The Trust repaid no loan through exercise price receipts or share sales.

This dormancy is reflected in the Trust's zero holding and minimal ₹20,000 outstanding loan balance.
