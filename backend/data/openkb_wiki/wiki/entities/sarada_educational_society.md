# Sarada Educational Society

Sarada Educational Society is an organization engaged in educational and social welfare activities. It is highlighted in the annual report of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] for its collaboration on Corporate Social Responsibility ([[concepts/corporate-social-responsibility]]) initiatives.

## Key Details

* **CSR Partnership:** The society received funding allocations amounting to ₹30 Lakhs from [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] during the financial year 2024-25.
* **Focus Area:** The funds provided to the society are dedicated to supporting and educating dropout students, contributing to the company's community development and inclusive growth objectives under [[concepts/corporate-social-responsibility]].
