# Moored Mine

## Overview

The Moored Mine is an indigenously developed sea mine manufactured by [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]]. It is a compact underwater ordnance system designed for area denial against both surface and sub-surface targets. The weapon is anchored to the seabed and suspended at a pre-determined depth, forming part of India's growing indigenous naval warfare capability.

## Technical Features

- **Multi-influence signal processing system**: The core of the mine is AMS's advanced signal processing technology, capable of detecting acoustic, magnetic, and pressure signatures.
- **Target discrimination**: The system can discriminate genuine targets from countermeasures.
- **Programmable target logic**: Allows configuration of engagement parameters.
- **Secure arming**: Incorporates secure arming protocols for safe deployment.
- **Extended underwater endurance**: Designed for prolonged deployment in strategic waters.

## Operational Role

The Moored Mine is designed for defensive mining of harbours, chokepoints, and strategic waters. It constitutes a potent sea-denial capability, addressing the specialised requirements of naval mine warfare. The system demonstrates the company's mastery of underwater sensing, target classification, and fuzing technologies.

## Strategic Significance

With this solution, [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] is positioned as a unique player offering all types of underwater mines in naval warfare. The programme forms part of the company's broader portfolio in [[concepts/underwater-warfare]], alongside the [[entities/mini_torpedo]], [[entities/limpet_mines]], and the [[entities/asw_weapon_platform]]. Backward integration within the Apollo Group provides access to qualified energetic materials, enabling end-to-end realisation of the weapon.
