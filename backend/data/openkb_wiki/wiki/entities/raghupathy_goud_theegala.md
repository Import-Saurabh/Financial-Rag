# Raghupathy Goud Theegala

Raghupathy Goud Theegala is the **Chairman** of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] and has served on its Board as a **Non-Executive Independent Director since 21 August 2017**, with his current term of appointment running from 22 August 2022. In the FY 2025-26 Annual Report he authored the Chairman's Message, titled *"India's Defence Mission. Apollo's Global Chapter."*

## Background and Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in **Commerce** from Osmania University.
- Bachelor's degree in **Law** from Bangalore University.
- Practised law for three years before joining the **A.P. Police** as a Sub Inspector through the Public Service Commission.
- Prior to joining Apollo's Board, he served with the **Andhra Pradesh Lokayukta and Upa-Lokayukta**, where he gained extensive experience in addressing financial irregularities, abuse of power and corruption.

His legal and anti-corruption investigative background gives the Board oversight experience relevant to [[concepts/corporate-governance-and-compliance]].

## Board and Committee Roles — FY 2025-26

Theegala holds a leadership position across most of Apollo's statutory and non-mandatory Board committees, chairing three of them:

| Committee | Role | Meetings held / attended (FY 2025-26) |
|---|---|---|
| Stakeholders Relationship Committee | **Chairman** | 2 meetings (28 Jul 2025, 9 Feb 2026) — attended both |
| Securities Allotment Committee | **Chairman** | 6 meetings — attended all 6 |
| Audit Committee | Member | 5 meetings — attended all 5 |
| Nomination & Remuneration Committee | Member | 1 meeting (28 Jul 2025) — attended |
| Corporate Social Responsibility Committee | Member | 2 meetings (23 May 2025, 23 Mar 2026) — attended both |

The Board of Directors met six times during the year (23 May 2025, 28 May 2025, 28 Jul 2025, 4 Nov 2025, 19 Nov 2025 and 9 Feb 2026), with the maximum interval between meetings not exceeding 120 days. The 28th Annual General Meeting was held on 16 September 2025. A separate meeting of the Independent Directors was held on 26 March 2026, at which the performance of the Board and its Chairperson and the quality of information flow between management and the Board were reviewed. He was also named as Compliance Officer for the Stakeholders Relationship Committee's reporting alongside Company Secretary [[entities/rukhya_parveen]].

He serves as an **Independent Director of IDL Explosives Limited** ([[entities/idl_explosives]]), a step-down subsidiary of Apollo, holding one directorship and one chairmanship/membership of an Audit/Stakeholders Relationship Committee in another public limited company.

## Remuneration

As a Non-Executive Independent Director, Theegala has no pecuniary relationship or transactions with the Company beyond sitting fees and reimbursement of conveyance, travel and other expenses for attending Board and Committee meetings. Sitting fees are paid at **₹20,000 per day**. For FY 2025-26 his total sitting fees amounted to **₹2.20 Lakhs** (FY 2024-25: ₹2.40 Lakhs), with no stock options, sweat equity, commission or profit-related payments. The total compensation paid to key management personnel during FY 2025-26 was ₹263.55 Lakhs, of which his sitting fees form a small non-executive component, alongside the remuneration of Managing Director [[entities/karunakar_reddy_baddam]], Whole-Time Directors [[entities/krishna_sai_kumar_addepalli]] and [[entities/venkata_siva_prasad_chandrapati]], Chief Financial Officer [[entities/sudarshan_chiluveru]] and Company Secretary [[entities/rukhya_parveen]].

The related-party disclosures in the FY 2025-26 Annual Report record him within the list of related parties and show his sitting fees as his only transaction during the year — ₹2.20 Lakhs in FY 2025-26 against ₹2.40 Lakhs in FY 2024-25. The outstanding sitting fees payable to him as at 31 March 2026 were **₹0.18 Lakhs**, down from **₹2.16 Lakhs** as at 31 March 2025. Transactions with related parties are made on terms equivalent to those prevailing in arm's length transactions, and outstanding balances are unsecured and interest-free. These disclosures connect to [[concepts/related-party-transactions]].

## Chairman's Message — FY 2025-26

In the Annual Report, Theegala framed FY 2025-26 as a landmark year in which Apollo moved "decisively from subsystems to platforms." Key themes of his message:

- **Policy tailwinds**: India's defence sector is at an inflection point driven by the Government's *Viksit Bharat @ 2047* vision, anchored in Aatmanirbhar Bharat and accelerated indigenisation. He cited the Union Budget FY 2026-27 allocation of ₹7.8 lakh crore to the Ministry of Defence, including ₹2.19 lakh crore of capital outlay, with around **75% of the capital acquisition budget reserved for domestic industry**. This connects to defence indigenization and [[concepts/defense-program-supply-chains]].
- **41 years of credibility**: Apollo's electronics and mission-critical subsystems serve sensitive programmes including nuclear submarines, torpedoes, underwater mines, decoys, UAVs, aerial bombs, artillery systems and missiles — capabilities linked to [[concepts/underwater-warfare]].
- **Pivotal acquisition**: He described the acquisition of [[entities/idl_explosives]] as central to Apollo's transformation, extending participation across the defence value chain from weapon-system intelligence to the energetics that give it effect, and argued this completeness distinguishes an OEM from a supplier. See [[concepts/defence-energetics]] and [[concepts/tier_1_defense_oem_integration]].
- **Global OEM ambition**: He stated that Apollo aims to stand among emerging global defence OEMs — designing, developing, integrating and delivering complete weapon systems, first for India's Armed Forces and progressively for trusted partner nations — with global OEM status described as earned through certified quality, delivery reliability, indigenous IP and customer confidence. See [[concepts/defense-exports-and-global-market-entry]].
- **Future warfare**: He noted that autonomous systems, network-centric operations, precision technologies and intelligent unmanned platforms will shape future battlefields, and that nations will seek partners delivering these with sovereignty, speed and trust.
- **Gratitude**: He credited Apollo's people, its customers in the Armed Forces, [[entities/drdo]] and government institutions, its technology partners, and shareholders.

## Financial Context — FY 2025-26

The standalone financial statements accompanying the Annual Report show Apollo delivering revenue from operations of ₹76,485.32 Lakhs and profit attributable to equity shareholders of ₹12,057.89 Lakhs, with basic earnings per share of ₹3.57. The company's capital structure strengthened during the year, with the debt-equity ratio improving to 0.73 from 1.07, consistent with the Chairman's framing of a year of transformation. See [[concepts/financial-ratios]], [[concepts/earnings-per-share]] and [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part8]].

## Consolidated Financial Context — FY 2025-26

The consolidated financial statements for FY 2025-26 corroborate the scale of the transformation described in his message. Consolidated revenue from operations rose to **₹90,432.38 Lakhs** from ₹56,206.92 Lakhs, with the Group operating as a **single reportable segment** — the manufacture and sale of electromechanical components and systems and allied services. Consolidated profit for the year was **₹11,292.04 Lakhs**, and total other equity closed at **₹1,27,736.57 Lakhs** after a securities premium inflow of ₹57,407.25 Lakhs from the preferential issue and warrant conversions. Goodwill on the consolidated balance sheet rose from ₹1.89 Lakhs to **₹14,519.55 Lakhs**, principally from acquisitions, consistent with the acquisition-led platform strategy he highlighted. The Group's structure comprises [[entities/ananya_sip_rf_technologies_private_limited]] (51%), [[entities/apollo_defence_industries_private_limited]] (76%), [[entities/apollo_strategic_technologies_private_limited]] (51%) and [[entities/idl_explosives]] (100%). See [[concepts/consolidated-financial-statements]], [[concepts/goodwill]], [[concepts/segment-reporting]], [[concepts/revenue-recognition]] and [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part10]].
