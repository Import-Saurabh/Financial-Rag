# Apollo Strategic Technologies Private Limited

**Type:** Organization

Apollo Strategic Technologies Private Limited (ASTPL) (CIN: U26109TS2024PTC191803) is a step-down subsidiary of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] under [[entities/apollo_defence_industries_private_limited]]. It became a step-down subsidiary on December 10, 2024. The group holds 51% of ASTPL per the AOC-1 step-down subsidiary disclosure for FY 2025-26, and the company is classified as an aerospace and defence entity incorporated in India. As of March 31, 2026, ASTPL is yet to commence business operations. The company is also referred to in the standalone financial statements as "Apollo Strategic Technology Pvt Ltd" and "Apollo Strategic Technologies Private Limited".

## Key facts

- **Relationship:** Step-down subsidiary of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] via [[entities/apollo_defence_industries_private_limited]].
- **CIN:** U26109TS2024PTC191803.
- **Date of becoming step-down subsidiary:** December 10, 2024.
- **Country of incorporation:** India.
- **Nature of business:** Aerospace and defence; yet to commence business operations as of March 31, 2026.
- **Reporting currency:** INR.
- **Accounting period reviewed:** April 1, 2025 to March 31, 2026.
- **Shareholding by the group:** 51% (disclosed as 51.00% in the FY 2025-26 Business Responsibility & Sustainability Report, in the standalone financial statements' investment-in-subsidiaries disclosure, and as 51% direct/indirect ownership interest in the consolidated financial statements' description of the Group).
- **Contribution to overall performance of the company:** 51%.
- **Direct/indirect shareholding of ASTPL in the listed entity/subsidiary:** Nil.
- **Related party classification:** Included in the omnibus approval for material related party transactions sought at the 29th Annual General Meeting. Listed in the standalone financial statements (Note 34) as a step-down subsidiary related party, and also listed as a step-down subsidiary related party in the consolidated financial statements' Note 35 related party disclosures for FY 2025-26.

## Consolidation and group structure treatment (FY 2025-26 consolidated financial statements)

- **Description of the Group (consolidated Note 37):** ASTPL is disclosed as a step-down subsidiary of the group, incorporated in India, with a Percentage of Direct/Indirect Ownership Interest of **51%** — alongside [[entities/ananya_sip_rf_technologies_private_limited]] (subsidiary, 51%), [[entities/apollo_defence_industries_private_limited]] (subsidiary, 76%) and [[entities/idl_explosives]] (step-down subsidiary, 100%). This confirms ASTPL's continuing inclusion in the consolidated group perimeter for FY 2025-26.
- **Additional information disclosure (consolidated Note 51):** ASTPL is expressly named in the consolidated financial statements' Note 51 disclosure ("Disclosure of additional information in Consolidated Financial Statements as required in para II in general instructions as part III of division II to Schedule III to the Companies Act, 2013") as a step-down subsidiary within the line item "Apollo Defence Industries Private Limited-Consolidated (including step-down subsidiary Apollo Strategic Technologies Private Limited and IDL Explosives Limited)". That combined line reported net assets (total assets minus total liabilities) of ₹(1,808.97) Lakhs, i.e. -1.38% of consolidated net assets; a share in profit and loss of ₹(1,812.52) Lakhs (-16.88% of consolidated profit or loss); a share in other comprehensive income of ₹13.53 Lakhs (56.13% of consolidated other comprehensive income); and a share in total comprehensive income of ₹(1,798.98) Lakhs (-16.72% of consolidated total comprehensive income).
- **Prior-year comparative (Note 51, FY 2024-25):** The FY 2024-25 comparative table in the same note lists only Apollo Defence Industries Private Limited separately, with net assets of ₹(9.99) Lakhs (-0.02%) and a share in profit and loss of ₹(10.40) Lakhs (-0.18%); ASTPL is not separately named in the FY 2024-25 table, reflecting that the combined ADIPL consolidated presentation (incorporating ASTPL) is a feature of the FY 2025-26 reporting period.
- **Joint ventures and associates:** Note 51 confirms that the company has no joint ventures or associates as of the year ended 31 March 2026 (consistent with the prior-year note).
- **Related party disclosures (consolidated Note 35):** ASTPL is listed among the group's related parties as a step-down subsidiary, and is encompassed by the statement that transactions with related parties are made on terms equivalent to those that prevail in arm's length transactions, with outstanding balances at year-end unsecured and interest free (except where interest-bearing loans apply).
- **Business combination and goodwill context (consolidated Note 47):** The consolidated financial statements record goodwill arising on the acquisition of IDL Explosives Limited by Apollo Defence Industries Private Limited of ₹14,517.67 Lakhs during FY 2025-26 (against ₹1.89 Lakhs previously); the acquisition was completed on 15 November 2025 and the purchase price allocation remains provisional, with the measurement period scheduled to conclude no later than 15 November 2026. The original ₹1.89 Lakhs of goodwill relates to the group's earlier 51% acquisition of Ananya SIP RF Technologies Private Limited in FY 2020-21. See [[concepts/goodwill]] and [[concepts/ind-as-103-business-combinations]].
- **Segment context:** The group reports as a single reportable segment — manufacturing and sale of electromechanical components and systems and allied components and services — within which ASTPL's (currently dormant) aerospace and defence activities sit. See [[concepts/segment-reporting]] and [[concepts/consolidated-financial-statements]].
- **Subsequent events and approval of consolidated financial statements (Notes 54 and 55):** The consolidated financial statements record no material events after the reporting date that require adjustment or disclosure, and were approved for issue by the Board of Directors at its meeting held on 18 May 2026. As a subsidiary included within those statements, ASTPL's balances and disclosures are covered by that approval.
- **Audit trail (Note 53):** The consolidated financial statements disclose that the accounting software used for maintaining the books of account has an audit trail (edit log) facility that operated throughout the year for all relevant transactions, with no instance of the feature being tampered with. See [[concepts/audit-trail-rule-11g]].

## Loans from the listed entity (standalone financial statements)

- **Classification:** Unsecured loan to a step-down subsidiary, disclosed under Non-current assets → Loans within the standalone financial statements. Under Note 42 (Section 186(4) of the Companies Act, 2013), it is disclosed as a loan without repayment period advanced to a step-down subsidiary.
- **Outstanding as at March 31, 2026:** ₹12.36 Lakhs (the amount reported under Section 186(4) as a loan without repayment period; the related party outstanding balance disclosure reports the loan at ₹12.10 Lakhs and accrued interest at ₹0.26 Lakhs).
- **Interest rate:** 9.90% per annum, consistent with the rate proposed for the group's intercorporate funding arrangements.
- **Interest payment terms:** Payable on a quarterly basis.
- **Repayment terms:** No stipulated repayment period; the loan is unsecured.
- **Group context:** ASTPL's loan forms part of the aggregate ₹31,555.75 Lakhs of unsecured, considered-good loans to subsidiaries and step-down subsidiaries as at March 31, 2026 (March 31, 2025: ₹1,786.61 Lakhs), alongside loans to [[entities/ananya_sip_rf_technologies_private_limited]], [[entities/apollo_defence_industries_private_limited]] and [[entities/idl_explosives]].
- **Measurement:** Such loans to group entities are measured under the Company's financial instruments accounting policies, with impairment assessed under the expected credit loss framework. See [[concepts/financial-instruments]].

## Group and governance treatment

- **BRSR disclosure (FY 2025-26):** The Business Responsibility & Sustainability Report lists ASTPL as a step-down subsidiary in which the listed entity holds 51.00% of shares. ASTPL does **not** participate in the business responsibility initiatives of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]].
- **Subsidiary oversight:** The Audit Committee of the listed entity reviews the consolidated financial statements and the investments made by its unlisted subsidiary companies, and significant developments of the unlisted subsidiaries are periodically placed before the Board. Apollo Micro Systems Limited states it has no material subsidiary, so ASTPL is not classified as one.
- **Investment-in-subsidiaries disclosure (Note 48):** ASTPL is listed as a partly owned step-down subsidiary of the group, with the group holding 51% and a country of incorporation of India, consistent with the consolidated Note 37 description of the Group and the consolidated Note 51 additional information disclosure.
- **Compliance context:** The group holds all 51% via [[entities/apollo_defence_industries_private_limited]], and related party dealings are governed by the Related Party Transaction Policy reviewed periodically by the Audit Committee, alongside the group's broader [[concepts/corporate-governance-and-compliance]] framework.
- **Borrowing security context:** The group's secured borrowings from banks and non-banking financial companies are secured by hypothecation of specific assets, equitable mortgage of properties of the Company, its promoters and third parties, and personal guarantees of two Directors; the unsecured intercorporate lending to entities such as ASTPL sits outside this security structure.
- **Contingent liabilities context:** The consolidated financial statements disclose group contingent liabilities of ₹6,356.28 Lakhs in claims not acknowledged as debts and ₹4,832.63 Lakhs in outstanding guarantees and counter-guarantees, together with a capital commitment of ₹2,130.91 Lakhs (net of advances) for purchase of property, plant and equipment. See [[concepts/provisions-and-contingent-liabilities]] and [[concepts/capital-work-in-progress]].
