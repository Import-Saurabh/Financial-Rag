# Krishna Sai Kumar Addepalli

**Role:** Whole-Time Director (Operations), [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]]

Krishna Sai Kumar Addepalli (DIN: 03601692) serves as the Whole-time Director of Operations at Apollo Micro Systems Limited (AMS). An engineering graduate specialising in Electronics and Communication, he brings over 24 years of diverse experience spanning marketing, finance and operations. He was appointed to his current term on 22 August 2022.

## Key Facts

- **Position:** Whole-Time Director (Operations) and Executive Director on the board of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited|Apollo Micro Systems Limited]]. Identified as Key Management Personnel (KMP) in the related party disclosures.
- **DIN:** 03601692.
- **Current term appointment:** 22 August 2022.
- **Background:** Engineering graduate in Electronics and Communication.
- **Experience:** More than 24 years across marketing, finance and operations.
- **Tenure with AMS:** Joined the company in 2003.
- **Other directorships:** 1 — [[entities/idl_explosives|IDL Explosives Limited]].
- **Other committee memberships:** 1 — IDL Explosives Limited.

## Contributions at AMS

According to the [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part1|Annual Report 2025-26]], Mr. Addepalli has played a pivotal role in:

- Broadening the Company's customer base and spearheading its expansion into new markets.
- Driving quality process reforms.
- Strengthening the company's [[concepts/defense-program-supply-chains|supply chain management]].
- Business development, with a stated dedication to expanding AMS's presence in both defence and non-defence sectors.

## Consolidation and Auditor's Report (FY 2025-26)

Mr. Addepalli's directorship at [[entities/idl_explosives|IDL Explosives Limited]] places him within the Group structure covered by the consolidated financial statements. As disclosed in the Group structure note (Note 37), IDL Explosives Limited became a wholly-owned (100%) step-down subsidiary of AMS during the year through a share acquisition, accounted for as a [[concepts/ind-as-103-business-combinations|business combination under Ind AS 103]], resulting in provisional [[concepts/goodwill|goodwill]] of ₹14,517.67 lakhs recognised on the basis of book values pending completion of the Purchase Price Allocation exercise. The other group entities listed are [[entities/ananya_sip_rf_technologies_private_limited|Ananya SIP RF Technologies Private Limited]] (subsidiary, 51%), [[entities/apollo_defence_industries_private_limited|Apollo Defence Industries Private Limited]] (subsidiary, 76%) and [[entities/apollo_strategic_technologies_private_limited|Apollo Strategic Technologies Private Limited]] (step-down subsidiary, 51%).

The [[concepts/consolidated-financial-statements|Consolidated Financial Statements]] for the year ended 31 March 2026 were audited by [[entities/s_t_mohite_and_co|S.T. Mohite & Co]], Chartered Accountants (ICAI Firm Registration Number: 011410S), which issued an unmodified opinion. The auditor's report identified four [[concepts/key-audit-matters|Key Audit Matters]]: [[concepts/revenue-recognition|revenue recognition]] under Ind AS 115, [[concepts/capital-work-in-progress|capital work in progress]] (CWIP of ₹19,629.40 lakhs), [[concepts/inventory-obsolescence|inventories]] (₹79,528.92 lakhs), and the provisional goodwill arising from the IDL Explosives acquisition. The report also confirmed that internal financial controls over financial reporting were adequate and operating effectively as at 31 March 2026.

## Segment and Business Context

As disclosed in the segment information note (Note 36), the Group operates as a single reportable segment — the manufacturing and sale of electromechanical components and systems and allied components and services — with the Chief Operating Decision Maker monitoring the business as a whole. Revenue for FY 2025-26 was ₹90,432.38 lakhs, disaggregated by product (electronical components/goods) and geography (domestic India; no exports).
