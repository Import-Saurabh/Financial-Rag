# Sridevi Madati

**Sridevi Madati** is a Practising Company Secretary and Partner at [[entities/mnm_and_associates]], a firm of Company Secretaries (Firm Registration No. P2017TL059600) based in Hyderabad. She signs as a partner holding Membership No. F6476 and Certificate of Practice No. COP 11694.

## Role in Relation to Apollo Micro Systems Limited

Sridevi Madati acts as an independent certifying professional for [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]]. In the FY 2025-26 annual report she issued two certificates, both dated 8 September 2026 at Hyderabad and addressed to the Members of the company:

### 1. Certificate on Corporate Governance (Annexure-I)
She examined the company's compliance with the conditions of Corporate Governance for the year ended 31 March 2026 as stipulated in Chapter IV of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015. She certified that the company had complied with those conditions, while noting that:
- Compliance is the responsibility of the management.
- The examination was limited to procedures and implementation thereof, not an audit or an opinion on the financial statements.
- The certification is not an assurance of future viability or of management's efficiency or effectiveness.

The certificate carries UDIN F006476H001388351.

### 2. Certificate on Non-disqualification of Directors
She certified that none of the Directors of Apollo Micro Systems Limited is debarred or disqualified by the [[entities/securities_and_exchange_board_of_india]] or the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (or any such authority) from being appointed or continuing as a Director of the Company. That certificate is published on the company's website in the "Investor" section.

## Significance

Her certifications form part of the statutory annexures underpinning the company's [[concepts/corporate-governance-and-compliance]] disclosures — including the compliance affirmation across SEBI Listing Regulations 17 to 27 and Regulation 46(2)(b) to (i) — and the associated governance certifications such as the CFO certification signed by [[entities/sudarshan_chiluveru]].
