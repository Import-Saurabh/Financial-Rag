# Karunasree Samudrala

**Karunasree Samudrala** is an Independent Director on the Board of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]]. She is described in the FY2025-26 Annual Report as a distinguished Fellow of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI). She joined the Board in May 2018 and her current term appointment is dated 29 May 2023. She is also named among the Company's related parties as a Non-Executive Director in the consolidated financial statements for FY2025-26.

## Qualifications

- Fellow of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI)
- Commerce degree from Kakatiya University, Telangana
- Certifications in:
  - International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS)
  - Certified Internal Auditor (CIA)
  - Concurrent Audit of Banks (ICAI)

## Professional Experience

With a career spanning over **23 years**, she brings expertise in accounting, auditing and taxation. She has led numerous audit engagements and gained hands-on experience across statutory, internal and tax audits. Her audit experience extends across multiple sectors, including FMCG, stock markets and banking.

Her professional journey has involved close collaboration with statutory auditors, income tax authorities and investor relationship management.

## Board Role

Within the Board of Apollo Micro Systems Limited, she serves as an **Independent Director** — part of the independent oversight that also includes [[entities/raghupathy_goud_theegala]] (Chairman and Independent Director) and fellow independent/non-executive directors [[entities/chandrashekar_matham]] and [[entities/kavya_gorla]]. The executive leadership of the Board comprises Managing Director [[entities/karunakar_reddy_baddam]], Whole-Time Director (Technical) [[entities/venkata_siva_prasad_chandrapati]] and Whole-Time Director (Operations) [[entities/krishna_sai_kumar_addepalli]], alongside Non-Executive Director [[entities/aditya_kumar_halwasiya]].

She is one of three Non-Executive Independent Directors on the eight-member Board, and the only woman Independent Director. She holds directorships in three other public limited companies: Roopa Industries Limited, [[entities/idl_explosives]] and Eldorado Agritech Limited, and also serves on three audit/stakeholder committees in those companies.

## Committee Roles

Karunasree Samudrala plays a key role in the Board's committee structure:

- **Audit Committee**: Chairman (Non-Executive Independent Director). The committee met 5 times during FY2025-26; she attended all 5 meetings.
- **Nomination & Remuneration Committee**: Chairman (Non-Executive Independent Director). The committee met once during FY2025-26; she attended.
- **Risk Management Committee**: Member (Non-Executive Independent Director). The committee met twice during FY2025-26; she attended both meetings.
- **Securities Allotment Committee**: Member. The committee met 6 times during FY2025-26; she attended all 6 meetings.

Her financial and audit background aligns with the governance framework described in the report, under which the company's Audit Committee oversees the Whistle Blower/Vigil Mechanism, and statutory and secretarial compliance runs on a structured compliance calendar with maker-checker-approver controls and periodic certification to the Board. See [[concepts/corporate-governance-and-compliance]].

## Remuneration and Related-Party Disclosures

As a Non-Executive Independent Director, she receives sitting fees for attending Board and Committee meetings. For FY2025-26, she was paid ₹2.20 Lakhs as sitting fees, compared with ₹2.40 Lakhs in FY2024-25. She does not receive any salary, stock options, or commission.

The consolidated financial statements for FY2025-26 identify her among the Company's related parties as a Non-Executive Director, alongside the other key management personnel and non-executive directors of the Group. The related-party note in the consolidated statements discloses sitting fees of ₹2.20 Lakhs paid to her during FY2025-26 (₹2.40 Lakhs in FY2024-25) and remuneration payable of ₹0.18 Lakhs outstanding as at 31 March 2026, down from ₹2.16 Lakhs as at 31 March 2025. No dividend, rent or other transactions with her are disclosed for the year. See [[concepts/related-party-transactions]] and [[concepts/consolidated-financial-statements]].
