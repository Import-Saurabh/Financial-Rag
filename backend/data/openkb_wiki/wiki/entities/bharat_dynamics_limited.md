# Bharat Dynamics Limited

Bharat Dynamics Limited (BDL) is an Indian defense public sector undertaking and a major manufacturer of missiles and allied defense systems, operating under the Ministry of Defence. In the [[summaries/2025_Aug_2025_Transcript]], BDL appears as a central partner and customer for Apollo Micro Systems across several flagship missile programs, a relationship reinforced in the [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part1]] through a formal collaboration MoU and personnel links.

## Role in Apollo's Defense Programs

- **MIGM:** BDL is jointly named with Apollo as a development-cum-production partner. Technology transfer documentation is being completed, and the formal order is expected within the financial year. Apollo is preparing full manufacturing capabilities, including warhead work through [[entities/idl_explosives]].
- **QRSAM:** Apollo supplies four critical subsystems for BDL's Quick Reaction Surface-to-Air Missile program, including the onboard computer, navigation system, power distribution units, and actuation system. Orders are expected before December once BDL receives its order.
- **Naval platforms:** The FY 2025-26 Annual Report lists BDL as an MoU partner to Apollo for the joint development of naval platforms, extending the relationship from missile programs into maritime systems.

## Broader Context

BDL is one of the main prime integrators in India's defense missile ecosystem, working closely with [[entities/drdo]] on programs such as MIGM and QRSAM. Apollo's contributions to BDL-led programs illustrate the broader trend of [[concepts/tier_1_defense_oem_integration]] and the deepening of [[concepts/defense-program-supply-chains]] in Indian defense manufacturing.

BDL's role also reflects the government's [[concepts/defense_indigenization]] push, in which public sector firms and private suppliers collaborate to build indigenous missile capabilities.

BDL is also a source of domain talent for the private defense sector. [[entities/chandrashekar_matham]], an Independent Director of Apollo Micro Systems, previously served as a Senior Manager in R&D at Bharat Dynamics, Hyderabad, where he contributed to the indigenous design and development of embedded test systems for missile technology — experience now reflected in Apollo's board-level technical oversight.
