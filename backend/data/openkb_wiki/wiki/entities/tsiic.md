## Overview

Telangana State Industrial Infrastructure Corporation (TSIIC) is a Telangana state government agency responsible for developing and allotting industrial land and infrastructure. It is the nodal body behind the **TSIIC Hardware Park**, an industrial park in Hyderabad catering to electronics, hardware and defence manufacturing tenants.

## Role in Apollo Micro Systems' Expansion

TSIIC appears in the [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part1]] as the land-allotting authority underpinning [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]]'s manufacturing scale-up:

- **Earlier allotment**: Apollo acquired 2.5 acres of land from Telangana State Industrial Corp Ltd as part of its incremental capacity build-out in Hyderabad (recorded on the company's journey timeline).
- **FY 2025-26 allotment**: TSIIC allotted additional land in **Hardware Park Phase-II**, adjacent to Apollo's existing facility, taking the company's footprint there to approximately **10.68 acres**.
- **Purpose**: This land supports Apollo's greenfield expansion (referred to as Unit III), where the company is investing approximately **₹300 crore** to manufacture, integrate and test advanced weapon platforms including Grad Rockets, ASW Rockets, Anti-Tank Mines and Artillery Munitions.

## Title Deed Status

The [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part11]] regulatory disclosures clarify the legal status of the TSIIC land. The allotment is recorded under property, plant and equipment as land with a gross carrying value of **₹2,761.82 lakhs**, held since **11 July 2025**. However, the **title deed/registration is yet to be executed in the name of the Company** as per the terms of allotment, and the property is therefore disclosed among the immovable properties whose title deeds are not held in the name of the Company as at the reporting date. No promoter, director, relative of a promoter/director, or employee holds the title. Further details appear under [[concepts/property-plant-and-equipment]].

## Strategic Significance

The TSIIC allotment is one of the enabling inputs behind Apollo's transition from a defence electronics supplier to an integrated platform and weapons manufacturer. It sits alongside the company's separate MoU with the Government of Telangana for a greenfield Defence and High-Energetic Explosives project with a proposed investment of roughly ₹1,500 crore.

Land and industrial infrastructure provided by TSIIC support the broader domestic capacity build-out described under [[concepts/defense-program-supply-chains]] and the indigenous-manufacturing push central to [[concepts/tier_1_defense_oem_integration]].
