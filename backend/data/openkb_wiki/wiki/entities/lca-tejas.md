# LCA Tejas

## Overview

LCA Tejas is India's indigenous light combat aircraft platform. Within the wiki it appears as a **demand driver for domestic defence manufacturing** — specifically as the anchor programme through which [[entities/mtar-technologies]] is building an actuation and flight-control assemblies business in the domestic defence vertical.

## MTAR's Role on the Platform

From the Q1 FY27 earnings call ([[summaries/2026_Aug_2026_Transcript]]):

- MTAR is **qualified into the actuator programme** for the **Tejas Mark-1A** variant.
- The actuator opportunity is sized at roughly **INR140–150 crore**, and management expects this to grow further ("it's going to go in further than that").
- Volume orders for **actuator assemblies** were described as anticipated, alongside orders for **wing kits** and **electromechanical actuators** for other defence programmes.
- MTAR characterises its Tejas work as one of several **niche, high-criticality** defence areas rather than a single large platform contract — the company deliberately does not detail all of them publicly.

## Programme Economics and Context

- Management placed the overall domestic defence opportunity — Tejas actuators plus wing kits plus electromechanical actuators across programmes — at **more than INR250 crore**.
- The Tejas actuator award is presented as part of a broader shift from first-article development to volume production, a pattern also visible in MTAR's MNC aerospace work. See [[concepts/order-book-and-revenue-visibility]] and [[concepts/defense-program-supply-chains]].
- Domestic defence work sits inside MTAR's aerospace and defense segment, which management expects to **double revenue in FY27** with a further 3–4 year ramp, targeting INR600–700 crore by around FY30.

## Strategic Significance

- The programme is a concrete instance of [[concepts/defense_indigenization]]: an Indian private-sector manufacturer qualifying onto a frontline indigenous combat aircraft.
- It illustrates the [[concepts/tier_1_defense_oem_integration]] pathway — qualification with the platform's design/production agencies, followed by volume orders and content growth per aircraft.
- For MTAR specifically, Tejas is one of the three pillars (with [[entities/pfbr]]-linked nuclear work and [[entities/mahi-banswara]]-linked reactor orders) that underpin its multi-year growth roadmap.
