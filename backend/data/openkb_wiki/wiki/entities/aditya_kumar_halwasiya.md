# Aditya Kumar Halwasiya

Aditya Kumar Halwasiya is a Non-Executive (Non-Independent) Director on the Board of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]], as profiled across the company's Annual Report 2025-26 ([[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part1]], [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part2]], [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part4]], [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part8]] and [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part10]]). He is described as a dynamic third-generation entrepreneur, investor and scion of the Pan-India Universal-Halwasiya Group.

## Background and Education

- Carries forward the legacy of the **Universal-Halwasiya Group**, founded by the Late Shri Madan Mohan Halwasiya in the early 1960s, with annual turnover in excess of INR 2,000 crore.
- Holds a Master's degree in Global Finance from Fordham University, New York, USA.
- Holds a Bachelor's degree in Commerce from St. Xavier's College, Kolkata.
- Born 09/08/1994 (aged 30 as of the FY 2025-26 report).

## Business Roles

- **Shareholder and director at Universal Petro-Chemicals Ltd**, a manufacturer in the automobile, industrial oils and specialty chemicals sectors, where he has actively looked after business marketing since 2019.
- Plays an **advisory role at Columbia Petrochem Ltd**, another family venture in the same industry, since 2019.
- Actively manages and oversees a substantial portfolio of **capital market investments and real estate projects across India**.
- Director at **Cupid Limited** and **Olka Technologies (OPC) Private Limited**.

## Committee Memberships Across Companies

- **Cupid Limited**: Member of the CSR Committee, Stakeholders Relationship Committee and Risk Management Committee.
- **Universal Petro-Chemicals Ltd**: Member of the Audit Committee and Nomination and Remuneration Committee.

## Role at Apollo Micro Systems

- Appointed to the Board on **21/01/2023** (DIN: 08200117).
- Serves as a **Non-Executive – Non-Independent Director** within a balanced eight-member Board that also comprises the Chairman, Managing Director, Whole-Time Directors and Independent Directors.
- As disclosed in the Corporate Governance Report (Annexure H of the FY 2025-26 Annual Report), he holds **three directorships in other public limited companies** — Universal Petro-Chemicals Ltd, Tourism Finance Corporation of India Limited and Cupid Limited — and **three chairperson/membership positions in Audit and Stakeholders Relationship Committees** of those same public limited companies.
- All statutory limits on directorships and committee positions are observed: none of the Directors is a director in more than 8 listed entities or more than 20 Indian companies (not more than 10 public limited companies), and no Director is a member of more than 10 Committees or Chairperson of more than 5 Committees across all public companies.
- Holds **15,00,000 equity shares** in the Company, either by self or on a beneficial basis for any other person.
- Has no relationship with other Directors or Key Managerial Personnel, and has **no resignations from listed entities in the past three years**.
- His directorship sits within the Board committees framework covering Audit, Nomination and Remuneration, Stakeholder Relationship, Risk Management, Corporate Social Responsibility, Executive and Securities Allotment — see [[concepts/corporate-governance-and-compliance]].
- The Board met **six times** during FY 2025-26 (23rd May 2025, 28th May 2025, 28th July 2025, 04th November 2025, 19th November 2025 and 09th February 2026), with the maximum interval between meetings not exceeding 120 days; the 28th AGM was held on 16th September 2025.
- In terms of Section 152(6) of the Companies Act, 2013, he **retires by rotation** and, being eligible, was recommended by the Board for **re-appointment**, which was approved by the shareholders at the Annual General Meeting. A corresponding retirement by rotation is scheduled at the 29th AGM.
- **Remuneration**: as a Non-Executive Director he has no pecuniary relationship or transaction with the Company beyond sitting fees and reimbursement of expenses; no sitting fees or remuneration were paid to him during FY 2025-26, and he received no stock options, sweat equity, commission or provident fund contribution from the Company.

## Related Party Disclosures

- Named in the consolidated financial statements for FY 2025-26 (Note 35, Related party disclosures, in [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part10]]) as a **Director** within the Company's list of related parties, identified in accordance with Ind AS 24 (see [[concepts/related-party-transactions]]).
- The related party transactions table in the same note records that he received a **dividend of ₹3.75 lakhs** from the Company during the year ended 31 March 2026 (previous year: nil), reflecting his equity holding in [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]].
- No other transactions with him are disclosed (no remuneration, rent, sitting fees or advances), consistent with his Non-Executive status; related-party transactions are stated to be on terms equivalent to arm's length.
- His only direct pecuniary interest disclosed during FY 2025-26 was this dividend.
