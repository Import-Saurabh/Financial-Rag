## Overview

Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers (GRSE) is an Indian defence shipyard, referenced in the [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part1|Apollo Micro Systems Limited Annual Report 2025-26]] as one of the Company's strategic industry partners. GRSE is a Defence Public Sector Undertaking (DPSU) based in Kolkata that builds warships and naval platforms for the [[entities/indian_navy|Indian Navy]] and other maritime customers.

## Role in Apollo's Partnership Ecosystem

The Annual Report names GRSE under the "Strategic Partnerships / Forging New Alliances" section of the corporate overview. Apollo Micro Systems Limited describes itself as an **MoU partner to GRSE for joint development and supply of advanced Weapons and Electronic Systems**.

The second part of the FY 2025-26 Annual Report records this alliance as a formal FY 2025-26 milestone, grouping it under "Mutual Partnerships" alongside the tripartite alliance with the [[entities/indian_navy|Indian Navy]] and [[entities/iit_madras|IIT Madras]] (Chennai). The milestone entry confirms that Apollo **signed a Memorandum of Understanding with Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers (GRSE)** during the year, moving the relationship from an announced partnership to a documented agreement executed within the reporting period.

This alliance sits alongside Apollo's other collaboration agreements — including an approved collaborative R&D partnership with [[entities/bharat_electronics_limited|Bharat Electronics Limited]], MoUs with [[entities/munitions_india_limited|Munitions India Ltd]], [[entities/bharat_dynamics_limited|Bharat Dynamics Limited]] (naval platforms) and TCL, and the tripartite alliance with the [[entities/indian_navy|Indian Navy]] and [[entities/iit_madras|IIT Madras]]. Together these arrangements support Apollo's stated ambition to become a [[concepts/tier_1_defense_oem_integration|Tier-I defence OEM]] integrating complete weapon systems and platforms.

## Strategic Significance

The GRSE relationship matters on two fronts for Apollo:

- **Forward integration into platforms** — pairing Apollo's weapon electronics, sub-systems and energetics with a shipyard that builds complete naval platforms moves Apollo from component supplier toward platform-level participation.
- **Domain coverage** — it reinforces Apollo's Sea-domain presence, complementing its underwater warfare work (torpedo homing, [[entities/migm|MIGM/Vighna]] mines) and its naval armament activities.

The formalisation of the MoU during FY 2025-26 also fits Apollo's broader pattern of converting partnership announcements into executed agreements, which supports its positioning as a qualified supplier within production-phase defence supply chains under the DcPP framework.
