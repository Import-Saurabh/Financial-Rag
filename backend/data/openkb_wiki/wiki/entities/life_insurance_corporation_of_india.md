# Life Insurance Corporation of India

**Type:** Organization

Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) is India's state-owned life insurance company. In the context of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]], LIC acts as the insurer and fund manager for the Company's employee gratuity plan.

## Role in the Apollo Micro Systems Group

According to [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part10]], the Company's obligation towards gratuity is a **defined benefit plan funded with Life Insurance Corporation of India**. The regulatory framework under which the plan operates is governed by LIC as the "group gratuity insurance manager".

This makes LIC the trustee/custodian of the plan assets held against the Company's gratuity liability, which is measured through actuarial valuation as required under [[concepts/employee-benefits]] and the relevant Ind AS framework.

| Contributions | 96.25 | 2.15 |

| Actuarial (gain)/loss on plan assets | (0.78) | (0.05) |
| **Fair value of plan assets at end of year** | **26.32** | **1.30** |

Against a present value of gratuity obligations of ₹502.06 lakhs, the LIC-managed plan assets of ₹26.32 lakhs left a **net liability recognised in the balance sheet of ₹475.73 lakhs** (FY25: ₹184.61 lakhs). The relatively low funding level reflects that the plan is largely unfunded in substance, with LIC holding limited assets on the Company's behalf.
