## Overview

IIT Madras (the Indian Institute of Technology Madras, also referred to in Apollo Micro Systems' disclosures as "IIT Chennai") is one of India's premier technical and engineering institutes, based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. Within the context of the [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part1|Apollo Micro Systems Limited Annual Report 2025-26]] and [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part2|its second part]], it appears as an academic partner in Apollo's defence technology ecosystem.

## Relationship with Apollo Micro Systems Limited

The FY 2025-26 annual report records that [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited|Apollo Micro Systems Limited]] formed a **tripartite alliance** with the [[entities/indian_navy|Indian Navy]] and IIT Madras. The Memorandum of Understanding underpinning this alliance was signed at **Swavalamban 2025**, an event connected to India's self-reliance (Atmanirbhar Bharat) push in defence.

The alliance is presented as part of Apollo's broader strategic partnership strategy, alongside:

- Approved collaborative R&D partner status with [[entities/bharat_electronics_limited|Bharat Electronics Limited]]
- An MoU with [[entities/munitions_india_limited|Munitions India Limited]] for select opportunities
- An MoU with [[entities/bharat_dynamics_limited|Bharat Dynamics Limited]] for joint development of naval platforms
- An MoU with [[entities/grse|GRSE]] for joint development and supply of advanced weapons and electronic systems

## Strategic Significance

The IIT Madras alliance is cited in Apollo's "Value Delivery Model" as one of the outputs and outcomes of its integrated operating model, and is specifically associated with **transfer of technology** — the company notes a Technology of Transfer (ToT) award and a Tripartite Alliance as indicators of ecosystem trust. Academic partnerships of this kind support Apollo's stated approach to absorbing [[concepts/transfer-of-technology|transferred technologies]], building indigenous capability in [[concepts/underwater-warfare|underwater warfare]] and related [[concepts/directed-energy-weapons|directed energy weapon]] domains, and strengthening its position as a [[concepts/tier_1_defense_oem_integration|Tier-1 defence OEM]].

Part 2 of the annual report restates the IIT Madras tripartite alliance as a headline FY 2025-26 milestone under "Mutual Partnerships," pairing it with the newly signed MoU with [[entities/grse|GRSE]]. This confirms that the alliance is treated as a core, ongoing element of Apollo's partnership framework rather than a one-off announcement. Because the report treats the alliance as a capability-building and indigenisation lever aligned with [[concepts/defense_indigenization|defence indigenisation]] policy, IIT Madras represents the academic leg of a government–industry–academia collaboration model increasingly central to India's defence research ecosystem.
