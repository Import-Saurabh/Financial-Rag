# HDFC Bank Limited

HDFC Bank Limited is an Indian private-sector bank that serves as one of the principal lenders to [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]], appearing in the standalone financial statements for the year ended 31 March 2026. Its facilities span both secured non-current vehicle loans and demand-repayable working capital borrowing.

## Facilities Extended to Apollo Micro Systems Limited

### Vehicle Loans (Non-current)

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Interest rate (p.a.) | 7.63% to 9.80% |
| Tenure | 36–60 instalments |
| Outstanding as at 31.03.2026 | ₹ 130.12 lakhs |
| Outstanding as at 31.03.2025 | ₹ 112.50 lakhs |

These vehicle loans are secured by hypothecation of the specific vehicles financed, and the borrowings are further secured by personal guarantees of two Directors and others in their individual capacities.

### Working Capital Overdraft (Current Borrowings)

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Interest rate | 9.75% |
| Nature | Overdraft |
| Outstanding as at 31.03.2026 | ₹ 14,534.05 lakhs |
| Outstanding as at 31.03.2025 | ₹ 12,100.84 lakhs |

## Security and Covenant Arrangements

HDFC Bank's loans, including Letters of Credit and Bank Guarantees, are repayable on demand and are secured by:

- Hypothecation of inventories, book debts and all other current assets, ranking pari passu with loans from [[entities/state_bank_of_india]], [[entities/bandhan_bank]] and Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs).
- Equitable mortgage of specific properties of the Company, its promoters and third parties, separately for each lender.
- Personal guarantees of two Directors and others in their individual capacities.

The company reports no default in repayment of loans or interest payments to HDFC Bank as at 31 March 2026 and 31 March 2025, and confirms that funds raised from borrowings were utilised for the purposes for which they were borrowed.

## Context

HDFC Bank's exposure forms part of the Company's broader borrowing profile, which totalled ₹ 11,925.97 lakhs in non-current borrowings and ₹ 36,578.53 lakhs in current borrowings at 31 March 2026. The vehicle loans are treated as [[concepts/financial-instruments]] measured at amortised cost under the effective interest rate method, while the underlying financed assets fall under [[concepts/property-plant-and-equipment]].
