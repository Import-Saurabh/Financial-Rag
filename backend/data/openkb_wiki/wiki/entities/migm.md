# MIGM (Man-Portable Anti-Tank Guided Missile)

The MIGM is a man-portable anti-tank guided missile being developed as part of India's defense indigenization efforts. In the Q1 FY26 earnings call, Apollo Micro Systems confirmed its role as a development-cum-production partner alongside [[entities/bharat_dynamics_limited]] (BDL).

## Key Facts from the Earnings Call

- Apollo and BDL are development-cum-production partners on the MIGM program.
- Technology transfer (ToT) documentation is currently being completed.
- The formal production order is expected within the financial year.
- Apollo is preparing full manufacturing capabilities, including the warhead segment, through [[entities/idl_explosives]].
- The program reflects broader [[concepts/defense_indigenization]] and Make in India priorities, with Apollo positioned as a key supplier in [[concepts/defense-program-supply-chains]].

Apollo's involvement in MIGM is part of its larger contribution to DRDO-led missile programs, which management estimated at roughly 63% of electronics and electromechanical work. The program also underscores the company's strategy of partnering with state-owned defense enterprises and integrating acquisitions like IDL Explosives to expand in-house capabilities.
