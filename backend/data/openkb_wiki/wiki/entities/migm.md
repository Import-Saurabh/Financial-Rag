# MIGM (Multi-Influence Ground Mine)

The MIGM — the **Multi-Influence Ground Mine**, named **Vighna** (also spelled *Vighana*) — is India's first indigenous naval mine of its class, developed under the Development cum Production Partner (DcPP) model with [[entities/drdo]]. [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] is the **approved production agency** for the mine, positioning it within India's broader [[concepts/underwater-warfare]] and [[concepts/defense_indigenization]] priorities.

## Key Facts from the Annual Report 2025-26

- Apollo is the approved production agency for the Multi-Influence Ground Mine (MIGM), Vighana, under DcPP by [[entities/drdo]].
- The MIGM is built to sharpen the [[entities/indian_navy]]'s edge against modern stealth ships and submarines.
- The capability it represents was previously held only by the United States, Russia, China and Italy — with this milestone, India joins the small group of technologically advanced nations in undersea warfare.
- Naval mines function as an unseen threat, giving a maritime nation the means to defend its waters regardless of naval strength, keeping underwater and surface navies at bay and forcing them away from the coastline toward open sea.
- Apollo expects to become a DcPP partner for other mine variants under [[entities/drdo]], extending its position across the naval mine portfolio.
- The mine family is showcased alongside Moored Mines, Land Mines (the PRACHAND anti-tank munition) and Limpet Mines; the Annual Report states combat trials for the [[entities/indian_navy]] have been completed successfully, and that several man-portable variants have been built as per mission requirements.

## Related Naval Mine Portfolio

The Annual Report positions the MIGM within a wider mines and underwater weapons portfolio:

- **Moored Mines** — used where waters are too deep for bottom mines; use acoustic, magnetic and pressure sensors, or more advanced optical shadow and electro-potential sensors, held at a set depth by a weighted anchor.
- **Land Mines** — PRACHAND, an anti-tank munition for full-width attack; new variants are in development powered by AI sensor fusion.
- **Limpet Mines** — hand-placed by divers on the underside of a ship's hull, held by magnets and man-portable.
- The mine portfolio sits alongside torpedoes, decoys, underwater autonomous platforms and loitering munitions in Apollo's diversified weapons offering, supported by the company's [[concepts/defence-manufacturing-licensing]] covering Torpedoes and Underwater Mines.

## Program Significance

Apollo's involvement in MIGM is part of its larger contribution to [[entities/drdo]]-led programmes, and it reflects the Company's strategy of partnering with state-owned defence enterprises while integrating acquisitions such as [[entities/idl_explosives]] to bring energetics and warhead capability in-house. The mine programme strengthens Apollo's position within domestic [[concepts/defense-program-supply-chains]] and its credentials as an integrated supplier under the [[concepts/tier_1_defense_oem_integration]] model, supported by industrialisation depth in [[concepts/transfer-of-technology]].

## Note on Acronym Usage

In the Q1 FY26 earnings call (see [[summaries/2025_Aug_2025_Transcript]]), the acronym "MIGM" was used in reference to a **man-portable anti-tank guided missile** programme on which Apollo and [[entities/bharat_dynamics_limited]] (BDL) were described as development-cum-production partners, with technology transfer documentation being completed, a formal production order expected within the financial year, and warhead manufacturing being prepared through [[entities/idl_explosives]]. The Annual Report 2025-26 (see [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part1]]), by contrast, expands MIGM as **Multi-Influence Ground Mine**, the naval mine described above. Readers should be aware that the same acronym appears in the wiki's source base with two different expansions and refer to the originating document for context.
