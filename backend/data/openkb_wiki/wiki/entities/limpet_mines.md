# Limpet Mines

**Type:** Product (underwater ordnance)

## Overview

Limpet Mines are compact underwater explosive devices designed to be covertly attached to the hulls of ships and maritime assets by combat divers and special forces. The Limpet Mine is one of the indigenously developed underwater weapon systems in the portfolio of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]], positioned as a capability for harbour denial and naval special operations missions.

## Key Features

- **Safety-and-arming mechanisms:** Advanced mechanisms that ensure both operational effectiveness and handler safety.
- **Programmable time-delay fuzing:** Allows controlled detonation timing after attachment.
- **Anti-removal features:** Designed to prevent tampering or removal by hostile forces.
- **Rugged design:** Emphasises reliability under extreme underwater conditions, ease of deployment, and secure arming protocols.

## Backward Integration

Backward integration within the Apollo Group provides access to qualified energetic materials, enabling end-to-end realisation of the weapon. This links the product to the group's [[concepts/defence-energetics]] capability, including step-down subsidiary [[entities/idl_explosives]].

## Strategic Role

The product addresses the specialised requirements of naval special operations and harbour denial missions. It extends the company's growing portfolio in underwater ordnance, alongside the [[entities/mini_torpedo]], [[entities/moored_mine]], and the [[entities/asw_weapon_platform]], all falling under the [[concepts/underwater-warfare]] domain and aligned with [[entities/indian_navy]] requirements.
