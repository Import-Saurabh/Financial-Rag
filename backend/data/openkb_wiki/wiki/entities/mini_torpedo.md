# Mini Torpedo

## Overview

The Mini Torpedo is a compact, lightweight underwater weapon designed for engagement of small and mid-size sub-surface threats, including midget submarines and underwater swimmer delivery vehicles. It is developed indigenously by [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] and represents a critical capability in littoral and shallow-water warfare, an area of growing operational priority.

## Key Features

- Integrates in-house acoustic homing, guidance electronics, actuation, and safety-and-arming technologies into a single platform.
- Reduced form factor enables deployment from a variety of launch platforms, including surface ships, helicopters, and unmanned systems.
- Leverages AMS's proven building blocks in signal processing and underwater sensing.
- Underscores the company's evolution into a complete underwater weapon systems house.

## Strategic Significance

The Mini Torpedo forms part of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]]'s expanding [[concepts/underwater-warfare]] portfolio, alongside programmes such as the [[entities/asw_weapon_platform]], [[entities/moored_mine]], and [[entities/limpet_mines]]. It reflects the broader push toward [[concepts/defense_indigenization]] in Indian defence manufacturing, with AMS positioning itself as a system-of-systems integrator capable of taking end-to-end responsibility for complete weapon platforms rather than discrete subsystems.

The programme was among the key R&D achievements reported for FY2025-26, during which AMS invested ₹7,253.39 lakhs in R&D activities.
