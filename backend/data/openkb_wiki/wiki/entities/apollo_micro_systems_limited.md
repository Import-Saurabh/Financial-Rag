# Apollo Micro Systems Limited

## Overview

Apollo Micro Systems Limited is an enterprise operating in the defense and electronics sector. It is documented in its FY 2024–25 annual report ([[summaries/2025_Financial_Year_2025_from_bse_part1]], [[summaries/2025_Financial_Year_2025_from_bse_part2]]) and most recently in its Q1 FY26 earnings conference call transcript ([[summaries/2025_Aug_2025_Transcript]]). The company positions itself as a key contributor to India's defense indigenization, supplying electronics and electromechanical systems across DRDO-led missile, torpedo, and mine programs ([[concepts/defense_indigenization]], [[concepts/defense-program-supply-chains]], [[concepts/tier_1_defense_oem_integration]]). It maintains a robust corporate governance structure and active CSR participation ([[concepts/corporate-social-responsibility]], [[concepts/corporate-governance-and-compliance]]).

## Governance and Operations

- **Board & Committees:** Managed by a balanced Board of Directors supported by specialized committees including Audit, Nomination and Remuneration, Stakeholders Relationship, Corporate Social Responsibility, and Risk Management Committees.
- **Statutory Compliance:** Adheres to SEBI (LODR) Regulations, Companies Act, Secretarial Standards, and maintains zero non-compliance penalties on core statutory audits.
- **Policies:** Implements frameworks like Whistle Blower Policies, Dividend Distribution Policies, and Material Events Policies.
- **Credit & Ownership:** Credit rating upgraded by ACUITE by two notches to A-/A2+, and promoter pledge reduction is on track toward zero by FY26.

## Financial Performance and Outlook

- **Q1 FY26 Results:** Revenue up 46% YoY to ₹234 crore; EBITDA up 83% to ₹41 crore (margin expanded from 25% to 31%); PAT more than doubled to ₹18 crore (margin up 400 bps to 13%).
- **Order Book:** Current order book stands at approximately ₹735 crore.
- **Guidance:** Revenue expected to grow at 45–50% CAGR over FY26–27 from the core business, excluding the IDL acquisition. Working capital cycle days are expected to reduce by 100–120 days from FY27.
- **Capex:** Ongoing capex is on schedule, with Phase 2 started; margin expansion may moderate in H2 FY26 and FY27 as capex ramps.

## Defense Programs and Supply Chains

- **MIGM:** Apollo and [[entities/bharat_dynamics_limited]] are development-cum-production partners; ToT documentation is being completed, with the formal order expected within the financial year. Warhead work is planned through [[entities/idl_explosives]] ([[entities/migm]]).
- **QRSAM:** Apollo supplies four critical subsystems, including the onboard computer, navigation system, power distribution units, and actuation system; orders are expected before December after BDL receives its order ([[entities/qrsam]]).
- **Moored Mine/Deep Sea Mine:** The system has been developed and offered to [[entities/drdo]], with a significant share of the DAC-approved order expected.
- **Project Kusha:** Apollo is involved in onboard systems, is the only industry supplier to have delivered systems, and trials are planned within months ([[entities/project_kusha]]).
- **Torpedo Programs:** Updates cover EHWT, ALWT, and Varunastra, including homing systems and signal processing; user trials and expected orders are slated for coming months.
- **BrahMos & Pinaka:** Apollo supplies subsystems for [[entities/brahmos]] and guidance electronics to MIL and EEL for [[entities/pinaka]]; Pinaka production orders are expected in the next 3–4 months.
- Apollo states it contributes around 63% of electronics and electromechanical work for DRDO missile programs.

## Export Strategy and Indigenization

- **Maiden Export Order:** $13.37 million (~₹113.81 crore) for an advanced avionics system for dual civil and military aircraft platforms, marking its entry into [[concepts/defense-exports-and-global-market-entry]].
- **Product Development:** Developing export-qualified defense products including mines, aerial bombs, submarine bombs, rockets, and kamikaze boats, with ₹100 crore earmarked.
- **Partnerships:** A collaboration with GRSE exists for joint product development.
- **Emerging Capabilities:** Building in-house RF capabilities and developing defense cargo drones, with a model shown at Aero India.

## Acquisitions and Fundraising

- **IDL Explosives:** Acquisition formalities are nearing completion; consultants, including ex-DRDO scientists, are working on technology transfer and explosives manufacturing for missiles and MIGM ([[entities/idl_explosives]]).
- **Fundraising:** Received in-principle approval from stock exchanges for preferential equity allotment; 50% of the amount mobilized has been received. Warrants worth ₹400+ crore include ₹100 crore already received and ₹325 crore expected within four months, primarily for working capital.

## Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)

- **CSR Obligation:** Calculated at ₹63.06 Lakhs (2% of average net profits of ₹3,153.23 Lakhs) for FY 2024–25.
- **Actual Spend:** ₹100.00 Lakhs, resulting in an excess spend of ₹36.94 Lakhs for future set-offs.
- **Key Partners:** Collaborated with entities like [[entities/arunodaya_trust]] for environmental sustainability and [[entities/sarada_educational_society]] for educational support.
