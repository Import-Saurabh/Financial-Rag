# Apollo Micro Systems Limited

## Overview

Apollo Micro Systems Limited (CIN: L72200TG1997PLC026556) is an enterprise operating in the defense and electronics sector, headquartered in Hyderabad (Regd Office: Plot No.128/A, Road No.12, BEL Road, IDA-Mallapur, Uppal Mandal, Hyderabad-500076, Telangana; BSE Scrip Code 540879; NSE Symbol: APOLLO; ISIN INE713T01028). It is documented across its FY 2024–25 annual report ([[summaries/2025_Financial_Year_2025_from_bse_part1]], [[summaries/2025_Financial_Year_2025_from_bse_part2]]), its Q1 FY26 earnings conference call transcript ([[summaries/2025_Aug_2025_Transcript]]), and its FY 2025-26 Annual Report ([[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part1]], [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part2]], [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part3]], [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part4]], [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part5]], [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part6]], [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part7]], [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part8]], [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part9]], [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part10]], [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part11]]).

Incorporated on 3 March 1997 as a private limited company and converted to a public limited company with effect from 1 April 2017, and tracing its heritage to 1985, the company cites a 41-year track record of indigenous design and delivery and describes itself as a Tier-I Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM). It positions itself as a key contributor to India's defense indigenization, supplying electronics and electromechanical systems across DRDO-led missile, torpedo, and mine programs ([[concepts/defense_indigenization]], [[concepts/defense-program-supply-chains]], [[concepts/tier_1_defense_oem_integration]]). It maintains a robust corporate governance structure and active CSR participation ([[concepts/corporate-social-responsibility]], [[concepts/corporate-governance-and-compliance]]).

The FY26 report frames the company under the theme "Sovereign by Design. Global by Ambition," reflecting a strategic transition from a Tier-I supplier of critical electronics to a recognised global OEM of complete weapon systems and platforms, with capability across Land, Air and Sea domains. The Company holds a valid Defence Industrial Licence, is certified to AS9100 Rev D (Aerospace Quality Management), and has delivered mission-critical systems to [[entities/drdo]] and the Indian Armed Forces. Its equity shares are listed on [[entities/bse_limited]] and [[entities/national_stock_exchange_of_india]] (Symbol: APOLLO).

All amounts in the consolidated financial statements are stated in ₹ lakhs unless otherwise specified. The consolidated financial statements for the year ended 31 March 2026 were approved for issue by the Board of Directors on 18 May 2026.

## Group Structure

The consolidated group comprises the Holding Company and its subsidiaries (collectively, "the Group"):

- [[entities/ananya_sip_rf_technologies_private_limited]] — Subsidiary, 51% held, incorporated in India (acquired FY 2020-21; goodwill of ₹1.89 lakhs recognised)
- [[entities/apollo_defence_industries_private_limited]] — Subsidiary, 76% held, incorporated in India
- [[entities/apollo_strategic_technologies_private_limited]] — Step-down subsidiary, 51% held, incorporated in India
- [[entities/idl_explosives]] — Step-down subsidiary, 100% acquired during FY 2025-26, incorporated in India

The Group's core business is the supply of electronics and electro-mechanical systems and components, including design, research & development of systems used in missile programmes (weapon systems electronics), underwater missile programmes (weapon systems electronics), avionics systems, ship borne systems and submarine systems. The Group operates as a single reportable segment, and the Chief Operating Decision Maker monitors operating results of the Company as a whole ([[concepts/segment-reporting]]).

## Acquisition of IDL Explosives Limited

On 2 May 2025, [[entities/apollo_defence_industries_private_limited]] ("ADIPL"), a subsidiary of the Company, entered into a Share Purchase Agreement to acquire 100% of the equity share capital of [[entities/idl_explosives]] ("IDL"). The transaction was completed on 15 November 2025 (the "acquisition date"), on which date ADIPL obtained control of IDL, thereby making IDL a wholly owned step-down subsidiary of the Company. IDL is engaged in the manufacture of industrial explosives, accessories and allied products, and qualifies as a business as defined in Ind AS 103. The acquisition was undertaken to expand the Group's defence and explosives manufacturing capability and to obtain access to IDL's manufacturing licences and infrastructure ([[concepts/ind-as-103-business-combinations]], [[concepts/defence-acquisitions-and-integration]], [[concepts/defence-manufacturing-licensing]]).

## Significant Accounting Policies (FY 2025-26)

The consolidated financial statements are prepared in accordance with Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS). Key policies include:

- **Provisions and contingent liabilities** — Provisions are recognised when a present legal or constructive obligation arising from a past event is probable and can be reliably estimated; discounting is applied where the time value of money is material, with the unwinding recognised as a finance cost. Contingent liabilities are disclosed unless the possibility of outflow is remote, and contingent assets are not recognised but disclosed when inflow is probable ([[concepts/provisions-and-contingent-liabilities]]).
- **Government grants** — Recognised only when there is reasonable assurance of compliance with conditions and receipt. Revenue grants are recognised as other income without deducting from the cost of the asset; grants compensating additional depreciation are recognised over the useful life of the related asset. Loans provided below market rate are treated as a government grant measured at fair value, recognised as deferred income and released over the loan tenure ([[concepts/government-grants]]).
- **Earnings per share** — Basic EPS is computed on the net profit attributable to equity shareholders divided by the weighted average number of equity shares outstanding; diluted EPS adjusts for dilutive potential equity shares ([[concepts/earnings-per-share]]).
- **Statement of cash flows** — Reported using the indirect method, with cash flows segregated into operating, investing and financing activities.
- **Investment property** — Initially at cost including transaction costs; subsequently at cost less accumulated depreciation and impairment. Depreciation on buildings is on a straight-line basis per Schedule II of the Companies Act, 2013, and derecognition gains/losses are recognised in profit or loss.
- **Research and development** — Research expenditure is expensed as incurred; development expenditure is capitalised only if reliably measurable, technically and commercially feasible, with probable future economic benefits and sufficient resources to complete.
- **Borrowing costs** — Borrowing costs directly attributable to acquisition, construction or production of a qualifying asset are capitalised; other borrowing costs are expensed.
- **Events after the reporting date** — Adjusting events are recognised; material non-adjusting events are disclosed.
- **Cash dividend to equity holders** — A liability is recognised when the distribution is authorised and no longer at the Company's discretion, with a corresponding amount recognised directly in other equity.
- **Changes in material accounting policies** — The Company adopted the amendments to Ind AS 1 on classification of liabilities as current or non-current and non-current liabilities with covenants from 1 April 2025, applied retrospectively. Further Ind AS 1 amendments on covenant breaches are effective from 1 April 2026.
- **New accounting pronouncements** — The Ministry of Corporate Affairs notifies new standards or amendments under the Companies (Indian Accounting Standards) Rules; the Company applied the relevant amendments effective 1 April 2024 for the first time during the year.

### Financial risk management (Note 45)

The Group is exposed to market risk, credit risk and liquidity risk; senior management oversees these risks and the Board reviews the related policies. Financial instruments affected include loans and borrowings and refundable deposits ([[concepts/financial-risk-management]]).

- **Interest rate risk** — relates primarily to long-term debt obligations with floating interest rates. The Group maintains a balanced portfolio of fixed and variable rate loans and does not enter into interest rate swaps. Sensitivity to a reasonably possible change in interest rates (with all other variables held constant): a +1% change reduces profit before tax by ₹532.41 lakhs (FY25: ₹330.76 lakhs); a -1% change increases profit before tax by the same amounts ([[concepts/financial-risk-management]]).
- **Foreign currency risk** — arises primarily from operating activities (revenue or expense denominated in a foreign currency); the Company evaluates its exposure to exchange-rate fluctuations.
- **Credit risk** — arises principally from operating activities (trade receivables) and investing activities (deposits with banks and financial institutions). Credit risk is controlled by analysing credit limits and creditworthiness of customers, and collections are monitored continuously. No provision for expected credit losses on trade receivables was made based on past and recent collection trends. Credit risk on cash and cash equivalents is limited due to transacting with highly rated banks and financial institutions.
- **Liquidity risk** — the Group's objective is to maintain a balance between continuity of funding and flexibility through bank deposits and loans. The contractual undiscounted maturity profile as at 31 March 2026 shows borrowings of ₹37,316.36 lakhs on demand, ₹1,096.65 lakhs in less than 3 months, ₹3,289.95 lakhs in 3–12 months and ₹12,622.95 lakhs in 1–5 years; trade payables of ₹37,548.51 lakhs are all on demand (FY25: borrowings ₹24,551.74 lakhs on demand, ₹434.35 lakhs less than 3 months, ₹1,303.04 lakhs 3–12 months and ₹7,245.60 lakhs 1–5 years; trade payables ₹27,952.37 lakhs on demand).
