## Overview

Acuite Rating & Research Limited is a credit rating agency in India. In the Apollo Micro Systems Limited Annual Report for FY 2025-26, it is identified as the "credit rating agency" engaged by the Company to rate its bank facilities.

## Ratings Assigned

During the year under review, [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] obtained a credit rating from Acuite Rating & Research Limited on its ₹707.50 Crores Line of Credit, split as:

- Short term: ₹150.00 Crores
- Long term: ₹557.50 Crores

The ratings assigned were:

| Facility | Rating |
|---|---|
| Long-Term Rating | ACUITE A- (pronounced "ACUITE A minus") |
| Short-Term Rating | ACUITE A2+ (reaffirmed "ACUITE A Two Plus") |

The outlook on the long-term rating was assigned as **"Stable"** by the agency.

**International Rating:** Nil — no international rating was obtained.

## Disclosure

Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, the Company intimated the rating to the stock exchanges where its shares are listed — [[entities/bse_limited]] and [[entities/national_stock_exchange_of_india]].
