# Srilekha Jasthi

**Role:** Head, Strategy and Investor Relations — [[entities/mtar-technologies]]
**Appears in:** [[summaries/2026_Aug_2026_Transcript]] (Q1 FY27 earnings conference call, 30 July 2026)

## Who She Is

Srilekha Jasthi is the Head of Strategy and Investor Relations at MTAR Technologies Limited, the precision-engineering company operating in civil nuclear power, aerospace and defense, and clean energy. She is identified in the Q1 FY27 earnings call transcript as "Ms. Srilekha Jasthi – Head, Strategy and IR – MTAR Technologies Limited," and is listed among the four named members of the management panel for that call.

## Role on the Q1 FY27 Earnings Call

She was introduced by the moderator, Vidhi Vasa of MUFG, as part of the management team alongside [[entities/srinivas-reddy]] (Managing Director and Promoter) and [[entities/gunneswara-rao]] (Chief Financial Officer), with Orient Capital named as the company's investor relations partner.

Her responsibilities in the call itself were largely facilitative rather than presentational:

- She did not deliver a prepared opening statement; the MD's strategic remarks and the CFO's financial review covered that ground.
- She was named by the Managing Director as a follow-up resource on a specific investor query — the breakup of the quarter's INR16 crore interest cost, which he said the CFO "or Srilekha" could provide separately at a later point.
- She forms part of the investor-facing channel through which MTAR communicates on earnings, alongside the CFO.

## Investor Relations Context

The call she supported disclosed a record quarter for the company: revenue of INR360.7 crore, EBITDA of INR85.1 crore at a 23.54% margin, and PAT of INR50.2 crore, together with a closing order book of INR5,143 crore and a further INR800 crore of orders announced on the day. See [[concepts/order-book-and-revenue-visibility]] for the visibility framework management laid out.

This disclosure activity sits within the company's obligations under SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, with the transcript formally filed to [[entities/bse_limited]] and [[entities/national_stock_exchange_of_india]] and the filing countersigned by Company Secretary and Compliance Officer [[entities/priyanka-agarwal]].
