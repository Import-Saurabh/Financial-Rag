# IDL Explosives

IDL Explosives is an explosives manufacturing organization being acquired by [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] to support India's defense ordnance and missile programs.

## Role in Apollo Micro Systems

As of the Q1 FY26 earnings call (August 2025), the acquisition formalities for IDL Explosives were nearing completion. Consultants, including former DRDO scientists, are working on technology transfer and explosives manufacturing capabilities. These efforts are directed at producing warheads for missiles and the MIGM program, reinforcing Apollo's contribution to [[concepts/defense-program-supply-chains]].

## Strategic Importance

IDL Explosives strengthens Apollo's position as a [[concepts/tier_1_defense_oem_integration]] partner by adding in-house explosives and warhead production — a critical capability for indigenous defense manufacturing under [[concepts/defense_indigenization]]. The integration supports collaborations with organizations such as [[entities/bharat_dynamics_limited]] and [[entities/drdo]] on programs like [[entities/migm]].
