# IDL Explosives

IDL Explosives Limited (CIN: U28132TG2010PLC070529) is an Indian industrial explosives manufacturer that became a step-down subsidiary of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] after the acquisition of a 100% equity stake by [[entities/apollo_defence_industries_private_limited]] (ADIPL). Note 47 to the consolidated financial statements for the year ended 31 March 2026 records that ADIPL entered into a Share Purchase Agreement dated 02 May 2025 to acquire 100% of the equity share capital of IDL, with the transaction completed on 15 November 2025 — the acquisition date on which ADIPL obtained control and IDL became a wholly owned step-down subsidiary of the Company. The total purchase consideration was ₹10,700.00 lakhs (₹107 crore), satisfied in cash.

The Secretarial Audit Report and the Directors' Report for FY 2025-26 record that the formalities regarding the acquisition were completed on 17 November 2025 (earlier disclosures referenced 15 November 2025), with IDL consolidated with effect from that date. The Form AOC-1 statement of salient features of step-down subsidiaries in the FY 2025-26 Annual Report confirms this 17 November 2025 date of becoming a step-down subsidiary, and the consequent accounting period for the subsidiary runs from 17 November 2025 to 31 March 2026. In the related-party disclosures forming part of the 29th Annual General Meeting notice, in Note 34 to the standalone financial statements for the year ended 31 March 2026 and in the Form AOC-2 disclosure, IDL is listed as a step-down subsidiary of the Group, with 100% of its shares held by ADIPL and an indirect equity interest of 76% held by the listed entity through ADIPL. Note 37 (Description of the Group) and Note 35 (Related party disclosures) to the consolidated financial statements for the year ended 31 March 2026 likewise identify IDL Explosives Limited as a step-down subsidiary incorporated in India, with a 100% direct/indirect ownership interest held through ADIPL.

## Business Profile

IDL manufactures the full range of packaged and bulk explosives engineered specifically for mining and infrastructure projects, and also offers an extensive range of cartridge explosives. Demand is anchored in coal and mineral production and the continuing build-out of national infrastructure, with Coal India Limited, Singareni Collieries and private miners, quarrying, road and irrigation projects forming the core customer base.

The business brings established manufacturing units across India's key mining belts, long-standing relationships with Coal India subsidiaries and institutional customers, and a growing dealer and export franchise.

## Role in Apollo Micro Systems

As of the Q1 FY26 earnings call (August 2025), the acquisition formalities were nearing completion, with consultants including former DRDO scientists working on technology transfer and explosives manufacturing capabilities aimed at producing warheads for missiles and the MIGM program.

The acquisition has since completed and IDL has been fully integrated into the Group's operations, financial reporting and safety framework, alongside Apollo's Hyderabad electronics and systems facilities, taking Group manufacturing capacity to six plants under one governance structure, a common quality management system and a single digital traceability thread. On a consolidated basis, the Group's operations now additionally include the manufacture of industrial explosives through IDL Explosives Limited. Form AOC-1 records IDL's contribution to the overall performance of the Company at 76%, consistent with the Group's indirect shareholding through ADIPL. Note 48 to the standalone financial statements, which discloses the Company's investments in subsidiaries, records IDL Explosives Limited as a 100%-held step-down subsidiary domiciled in India.

The Business Responsibility & Sustainability Report (BRSR) published in the FY 2025-26 Annual Report situates IDL within the Group's corporate structure, identifying it as a step-down subsidiary 100% owned by ADIPL, alongside fellow step-down subsidiary [[entities/apollo_strategic_technologies_private_limited]] and direct subsidiaries [[entities/ananya_sip_rf_technologies_private_limited]] and ADIPL in the holding/subsidiary/associate table. The BRSR is prepared on a standalone basis for the listed entity, and records that IDL does not participate in the Business Responsibility initiatives of Apollo Micro Systems Limited. The Corporate Governance Report in the same Annual Report states that the listed entity does not have any material subsidiary company; accordingly, IDL is reviewed through the Audit Committee's oversight of the consolidated financial statements and of investments made by unlisted subsidiaries, but is not classified as a material subsidiary.

IDL received an Industrial Licence for high explosives, including TNT and RDX — with HMX also previously referenced by the Managing Director — marking Apollo's entry into specialised [[concepts/defence-energetics]]. Combined with the Company's existing strengths in fuzes and weapon electronics, this enables forward integration from electronics into complete energetic systems, complementing the broader [[concepts/defence-manufacturing-licensing]] framework that authorises Apollo to build complete weapon platforms.

The consolidation note lists IDL Explosives Limited as a step-down subsidiary incorporated in India, with a 100.00% holding as at 31 March 2026 (against nil as at 31 March 2025), consolidated for the purpose of the consolidated financial statements from its acquisition date; the consolidation procedure eliminates intragroup assets, liabilities, equity, income, expenses and cash flows in full, and attributes profit or loss and each component of other comprehensive income to the owners of the Holding Company and to the non-controlling interests even where this results in a deficit balance for the non-controlling interests.

Note 51 to the consolidated financial statements, which discloses additional information as required by Schedule III to the Companies Act, 2013, presents [[entities/apollo_defence_industries_private_limited]] on a consolidated basis — including its step-down subsidiaries [[entities/apollo_strategic_technologies_private_limited]] and IDL Explosives Limited. That combined segment contributed net assets of (₹1,808.97) lakhs ((1.38)% of consolidated net assets), a share of consolidated profit or loss of (₹1,812.52) lakhs ((16.88)%), a share of other comprehensive income of ₹13.53 lakhs (56.13%) and a share of total comprehensive income of (₹1,798.98) lakhs ((16.72)%). The note confirms that the Company has no joint ventures or associates as at 31 March 2026.

IDL's provisional goodwill is reflected in the Consolidated Balance Sheet, where goodwill stands at ₹14,519.55 lakhs as at 31 March 2026 compared with ₹1.89 lakhs as at 31 March 2025, forming part of total non-current assets of ₹70,300.15 lakhs (prior year ₹27,020.48 lakhs). Note 5 to the consolidated financial statements records gross goodwill of ₹14,519.55 lakhs at 31 March 2026, made up of the opening balance of ₹1.89 lakhs (relating to the earlier acquisition of a 51% interest in [[entities/ananya_sip_rf_technologies_private_limited]]) and goodwill arising on business combinations of ₹14,517.66 lakhs during the year, with no accumulated amortisation or impairment loss; reference is made to Note 47 for the underlying business-combination details. The Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss for FY 2025-26 reports profit for the year of ₹10,738.04 lakhs, of which ₹11,292.04 lakhs is attributable to the owners of the Company and (₹554.00) lakhs to non-controlling interests, with total comprehensive income attributable to non-controlling interests of (₹540.47) lakhs. The Consolidated Statement of Changes in Equity records non-controlling interest of (₹487.46) lakhs as at 31 March 2026 (31 March 2025: ₹66.53 lakhs), a movement consistent with the acquisition of an entity whose identifiable liabilities exceeded its identifiable assets and whose indirect interest is held through ADIPL.

The underlying assets and liabilities consolidated on account of the business combination are reflected across the consolidated notes. Additions on account of acquisition through business combinations comprised ₹8,770.22 lakhs of [[concepts/property-plant-and-equipment]] (Note 4(a)), ₹2,868.43 lakhs of [[concepts/capital-work-in-progress]] (Note 4(b)), ₹5.59 lakhs of right-of-use land assets (Note 4(c), within [[concepts/leases]]) and ₹48.91 lakhs of other intangible assets (software) (Note 4(d)). On the liability side, the reconciliation of non-current borrowings (Note 15) records borrowings of ₹17,098.13 lakhs brought in on account of the business combination, and current borrowings include ₹3,894.03 lakhs on the same basis. [[concepts/deferred-tax]] (Note 18) records a deferred tax liability of ₹405.18 lakhs on property, plant and equipment and a deferred tax asset movement of ₹8.90 lakhs on employee benefits recognised on account of the business combination. Employee benefit obligations assumed through the combination are reflected in the gratuity disclosures (Note 40), which record a present value of obligations on account of business combination of ₹261.44 lakhs and a vested past service cost of ₹89.50 lakhs, with the corresponding provisions reflected in Note 17.

## Acquisition Accounting under Ind AS 103

In the standalone financial statements for the year ended 31 March 2026, the auditors record that the Group acquired 100% of the equity shareholding of IDL Explosives Limited through a share acquisition, with the equity shares of IDL purchased from IDL's shareholders for cash consideration. The Independent Auditor's Report on the Consolidated Financial Statements describes the same transaction as a share acquisition whereby equity shares of the Company were allotted to the shareholders of IDL in consideration for the entire share capital of IDL. Consequent to the transaction, IDL became a wholly-owned step-down subsidiary of the Company, and the transaction has been accounted for as a business combination under [[concepts/ind-as-103-business-combinations]].

Note 47 sets out the acquisition accounting. The total purchase consideration for the acquisition was ₹10,700.00 lakhs, payable in cash, and the net cash outflow on acquisition was ₹8,965.22 lakhs after deducting cash and cash equivalents acquired of ₹1,734.78 lakhs. The amounts recognised in respect of identifiable assets acquired and liabilities assumed, measured at their provisional fair values as at the acquisition date, were: [[concepts/property-plant-and-equipment]] ₹4,888.12 lakhs; [[concepts/capital-work-in-progress]] ₹2,309.28 lakhs; intangible assets ₹24.01 lakhs; inventories ₹3,116.69 lakhs; trade receivables ₹3,786.10 lakhs; cash and cash equivalents ₹2,174.57 lakhs; other financial and non-financial assets ₹4,790.91 lakhs; trade payables and other financial liabilities ₹3,768.49 lakhs; borrowings ₹20,721.03 lakhs; and provisions ₹417.83 lakhs. Total identifiable net assets acquired were therefore negative ₹(3,817.67) lakhs, generating goodwill on acquisition of ₹14,517.67 lakhs, which reconciles the total consideration transferred to the acquired net asset position.

At the acquisition date, IDL's identifiable liabilities exceeded its identifiable assets (i.e. negative net assets), while the fair value of the consideration transferred was positive. This resulted in the recognition of goodwill amounting to ₹14,517.66 lakhs — the excess of consideration transferred over the net identifiable assets (negative) acquired — in accordance with paragraph 32 of Ind AS 103. As at the balance sheet date, the Company had not carried out a fair valuation of the identifiable assets acquired and liabilities assumed, and management proposed that the Purchase Price Allocation (PPA) exercise, including fair valuation of assets and liabilities and consequent reassessment of goodwill, be completed within the measurement period of twelve months from the acquisition date, as permitted under paragraphs 45–50 of Ind AS 103, concluding no later than 15 November 2026. The goodwill recognised as at the balance sheet date is therefore provisional in nature and based on the book values of IDL's assets and liabilities rather than their fair values, pending completion of the PPA. Under Ind AS 103.45–50 the Company will retrospectively adjust the provisional amounts recognised as at the acquisition date, with a corresponding adjustment to goodwill, to reflect new information obtained about facts and circumstances that existed at the acquisition date. The goodwill of ₹14,517.67 lakhs is attributable to the assembled workforce, manufacturing know-how and statutory licences held by IDL, and synergies expected from integration with the Group's defence manufacturing business, none of which qualify for separate recognition as an identifiable intangible asset; the goodwill so recognised is not expected to be deductible for income tax purposes. Acquisition-related costs of ₹2.73 lakhs, comprising legal, due diligence and advisory fees incurred in connection with the acquisition, were recognised as an expense in the Statement of Profit and Loss for the year ended 31 March 2026. In the consolidated financial statements the matter is disclosed in Note 47, which is cross-referenced from the auditor's report; the consolidated balance sheet accordingly shows goodwill of ₹14,519.55 lakhs.

IDL contributed revenue of ₹13,764.39 lakhs and profit/(loss) after tax of (₹262.82) lakhs to the Group for the period from the acquisition date (15 November 2025) to 31 March 2026, as included in the consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss for the year. Had the acquisition of IDL been completed on 1 April 2025 (the first day of the financial year), the Group's revenue for the year ended 31 March 2026 would have been ₹1,14,647.77 lakhs and the Group's profit for the year would have been ₹7,936.79 lakhs.

This was identified as a [[concepts/key-audit-matters]] in the Independent Auditor's Report issued by [[entities/s_t_mohite_and_co]] on 18 May 2026, because the determination of the fair value of consideration transferred involves significant estimation and judgement, and because the quantum of goodwill recognised is material to the financial statements — any subsequent fair valuation during the measurement period could result in a significant retrospective adjustment to goodwill, with consequential impact on the opening balance sheet and comparatives. The auditor's procedures included understanding and evaluating the terms of the share agreement and the basis for accounting as a business combination under Ind AS 103; confirming that the legal acquirer (the Group Company) is also the accounting acquirer; agreeing the provisional goodwill to the underlying acquisition-date balance sheet of IDL; testing the journal entries recorded for the acquisition, recognition of the investment/net assets and resultant goodwill; evaluating the adequacy of Ind AS 103 disclosures, specifically that the initial accounting is incomplete for price allocation and that assets, liabilities and goodwill are recognised on a provisional basis; evaluating management's future profitability assumptions supporting the commercial rationale for the purchase (comparing projected cash flows and assessing the reasonableness of key assumptions such as revenue growth, margins and synergies against industry data and past forecasting accuracy); and obtaining a written representation from management that no impairment indicators exist for the provisional goodwill as at the reporting date. The consolidated auditor's report also records that the audit evidence obtained, including from other auditors, except in the case of a subsidiary which is not material, was sufficient and appropriate.

## Related-Party Transactions and Funding

As a related party of the Group, IDL is covered by the material related-party transaction approvals sought at the 2026 AGM. These include intercorporate loans proposed to be advanced to IDL as and when required during financial year 2027, up to ₹300 crore, for meeting day-to-day working capital requirements, as well as a proposed guarantee of up to ₹300 crore towards the raising or renewal of working capital debt. The guarantee is expected to carry an arm's-length guarantee commission as permitted by lenders, with IDL indemnifying the Company for any losses arising on invocation. Proposed loans carry an indicative interest rate of 9.90% and are unsecured, funded from internal accruals.

During FY 2025-26, the Company advanced a loan of ₹21,755.60 lakhs to IDL, of which ₹18,757.61 lakhs remained outstanding as on 31 March 2026. Note 5(ii) to the standalone financial statements for the year ended 31 March 2026, which sets out the Company's non-current "Loans" as unsecured and considered good, corroborates this: it lists IDL Explosives Limited — identified as a step-down subsidiary — as a related-party borrower with an outstanding balance of ₹18,757.61 lakhs (against the prior-year comparative of ₹1,786.61 lakhs in the aggregate subsidiary/step-down line), carrying interest at 9.90% per annum, unsecured, with interest payable on a quarterly basis and no stipulated repayment period. Aggregate loans to subsidiaries and step-down subsidiaries at 31 March 2026 were ₹31,555.75 lakhs, alongside loans to employees of ₹60.07 lakhs. Note 42 to the standalone financial statements, which discloses loans, guarantees and investments under Section 186(4) of the Companies Act, 2013, likewise records a loan to IDL Explosives Limited without a repayment period of ₹18,757.61 lakhs as at 31 March 2026 (prior year: nil), with no equity investment in IDL held directly by the listed entity.

Note 34 to the standalone financial statements, which sets out the related-party transactions of the Company for the year ended 31 March 2026, records the following dealings with IDL Explosives Limited as a step-down subsidiary: interest received of ₹557.79 lakhs (prior year: nil), loans and advances (net) of ₹18,255.60 lakhs (prior year: nil), and rent received of ₹0.45 lakhs (prior year: nil). The corresponding year-end outstanding balances with IDL were a loan of ₹18,757.61 lakhs, accrued interest of ₹502.01 lakhs and rent receivable of ₹1.35 lakhs. IDL was reported as a continuous, long-term Loans & Advances transaction, repayable on demand with interest at 9.90% p.a., approved by the shareholders on 16 August 2024 and by the Board on 23 July 2025. The Form AOC-2 disclosure for the year ended 31 March 2026 records rent received by the Company from IDL of ₹1.35 lakhs over 4.5 months, approved by the Board on 4 November 2025. The related-party disclosure table also records that the value of obligations undertaken by the listed entity or its subsidiary for which a guarantee, performance guarantee, surety, indemnity or comfort letter has been provided stands at ₹300 crore in respect of IDL. Consistent with the consolidated disclosures, Note 35 to the consolidated financial statements lists IDL Explosives Limited among the step-down subsidiaries that constitute related parties of the Group.

The standalone auditor's report for FY 2025-26 flags related-party transactions as a key audit matter given the volume and materiality of transactions during the year — including the provision of unsecured loans, the sale and leasing of properties and investments in the share capital of subsidiaries — and the need to comply with the Companies Act, 2013 and SEBI LODR Regulations; the auditor's procedures covered the controls over capturing and disclosing related-party transactions (note 34 to the standalone financial statements), the arm's-length pricing policy and audit committee/board approval process, and compliance with Sections 177, 185, 186 and 188 of the Companies Act, 2013. Annexure A to the auditor's report records that aggregate loans granted to subsidiaries and step-down subsidiaries during the year were ₹29,769.14 lakhs, with balances outstanding of ₹31,555.75 lakhs as at the balance sheet date, and that the terms on which loans were granted, subject to some inadequate documentation, are not prejudicial to the Company's interest. On a consolidated basis, the Group's cash flow statement for FY 2025-26 records loans to others of (₹4,198.16) lakhs as an investing outflow.

The Corporate Governance Report in the FY 2025-26 Annual Report confirms that there were no materially significant related-party transactions with the potential to conflict with the interests of the Company at large, and that all transactions with related parties during the year were in the ordinary course of business and on an arm's-length basis; the Audit Committee reviews related-party transactions periodically under a Board-approved "Related Party Transaction Policy", and the Company has not entered into any transaction with a promoter or promoter-group entity holding 10% or more of its shareholding. Consistent with this, the BRSR's "open-ness of business" disclosure on concentration of purchases, sales and related-party dealings reports loans and advances given to related parties at 97.83% of total loans and advances, and investments in related parties at 100% of total investments made — figures reflecting the Group's funding and investment flows into its subsidiary structure, of which IDL is part. IDL's related-party dealings therefore form part of the Company's wider [[concepts/related-party-transactions]] governance regime and its [[concepts/corporate-governance-and-compliance]] architecture.

IDL's Debt Service Coverage Ratio, based on the last audited financial statements, was (0.16) before the proposed transaction; the post-transaction ratio cannot be computed because the transactions are being undertaken under an omnibus approval and the final transaction amount has not yet been determined. For the same reason, the Debt to Equity Ratio could not be computed with reference to a specific figure.

Reporting currency is INR and the entity is domiciled in India.

In the related-party material forming part of the AGM notice, IDL was reported with turnover of ₹37,979.77 lakhs, a profit after tax of (₹395.64 lakhs) and a net worth of (₹3,924.41 lakhs). Aggregate transactions between the listed entity/subsidiaries and IDL, on a gross basis, amounted to ₹18,813.84 lakhs during FY 2025-26, with ₹4,104.26 lakhs recorded in the current financial year up to the quarter immediately preceding the quarter in which approval is sought. The negative net worth reported in these disclosures is consistent with the auditor's finding that IDL's identifiable liabilities exceeded its identifiable assets at the acquisition date, which is the reason the acquisition generated provisional goodwill rather than a bargain purchase, and is also consistent with the negative non-controlling interest of (₹487.46) lakhs recorded in the Consolidated Balance Sheet at 31 March 2026.

No default by IDL on any obligation under transactions or arrangements with the listed entity or its subsidiary was reported during the last financial year, and the related-party disclosures confirm that over the last three financial years IDL's accounts have not been classified as a non-performing asset by any of its bankers, it has not been declared a "wilful defaulter" by any of its bankers, it is not undergoing or facing any application for commencement of an insolvency resolution process or liquidation, and the disqualifications specified under Section 29A of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 are not applicable to it. No sale, lease or disposal of assets, and no valuation differences or transfer of key intangible assets, were reported in respect of IDL.

## Strategic Importance

IDL extends Apollo's participation across the defence value chain from the intelligence at the core of a weapon system to the energetics that give it effect — a combination few companies globally possess. This strengthens Apollo's position as a [[concepts/tier_1_defense_oem_integration]] partner and supports its role in [[concepts/defense-program-supply-chains]], reinforcing [[concepts/defense_indigenization]] under the Atmanirbhar Bharat programme.

The acquisition also supports collaborations with organisations such as [[entities/bharat_dynamics_limited]] and [[entities/drdo]] on programmes including [[entities/migm]], and underpins Apollo's ambitions in [[concepts/defense-exports-and-global-market-entry]]. It sits within the Group's wider consolidation of energetics capability, alongside the proposed acquisition of [[entities/premier_explosives_limited]] — part of the Group's wider [[concepts/defence-acquisitions-and-integration]] track record.

## Environmental and Governance Integration

Environmental stewardship of IDL's plant sites covers emissions and effluent management for chemical processes and embedding green design into upcoming greenfield capacity, including the planned energetics complex. A structured environmental governance overlay reviews all consents and licences, such as Pollution Control Board consents to establish and operate, PESO licences and hazardous-waste authorisations, with IDL's environmental compliance reporting integrated into the Group's compliance certification framework.

IDL has been brought into the Group through shared values, governance standards and review rhythms rather than operating as a silo, with safety training expanded to cover explosives handling, process safety and emergency response. Its related-party dealings are governed by the Group's audit committee review and shareholder approval processes, and its acquisition accounting is subject to the Group's [[concepts/internal-financial-controls]] over financial reporting, forming part of the Company's [[concepts/corporate-governance-and-compliance]] architecture. Annexure A to the consolidated auditor's report records an unmodified opinion on the adequacy and operating effectiveness of internal financial controls with reference to the consolidated financial statements.

Following the acquisition, the Group's safety framework extends to the manufacture, storage and transportation of explosives, which are governed by stringent licensing and process-safety requirements under the Explosives Act, 1884 and the Explosives Rules, 2008. IDL's plants operate institutionalised safety management systems, including permit-to-work controls, regular mock drills, statutory inspections and safety audits, and the unit's long safety track record and industry recognition further strengthen the Group's overall safety culture.
