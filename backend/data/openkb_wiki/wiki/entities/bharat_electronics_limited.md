# Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL)

Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL) is an Indian defence electronics organisation, widely known as a Government of India defence public sector undertaking under the Ministry of Defence. It is a major supplier of radar, communication, electronic warfare and other defence electronic systems to the Indian Armed Forces.

## Role in the 2026 Annual Report

BEL appears in the [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part1]] of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] in a single, strategically significant capacity: as the body that **recognised Apollo Micro Systems as an approved Collaborative R&D Partner**.

- The recognition appears in the Annual Report's Innovation section, listed under the Company's technology capabilities alongside its three technology pillars (Radio Frequency Systems, Artificial Intelligence and Inertial Navigation).
- It is also repeated in the Strategic Partnerships section, where Apollo lists "Approved Collaborative R&D Partner for Bharat Electronics Limited" as one of its formal alliance relationships.
- The status is presented as a validation of Apollo's research and development credentials by an established Indian defence electronics organisation.

## Significance

Within the report's framing, the BEL collaboration sits alongside other public-sector alliances — including agreements with [[entities/munitions_india_limited]], [[entities/bharat_dynamics_limited]] and [[entities/grse]], and Apollo's long-standing association with [[entities/drdo]] — as evidence of the Company's embedding in India's domestic defence ecosystem. Such partnerships support [[concepts/transfer-of-technology]] absorption, participation in [[concepts/defense-program-supply-chains]], and Apollo's stated ambition to move toward [[concepts/tier_1_defense_oem_integration]] and global OEM status.
