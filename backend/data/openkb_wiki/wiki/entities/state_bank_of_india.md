# State Bank of India

## Overview

State Bank of India (SBI) is an Indian public sector bank. In the context of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]], SBI acts as a major secured lender, providing both long-term term loans and short-term working capital facilities (overdraft).

## Key Facts from the FY 2025-26 Annual Report (Part 7)

### Term Loan
- Interest rate: **10.00% p.a.**
- Tenure: **27 quarterly instalments from 28.02.2026**
- Outstanding as at 31 March 2026: **₹6,638.35 lakhs**
- Outstanding as at 31 March 2025: **₹2,969.82 lakhs**

This is the largest single bank term loan disclosed in the non-current borrowings note, reflecting a significant increase in drawdowns during the year.

### Working Capital / Current Borrowings
- Nature: **Overdraft** repayable on demand
- Interest rate: **9.90% p.a.**
- Outstanding as at 31 March 2026: **₹12,284.88 lakhs**
- Outstanding as at 31 March 2025: **₹9,255.19 lakhs**

### Security Arrangements
- SBI's term loans are secured by hypothecation of the specific assets created out of the loans financed, along with equitable mortgage of specific properties of the Company, its promoters, and third parties.
- Working capital limits (including Letter of Credit and Bank Guarantee facilities) are secured by hypothecation of inventories, book debts, and all other current assets, ranking **pari passu** with facilities from [[entities/hdfc_bank]], [[entities/bandhan_bank]], and Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs).
- The loans are further secured by **personal guarantees of two Directors** of the Company and others in their individual capacities.
- SBI working capital limits are also secured by **lien over fixed deposits** of the Company.

### Compliance
- There is **no default** as at 31 March 2026 and 31 March 2025 in repayment of loans and interest payments to SBI.
- Funds raised from SBI borrowings were utilised for the purposes for which they were borrowed.
