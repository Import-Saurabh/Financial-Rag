# Bigshare Services Private Limited

Bigshare Services Private Limited is a Registrar and Transfer Agent (RTA) engaged by [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] to handle its shareholder servicing and registry functions. It acts as the intermediary between the company and its shareholders for matters relating to shares held in physical and demat form.

## Role and Contact Details

As disclosed in the Corporate Governance Report of the company's Annual Report for FY 2025-26, Bigshare Services functions as the Registrar and Transfer Agents unit for Apollo Micro Systems Limited:

- **Telephone:** 040 4014 4582
- **Email:** bsshyd@bigshareonline.com
- **Website:** www.bigshareonline.com

## Responsibilities

- Serves as the point of contact for investors holding securities in physical form.
- For securities held in Demat form, investors are directed to write to their concerned Depository Participant(s) or to the Registrar and Transfer Agents.
- Supports the share transfer and reconciliation processes that the company must complete under the [[entities/securities_and_exchange_board_of_india]] (SEBI) Depositories and Participants Regulations, 2018, with quarterly reconciliation certificates submitted to [[entities/bse_limited]] and [[entities/national_stock_exchange_of_india]].

## Context

The appointment of an RTA is a standard requirement of [[concepts/corporate-governance-and-compliance]] for listed entities, ensuring that shareholder grievances, share transfers and registry records are managed independently of the issuer. Bigshare Services' details appear in the "Address for Correspondence" section of the FY 2025-26 report, alongside the company's registered office information.
