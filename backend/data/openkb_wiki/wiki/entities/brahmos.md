# BrahMos Missile System

BrahMos is a supersonic cruise missile system developed by India's [[entities/drdo]] in collaboration with Russia, representing a key platform in India's defense arsenal. The missile is a prominent example of [[concepts/defense_indigenization]] and is central to India's missile program ecosystem.

In the Q1 FY26 earnings call, [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] confirmed that it is supplying a couple of subsystems for BrahMos and recently received an order for these components. Management stated that the company expects further contributions to the BrahMos program, underscoring Apollo's deepening role in [[concepts/defense-program-supply-chains]]. This work aligns with Apollo's broader strategy of indigenizing critical electronic and electromechanical systems for major defense programs. Further details are covered in [[summaries/2025_Aug_2025_Transcript]].
