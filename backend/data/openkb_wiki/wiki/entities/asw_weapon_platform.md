# Anti-Submarine Warfare (ASW) Weapon Platform

## Overview
The Anti-Submarine Warfare (ASW) Weapon Platform is a complete underwater weapon system developed by [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] for engagement of sub-surface threats. It is described in the company's annual report as its first complete weapon system designed entirely in-house — a ground-up indigenous development rather than a reverse-engineering effort.

## Key Facts (FY 2025-26)
- **Developer:** [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] (AMS), Hyderabad.
- **Nature:** First complete weapon system designed entirely in-house by AMS, marking its transition from subsystem supplier to complete weapon platform developer.
- **Intended customer alignment:** Developed in alignment with the operational requirements of the [[entities/indian_navy]].
- **Integrated competencies:** acoustic homing, guidance and control, actuation, warhead integration, and safety-and-arming mechanisms combined into a unified anti-submarine weapon.
- **Progress during FY 2025-26:** significant advances achieved across design, development, and subsystem realisation phases.
- **Strategic role:** anchors AMS's long-term ambition in the underwater warfare domain and its positioning as a system-of-systems integrator.

## Context
This platform sits alongside AMS's wider underwater ordnance portfolio, which includes the [[entities/mini_torpedo]], [[entities/moored_mine]], and [[entities/limpet_mines]]. Together these products position the company as a complete underwater weapon systems house, a domain covered by [[concepts/underwater-warfare]].

The programme reflects the broader push for defence indigenization in Indian defence manufacturing, where private-sector firms are moving from component supply into prime-contractor roles in select verticals.
