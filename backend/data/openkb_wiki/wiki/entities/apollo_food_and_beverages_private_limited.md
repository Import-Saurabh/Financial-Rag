# Apollo Food and Beverages Private Limited

Apollo Food and Beverages Private Limited is a related party of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]]. In the FY2025-26 standalone financial statements it is classified as an **enterprise controlled by Key Management Personnel (KMP)**, while in the FY2025-26 consolidated financial statements it is described as an enterprise in which a **director has significant influence**. It is not a subsidiary or step-down subsidiary of the Company.

## Key facts from the FY2025-26 financial statements

The entity appears in the related party disclosures of both the standalone and consolidated statements:

**Standalone statements (Note 34, [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part8]]):**

| Nature of transaction | FY2025-26 (₹ lakhs) | FY2024-25 (₹ lakhs) |
|---|---|---|
| Advance for supplies received/(repaid) | (450.00) | 450.00 |

**Consolidated statements (Note 35, [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part10]]):**

| Nature of transaction | FY2025-26 (₹ lakhs) | FY2024-25 (₹ lakhs) |
|---|---|---|
| Advance for supplies received/(repaid) | (450.00) | 450.00 |

- The transaction represents an advance for supplies that was received in FY2024-25 and subsequently repaid/adjusted in FY2025-26.
- The outstanding balance with the entity was ₹450.00 lakhs as at 31 March 2025, and nil as at 31 March 2026.
- The Company states that transactions with related parties are made on terms equivalent to those prevailing in arm's length transactions, and outstanding balances at year-end are unsecured and interest free.
