# Pinaka Rocket System

The Pinaka Rocket System is an indigenous multi-barrel rocket launcher developed under India's defense indigenization efforts. It is among the flagship artillery and rocket programmes of the Indian armed forces and forms part of the wider [[concepts/defense_indigenization]] push under the Make in India initiative.

## Apollo Micro Systems' Role

In the Q1 FY26 earnings call, [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] confirmed that it supplies guidance electronics for the Pinaka system to MIL and EEL. Production orders were expected within the next 3–4 months, and export execution was already underway.

The FY 2025-26 Annual Report reaffirms this position: Apollo states that it "remains a trusted partner and supplier for guidance and navigation systems across several programmes, including the Pinaka programme." The programme sits alongside Apollo's broader rocket portfolio, which also includes work on the BM-21 Grad rocket, and is highlighted among the FY27 growth areas spanning armament electronics, fire control systems and artillery platforms.

## Strategic Significance

Apollo's involvement in Pinaka reflects its broader role in [[concepts/defense-program-supply-chains]] and contributes to the company's [[concepts/defense-exports-and-global-market-entry]] strategy as it converts trusted-partner status on domestic programmes into a platform for global markets. It also supports Apollo's positioning as a [[concepts/tier_1_defense_oem_integration]] partner and its growing capability in [[concepts/inertial-navigation-systems]] and guidance technologies.
