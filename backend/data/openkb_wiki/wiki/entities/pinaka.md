# Pinaka Rocket System

The Pinaka Rocket System is an indigenous multi-barrel rocket launcher developed under India's defense indigenization efforts. In the Q1 FY26 earnings call, [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] confirmed that it supplies guidance electronics for the Pinaka system to MIL and EEL. Production orders are expected within the next 3–4 months, and export execution is already underway.

Apollo's involvement in Pinaka reflects its broader role in [[concepts/defense-program-supply-chains]] and contributes to the company's [[concepts/defense-exports-and-global-market-entry]] strategy. The program is also part of the wider [[concepts/defense_indigenization]] push under the Make in India initiative.
