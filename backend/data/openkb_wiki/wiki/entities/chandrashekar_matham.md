## Overview

Dr. Chandrashekar Matham is an Indian defence industrial research and development professional and academic who serves as a **Non-Executive Director** on the board of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] (DIN: 09844975), a position he has held since 21 January 2023. He is profiled as part of the board of directors in the company's [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part1|Annual Report 2025-26]], and his remuneration and outstanding balances are disclosed among the company's related party transactions in [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part8|Part 8 of the Annual Report 2025-26]] (standalone financial statements) and confirmed in [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part10|Part 10]] (consolidated financial statements).

## Education

- **Ph.D. in VLSI and Embedded Systems** from SK University, Anantapur, awarded in 2009.
- **M.Tech. in Electronics and Instrumentation Engineering (EIE)** from the Indian Institute of Science (I.I.Sc.), Bangalore, in 1994.
- **M.Sc. (Tech.) in Applied Electronics**, a three-year integrated programme, from Osmania University in 1986.

## Career

Dr. Matham brings over two decades of experience in **defence industrial research and development**, alongside more than a decade of teaching undergraduate and postgraduate engineering students at both Central and State Universities.

- **Senior Manager, R&D at [[entities/bharat_dynamics_limited|Bharat Dynamics]]**, Hyderabad, under the Ministry of Defence, Government of India. In this role he played a pivotal role in the indigenous design and development of **Embedded Test Systems for missile technology**.
- **Lecturer in Electronics** at SSGM College of Engineering, Shegaon, from 1987 to 1993, where he spent seven years training engineering students.

His combination of industry and academic experience has established him as a respected figure in the field of defence electronics and embedded systems.

## Role at Apollo Micro Systems

Dr. Matham sits on the Board of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] as a **Non-Executive Director**, contributing defence R&D expertise as the company positions itself as a Tier-I defence OEM and expands into weapon platforms and systems. His directorship sits within the company's framework of [[concepts/corporate-governance-and-compliance|corporate governance and compliance]].

During financial year 2025-26 the Board met six times (23 May 2025, 28 May 2025, 28 July 2025, 04 November 2025, 19 November 2025 and 09 February 2026), alongside the 28th Annual General Meeting held on 16 September 2025, and Dr. Matham participated in these proceedings. He also attended the separate meeting of Independent Directors held on 26 March 2026, which reviewed the performance of the Board and Committees, the performance of the Chairperson, and the quality and timeliness of information flow between management and the Board.

He is not a member of any of the Company's Board committees (Audit, Nomination & Remuneration, Stakeholders Relationship, Corporate Social Responsibility, Risk Management, Executive, or Securities Allotment). In the Board's skills matrix he is noted for **corporate governance and management** competencies.

### Other Directorships

Outside Apollo, Dr. Matham holds one directorship in another public limited company — [[entities/idl_explosives|IDL Explosives Limited]], a step-down subsidiary of Apollo Micro Systems (100% ownership interest, incorporated in India, per the group structure disclosed in Note 37 of the consolidated financial statements). He holds no chairpersonships or memberships of Audit or Stakeholders Relationship Committees in other public limited companies.

### Remuneration and Related Party Disclosures

As a Non-Executive Director, Dr. Matham draws no salary, commission, stock options or sweat equity. He receives only sitting fees of ₹20,000 per day for attending Board and Committee meetings and reimbursement of conveyance, travelling and other expenses.

Under the company's [[concepts/related-party-transactions|related party disclosures]] — presented in both the standalone financial statements (year ended 31 March 2026) and the consolidated financial statements notes for FY 2025-26:

- He is listed among the Group's related parties as a **Non-Executive Director**, identified per Ind AS 24.
- **Sitting fees**: ₹1.20 Lakhs for FY 2025-26, compared with ₹1.60 Lakhs for FY 2024-25.
- **Outstanding balance**: remuneration payable of ₹0.18 Lakhs as at 31 March 2026, down from ₹1.44 Lakhs as at 31 March 2025.
- He received **no dividend** from the Company during FY 2025-26 or FY 2024-25.

The related party disclosures state that transactions with related parties are made on terms equivalent to those in arm's length transactions, and that outstanding balances at year-end are unsecured and interest free (except for loans to subsidiary and step-down subsidiary companies). There is no relationship between him and any other Director of the Company.
