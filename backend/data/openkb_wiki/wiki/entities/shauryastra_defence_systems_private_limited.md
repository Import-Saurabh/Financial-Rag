# Shauryastra Defence Systems Private Limited

**Type:** Organization (Associate Company)

## Overview

Shauryastra Defence Systems Private Limited (SDSPL) is an Indian defence technology company in which [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] holds a **34% equity stake**, making it an Associate Company of the Apollo Micro Systems group. It appears in the company's register of related parties as an Associate Company, alongside fellow group entities [[entities/apollo_defence_industries_private_limited]], [[entities/apollo_strategic_technologies_private_limited]], [[entities/ananya_sip_rf_technologies_private_limited]], and [[entities/idl_explosives]].

SDSPL has been incorporated as a dedicated technology and development entity with a mandate to undertake the research, design and development of complete weapon systems, long-range systems and allied defence platforms for the Indian Armed Forces. Its stated purpose is to pursue unsolved and futuristic operational problem statements of the Armed Forces, leveraging Apollo Micro Systems' established design, development, manufacturing and integration capabilities.

## Leadership

The entity is guided under the leadership of **Dr. V. K. Saraswat**, former Secretary of the Department of Defence R&D and Director General of the [[entities/drdo]]. This leadership positions SDSPL as a platform for long-horizon, indigenous weapon-system development aligned with national defence priorities, connecting it to themes such as [[concepts/tier_1_defense_oem_integration]], [[concepts/defense_indigenization]] and [[concepts/transfer-of-technology]].

## Relationship with Apollo Micro Systems

- **Shareholding held by Apollo Micro Systems:** 34%
- **Relationship class:** Associate Company (not a subsidiary)
- [[entities/karunakar_reddy_baddam]], Managing Director of Apollo Micro Systems, is a Director of SDSPL and is deemed to be interested in transactions with it.
- [[entities/krishna_sai_kumar_addepalli]] is also listed among the Apollo Micro Systems directors/KMP with an interest in the SDSPL transactions.
- SDSPL holds **nil** shareholding in Apollo Micro Systems, and no default has been reported by SDSPL on any obligation undertaken under a transaction or arrangement with the company.
- Transactions with SDSPL during FY 2025-26 were **Nil**, and none had been undertaken in the current financial year up to the quarter preceding the approval being sought.

## Section 185 Support Resolution (29th AGM)

At the 29th Annual General Meeting, shareholders were asked to approve, by **Special Resolution** under Section 185 of the Companies Act, 2013, the giving of loans and/or guarantees and/or security in connection with loans taken or to be taken by SDSPL, up to a sum **not exceeding ₹200 crores** at any point in time. The board noted that such support may be extended for SDSPL's capital expenditure and/or working capital requirements, including the purchase of fixed assets for business expansion. Funds would be deployed out of internal resources/accruals or other appropriate sources, and any loan would carry interest at a rate agreed between the parties in the best interest of the company. The board recommended the resolution for approval, with Mr. Baddam Karunakar Reddy and his relatives deemed concerned or interested.

## Strategic Significance

SDSPL represents Apollo Micro Systems' move up the value chain toward complete weapon-system development, complementing AMS's component- and subsystem-level capabilities in defence electronics, [[concepts/counter-drone-systems]] and [[concepts/directed-energy-weapons]]. Its mandate over long-range systems connects it to the broader [[concepts/defense-program-supply-chains]] and [[concepts/government-defence-funding-schemes]] ecosystem shaping Indian defence procurement.
