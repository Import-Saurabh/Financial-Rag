# Gunneswara Rao

**Role:** Chief Financial Officer, [[entities/mtar-technologies]]
**Type:** Person (executive)

## Overview

Gunneswara Rao is the Chief Financial Officer of MTAR Technologies Limited and the management's principal voice on financial performance, working capital, capex and balance-sheet matters. He appears alongside Managing Director [[entities/srinivas-reddy]] and Head of Strategy & IR [[entities/srilekha-jasthi]] on the company's earnings calls. His name is rendered in the Q1 FY27 transcript both as "Gunneswara Rao" (management list and CFO section) and "Gunneshwara Rao" (the IR opening remarks).

On the Q1 FY27 earnings call held 30 July 2026, he presented the consolidated results and then fielded analyst questions on working capital, capex phasing and funding, debt, interest cost and the composition of the nuclear order book.

## Q1 FY27 Financial Performance (as presented)

Reported on a consolidated basis for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 — see [[concepts/consolidated-financial-statements]] and [[summaries/2026_Aug_2026_Transcript]]:

- Revenue from operations of INR360.7 crore versus INR156.6 crore in Q1 FY26 (+130.4%), described as the highest-ever quarterly revenue.
- EBITDA of INR85.1 crore versus INR28.4 crore (+199.7%), a margin of 23.54% against a full-year guidance of 24%, and up from 20.11% in the prior quarter on operating leverage.
- PBT of INR67.4 crore versus INR14.8 crore (+355%); PAT of INR50.2 crore versus INR10.8 crore (+364.5%), a PAT margin of 13.92% against 6.9%.
- Gross margin of 45.61% versus 47.65% a year earlier, which he attributed to revenue mix while noting continuous monitoring for improvement.
- Cash flow from operations of INR247.69 crore versus INR191.66 crore.
- ROCE of 17.2% versus 11.4%, with an expectation of reaching about 23% next year — see [[concepts/financial-ratios]].

He linked the margin trajectory and the PAT step-up to the shift of products from first-article development into production ramp-up, noting that customers in aerospace were ordering 10–15 times the initial volumes.

## Working Capital

The most striking disclosure of his section was the compression in working capital days, which he covered again in response to analyst questions:

- Working capital days of **59 days** for Q1 FY27, against 172 days for the whole of FY26.
- Inventory days down from 208 to 145 and receivable days down from 140 to 82.
- FY27 target revised sharply downward to about **100 days**, from an earlier guidance band of 150–175 days.
- Levers cited: renegotiated commercial terms and credit periods with both existing and new customers, credit terms measured from arrival at the customer's premises, faster delivery so cash is received sooner, daily and weekly monitoring of each working-capital element, and a targeted ~**INR70 crore per year of GST refunds**.
- He noted inorganic working-capital solutions were possible but rejected where they would come at the cost of margins.

## Capex and Capital Allocation

- Total capex of about **INR500 crore** required across FY27 and FY28 for the expansion programmes across verticals, with roughly a **70:30 split** between clean energy and other segments, and fungibility of some clean-energy assets into other sectors.
- Q1 FY27 capex spend of only about **INR30–35 crore**; INR80 crore of capital work-in-progress was capitalised during the quarter, moving into fixed assets — see [[concepts/capital-work-in-progress]] and [[concepts/property-plant-and-equipment]].
- Asset-turnover discipline: a minimum of **4–5x** asset turnover overall and at least **6x** on incremental capex.
- Funding to be a blend of internal accruals and debt; funding structure and prudence framed under [[concepts/capital-management]].

## Balance Sheet and Debt

- Gross debt of **INR423.6 crore** as of 30 June 2026, offset by **INR379 crore** of investments as of July-end, leaving effective net debt of only about INR20–30 crore.
- Interest cost of about **INR16 crore** for the quarter, expected to decline quarter-on-quarter; he deferred providing the fund-based versus non-fund-based split to a later follow-up.
- Characterised the balance sheet as very strong on both debt and working capital.

## Order Book Clarification

He corrected a participant's reading of the day's order announcement, clarifying that the incremental inflow announced on the call was **INR800 crore** on top of the ~INR5,100 crore closing order book — not an additional INR3,100 crore — taking the total to over INR5,900 crore as of the call date. See [[concepts/order-book-and-revenue-visibility]].

On the nuclear segment specifically, he quantified INR684 crore of orders already booked plus about INR130 crore expected in the current quarter, for roughly **INR815 crore** — consistent with the MD's reference to ~INR800 crore — and expressed confidence in winning that inflow. See [[concepts/civil-nuclear-power]].
