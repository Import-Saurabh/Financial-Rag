Kavya Gorla is a Non-Executive Non-Independent Director on the Board of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] (DIN 06407238, appointed 13 October 2022), as profiled across the company's [[summaries/2026_Annual_Report_2026_part1|Annual Report 2025-26]] and its statutory annexures.

## Background

- Holds a graduate degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering, combining technical training with a deep interest in aviation.
- Driven by her passion for airplanes, she pursued flight training in Florida, USA, and has been a licensed pilot since 2007.
- Developed expertise in airplane aerodynamics, engines, avionics and radio navigation systems.

## Aviation Career

- Served as a commercial pilot with several Indian airlines, including Air Deccan, Kingfisher Airlines and Jet Airways.
- Her last flight assignment was with Jet Airways India Limited.
- Since leaving active flying, she has channelled her experience into entrepreneurial ventures focused on the manufacture of heavy engineering applications across sectors such as mining, automobile, oil and gas, and electrical infrastructure.

## Role at Apollo Micro Systems

- Serves as a Non-Executive Non-Independent Director on the Board, alongside fellow non-executive and independent directors such as [[entities/aditya_kumar_halwasiya]], [[entities/karunasree_samudrala]] and [[entities/chandrashekar_matham]].
- The Board is chaired by [[entities/raghupathy_goud_theegala]] and includes executive leadership such as Founder and Managing Director [[entities/karunakar_reddy_baddam]], Whole-Time Director (Technical) [[entities/venkata_siva_prasad_chandrapati]] and Whole-Time Director (Operations) [[entities/krishna_sai_kumar_addepalli]].
- She holds no directorship in any other public limited company, allowing her to focus her board attention on Apollo Micro Systems.

### Committee Memberships

- **Nomination and Remuneration Committee** – member; the committee met once during FY 2025-26 (28 July 2025) and is responsible for director qualifications, remuneration policy, board diversity, and performance evaluation.
- **Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Committee** – member; the committee met twice during FY 2025-26 (23 May 2025 and 23 March 2026) and oversees the CSR policy, expenditure and monitoring, tying into the company's [[concepts/corporate-social-responsibility]] agenda.

### Board and Governance Framework

- The Board met six times during FY 2025-26 (23 May 2025, 28 May 2025, 28 July 2025, 4 November 2025, 19 November 2025 and 9 February 2026), with the maximum interval between meetings not exceeding 120 days.
- She works within a governance framework that includes the Audit, Nomination and Remuneration, Stakeholder Relationship, Risk Management, Corporate Social Responsibility, Executive and Securities Allotment committees, reflecting the company's broader [[concepts/corporate-governance-and-compliance]] practices.
- As a Non-Executive Director she does not draw remuneration from the Company; no remuneration was paid to her during FY 2025-26 and no stock options were granted to her.
- The 28th Annual General Meeting of the Company was held on 16 September 2025.

## Relevance

Her appointment contributes to board diversity and brings engineering, aviation and heavy-engineering manufacturing perspectives to a company positioning itself as a [[concepts/tier_1_defense_oem_integration|Tier-I defence OEM]] engaged in [[concepts/defense_indigenization|defence indigenisation]] across land, air and sea domains.
