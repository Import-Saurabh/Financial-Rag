# RevX Capital Fund-II

**RevX Capital Fund-II** is a capital fund that served as a lender to [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] through a subscription to unlisted, fully paid redeemable non-convertible debentures.

## Role and Relationship

RevX Capital Fund-II is the sole identified holder of the Company's Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs). The investment represents a debt financing arrangement rather than an equity stake — the fund has no shareholding in the Company.

## Key Facts (from FY 2025-26)

- **Instrument**: 15% Non-Convertible Debentures issued to RevX Capital Fund-II, at a face value of ₹4,000 lakhs.
- **Coupon rate**: 15.00% per annum.
- **Date of issue**: 29 March 2025.
- **Repayment terms**: Repayable on a monthly basis, with the final repayment dated 31 March 2028 (36 months from the date of issue).
- **Outstanding balance**:
  - As at 31 March 2025: ₹3,155.35 lakhs (of which ₹715.01 lakhs was repayable within one year and classified under current maturities of long-term borrowings).
  - As at 31 March 2026: **Nil** — the debentures were fully repaid/redeemed during FY 2025-26.
- **Presentation**: Classified within non-current borrowings (unlisted, fully paid redeemable non-convertible debentures) in the year in which the balance was outstanding.
- **Interest expense**: Interest on debentures recognised in finance costs was ₹744.78 lakhs for FY 2025-26, compared with ₹7.19 lakhs in FY 2024-25, reflecting a full year of the 15% coupon.

## Reconciliation Context

Within the non-current borrowings reconciliation, the opening balance of ₹6,976.48 lakhs (31 March 2025) was increased by ₹17,098.13 lakhs on account of business combinations and ₹4,957.48 lakhs of fresh borrowings, and reduced by ₹17,106.12 lakhs of repayments, bringing the closing balance to ₹11,925.97 lakhs at 31 March 2026. The extinguishment of the RevX Capital Fund-II debenture balance forms part of that repayment movement. Notably, the company reported no default in repayment of loans and interest payments as at 31 March 2026 and 31 March 2025.
