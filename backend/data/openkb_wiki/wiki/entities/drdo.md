# Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO)

The **Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO)** is the premier research and development agency under the Government of India's Ministry of Defence, responsible for developing defense technologies and systems for the Indian Armed Forces. It is central to India's push for self-reliance in defense manufacturing.

## Role in Apollo Micro Systems' Business

In the Q1 FY26 earnings call (see [[summaries/2025_Aug_2025_Transcript]]), DRDO is the key counterparty and technology originator behind most of **Apollo Micro Systems Limited**'s major defense programs. Apollo's management described the company as contributing around **63% of the electronics and electromechanical work for DRDO missile programs**.

Apollo describes a **four-decade-long association with DRDO** that has established a strong legacy in the development and manufacturing of advanced weapon system platforms, and cites itself as the industry participant with the **highest participation in DRDO's indigenous missile programmes**. It is also an approved vendor for DRDO and DPSUs.

Key programs involving DRDO mentioned across Apollo's disclosures:

- **Project Kusha** — Apollo is involved in onboard systems and is the only industry supplier to have delivered systems; trials are planned within months.
- **MIGM (Multi-Influence Ground Mine, "Vighna")** — India's first indigenous naval mine of its class, developed under the **Development cum Production Partner (DcPP)** model with DRDO. Apollo and [[entities/bharat_dynamics_limited]] are development-cum-production partners; technology transfer documentation is being completed with the formal order expected during the financial year. Apollo is the approved production agency for MIGM.
- **QRSAM** — Apollo supplies four critical subsystems; orders are expected after [[entities/bharat_dynamics_limited]] receives its order.
- **Moored mines / deep sea mines** — Apollo developed the system and offered it to DRDO, expecting a significant share of the DAC-approved order. The Company expects to become a DcPP partner for other mine variants under DRDO.
- **Torpedo programs** — EHWT, ALWT, and Varunastra, with Apollo supplying homing systems and signal processing. Torpedo homing capabilities were built through years of joint development with DRDO laboratories and the [[entities/indian_navy]].
- **Pinaka and Grad rockets** — Apollo remains a trusted partner and supplier of guidance and navigation systems across several programmes, including Pinaka, and has made strong progress on rocket programmes including the BM-21 Grad rocket.
- **Broader participation** — Apollo participates in **60+ programmes of DcPP as a sub-system partner** and, as a trusted partner of DRDO, plays a key role in various active defence programmes.

DRDO also features in the company's acquisition strategy: consultants, including ex-DRDO scientists, are working with [[entities/idl_explosives]] on technology transfer and explosives manufacturing for missiles and MIGM.

## Technology Transfer and Industrialisation

Apollo frames its role as converting DRDO **[[concepts/transfer-of-technology]]** into deployable, inductable systems. This requires building the complete industrialisation chain around the transferred design, which Apollo has been investing in across five areas: design-for-manufacture and design-for-test practices; qualified manufacturing and test infrastructure (environmental stress screening, EMI/EMC, vibration, shock and climatic testing); a defence-grade supply chain with indigenous alternatives for critical items; quality and certification systems aligned to defence quality assurance agencies (DGQA/DGAQA/DGNAI); and lifecycle support covering depot-level maintenance, spares, obsolescence management and mid-life upgrades. Engineers absorbed these transferred technologies on the job, working alongside DRDO laboratories and customer teams.

At the national level, the report notes a **privatisation drive** in which India is undertaking the complete transfer of DRDO-developed conventional missile technology — covering air-to-air, anti-ship and tactical ballistic missiles — to private companies through competitive bidding, designed to scale affordable mass production capacity and widen private participation in India's missile ecosystem. DRDO is expected to concentrate on research and advanced technology development while private players take on manufacturing at scale.

DRDO's technological capability was also credited in the calibrated military response during **Operation Sindoor**, in which air defence systems detected, tracked and neutralised threats using radars, control centres, artillery, drones and air- and ground-based missiles.

## Connection to Broader Themes

The relationship with DRDO is a core driver of the [[concepts/defense-program-supply-chains]] that generate Apollo's order book. It also exemplifies [[concepts/defense_indigenization]] and the Make in India initiative, as Apollo works with DRDO to localize critical defense subsystems and reduce import dependence. The company's export ambitions, described under [[concepts/defense-exports-and-global-market-entry]], build on the domestically developed technologies and qualifications earned through these DRDO collaborations, and the depth of its DRDO-qualified portfolio supports its positioning as a [[concepts/tier_1_defense_oem_integration]] and emerging platform OEM.
