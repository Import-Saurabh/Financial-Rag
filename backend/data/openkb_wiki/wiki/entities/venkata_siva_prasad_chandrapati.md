## Overview

**Venkata Siva Prasad Chandrapati** (DIN: 03601703) is the Whole-Time Director (Technical) of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]], the Hyderabad-headquartered defence technology company. He is the technical architect behind the company's research and development organisation and its weapon system electronics portfolio.

## Background and Qualifications

- Holds an **M.Tech in Digital Signal Processing**.
- Brings over **26 years of expertise** across multiple technologies.
- Joined Apollo Micro Systems in **1999**, after more than eight years of experience with other companies.
- His design and product development proficiency is described in the Annual Report as making him a top choice in the industry.
- Appointed to his current term as Whole-Time Director on **22-08-2022**.
- He holds **no directorship in any other public limited company**.

## Role and Contributions

The Annual Report 2025-26 credits Chandrapati as the driving force behind the company's R&D teams, particularly in leading and executing key defence projects.

- **Weapon system electronics**: He is described as the visionary behind the development of sophisticated weapon system electronics, contributing significantly to numerous indigenous defence programmes.
- **Space systems**: His deep understanding of space systems was instrumental in creating advanced Payload Checkout and Telemetry Systems for critical space programmes — an early capability of the company, whose first indigenous Payload Checkout System was supplied to [[entities/isro]] in 2000.
- **Technical leadership**: His knowledge and managerial abilities are cited as the driving force behind the company's R&D teams.

## Board Committees

The Corporate Governance Report (Annexure H of the FY26 report) records his committee roles and meeting attendance for the year:

- **Risk Management Committee** — Chairman (attended 02 meetings).
- **Stakeholders Relationship Committee** — Member (attended 02 meetings).
- **Executive Committee of Directors & CFO** — Member (attended 04 meetings).

He also attends the company's Board meetings as Executive Director, and his competence is mapped in the Board's skills matrix across corporate governance, business leadership, and understanding of the company's business and technology development.

## Remuneration

For the financial year 2025-26, his remuneration was **₹24.00 Lakhs** (gross salary), against **₹23.93 Lakhs** in the previous year. His remuneration-to-median-remuneration ratio was **8.49**. No stock options, sweat equity, commission, or provident fund contributions were paid to him beyond salary. Non-Executive and Independent Directors receive only sitting fees.

The related party disclosures (Note 35 of the consolidated financial statements) confirm his classification as **Key Management Personnel (Whole-Time Director)** and record the following balances and transactions, which are made on arm's length terms:

- **Compensation (short-term employee benefits)**: ₹24.00 Lakhs for FY 2025-26 (FY 2024-25: ₹23.93 Lakhs).
- **Dividend received**: ₹1.98 Lakhs for FY 2025-26 (FY 2024-25: ₹0.40 Lakhs), reflecting his shareholding in the company.
- **Remuneration payable (outstanding at year end)**: ₹1.74 Lakhs as at 31 March 2026 (₹1.55 Lakhs as at 31 March 2025).

## Context

His technical remit aligns with the company's stated transformation from an electronics supplier to a multi-domain systems house. The FY26 report notes the R&D team delivered zero attrition and that R&D spending reached 8% of revenue, supporting outcomes such as the Mini Torpedo, sensor suites for underwater autonomous vehicles, and FOG-based inertial navigation systems — areas consistent with the three technology pillars of radio frequency systems, artificial intelligence and [[concepts/inertial-navigation-systems]].
