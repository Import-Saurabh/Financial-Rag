# Munitions India Limited

**Type:** Organization — Defence Public Sector Undertaking (DPSU), Government of India

Munitions India Limited (MIL) is an Indian defence public sector undertaking in the ammunition and munitions segment. Within the wiki's current sources it appears as a strategic MoU partner of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]], reflecting the company's strategy of pairing in-house capability with public-sector partnerships across the ammunition value chain.

## Key Facts from Apollo's FY2025-26 Annual Report

- Apollo Micro Systems **signed an MoU with Munitions India Ltd for identified select opportunities**. The MoU is listed among Apollo's strategic partnerships for the year, alongside alliances with [[entities/bharat_electronics_limited]], [[entities/bharat_dynamics_limited]], [[entities/grse]], the [[entities/indian_navy]] and [[entities/iit_madras]].
- The partnership sits within Apollo's stated strategic rationale for alliances: access to un-penetrated markets, brand equity, an end-to-end one-stop solution across the value chain, the ability to pursue large-scale high-value projects, and an expanded addressable market through new products.
- It is one of several moves through which Apollo is positioning itself as a complete weapon-system and munitions supplier — a direction reinforced by its DPIIT Lifetime Arms Manufacturing Licence, the acquisition of [[entities/idl_explosives]], and its entry into energetics.
- The report does not disclose the specific programmes, product categories or contract values covered by the MoU.

## Context and Significance

MIL's role in Apollo's ecosystem is as a partner in the munitions domain, which complements Apollo's activities in [[concepts/defence-energetics]], fuzes and Safety & Arming mechanisms, and platform-level weapons integration. Partnerships of this kind are a recurring instrument in India's defence industrial policy, which emphasises indigenous design, development and manufacture under [[concepts/defense_indigenization]] and the Atmanirbhar Bharat programme.

For a Tier-I supplier transitioning toward OEM status — see [[concepts/tier_1_defense_oem_integration]] — collaborating with established public-sector ammunition producers provides manufacturing depth and credibility in a segment with very high qualification barriers. It also supports the resilience of [[concepts/defense-program-supply-chains]] that Apollo depends on for delivery to the Armed Forces and DPSUs.
