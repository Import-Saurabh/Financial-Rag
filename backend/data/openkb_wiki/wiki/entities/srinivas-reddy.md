# Srinivas Reddy

**Role:** Managing Director and Promoter, [[entities/mtar-technologies]]
**Spokesperson on:** Q1 FY27 earnings conference call, 30 July 2026

## Who He Is

Srinivas Reddy is the Managing Director and Promoter of MTAR Technologies Limited, the precision-engineering company serving India's civil nuclear, aerospace and defense, and clean energy sectors. He is the principal voice on the company's investor calls, opening with a strategic narrative before handing over to the CFO for the financial detail.

On the Q1 FY27 call he was joined by [[entities/gunneswara-rao]] (Chief Financial Officer) and [[entities/srilekha-jasthi]] (Head, Strategy & IR), with the call convened by Vidhi Vasa of MUFG and supported by Orient Capital as investor relations partners. The transcript was filed with the exchanges on 6 August 2026 by [[entities/priyanka-agarwal]], Company Secretary & Compliance Officer.

## Position Articulated on the Q1 FY27 Call

Reddy framed the company as sitting at an **inflection point**, with each of its key verticals positioned for the next phase of growth simultaneously. His argument: MTAR invested in differentiated capabilities "well ahead of broader market adoption" over five decades, and those capabilities are now converting into orders as the underlying sectors accelerate.

### On Civil Nuclear Power
- Described the quarter as delivering the **highest-ever order inflows** in the nuclear segment, anchored by Kaiga 5 & 6.
- Highlighted MTAR's contribution to the **PFBR** programme, which recently achieved criticality — the company supplied the majority of critical assemblies for the reactor, working directly with the government (IGCAR/Bhavini) rather than through EPC vendors. See [[entities/pfbr]].
- Positioned **Mahi Banswara** (four 700 MW reactors, NTPC–NPCIL) as a larger opportunity than Kaiga 5 & 6 given double the reactor count, but reached through EPC vendors; tenders are floated and orders are expected in the next financial year. See [[entities/mahi-banswara]], [[entities/npcil]].
- Claimed a 60–70% wallet share of NPCIL/Department of Atomic Energy reactor orders and tied the long-term case to the Government of India's 100 GW-by-2047 target. See [[concepts/civil-nuclear-power]].
- Emphasised that the vertical has **reduced cyclicality** versus 5–10 years ago, with execution commencing in the second half of FY27.

### On Clean Energy
- Confirmed the three-phase fuel-cell capacity build: Phase 1 commissioned, Phase 2 by September–October 2026, and a **multifold** Phase 3 commissioned by March 2027 with ramp-up from April 2027.
- Repeatedly declined to quantify Phase 3, citing a signed NDA with the customer.
- Stressed that expansion is not only physical — manpower training and automation are being run ahead of capacity coming on stream.
- Pointed to the new products run-rate (~INR100 crore in the quarter), ball screws with export demand, and a major MNC aerospace ball-screw contract as evidence of broadening within the segment. See [[concepts/clean-energy-and-fuel-cells]].

### On Aerospace & Defense
- Guided to a **doubling of segment revenue in FY27**, with a significant ramp-up from that base over 3–4 years, driven largely by existing qualified programmes rather than new wins.
- Cited the actuator assembly programme for [[entities/lca-tejas]] Mark-1A (INR140–150 crore), wing kits, and electromechanical actuators, against a domestic opportunity exceeding INR250 crore.
- Noted that completed first articles are now converting into **10–15x volumes** in aerospace, crediting a 1.5–2 year qualification effort and the pace of building customer-specific and NADCAP-approved special-process facilities. See [[concepts/defense-program-supply-chains]], defence indigenization.
- FY30 aspiration: aerospace at INR600–700 crore and the products business above INR1,000 crore.

### On New Verticals
- Announced entry into **data center infrastructure solutions**: a INR45 crore first order (export-only) to be executed by February–March 2027, against a customer requirement eight times that size on a repeating annual basis, with a dedicated facility being set up.
- Confronted with questions about US data-center capex slippage, he rejected the concern bluntly as "unwanted noise."
- Noted the oil & gas facility would be operational by October 2026, with first articles already delivered.

### On Order Book and Execution
- Reported an order book of INR5,143 crore at end-Q1 FY27 plus a further INR800 crore announced the same day — ahead of the INR5,000 crore closing target he had set a quarter earlier.
- Characterised the book as **short-cycle** (2 months to 2 years for most tranches), arguing that continuous inflows rather than a long-dated backlog drive revenue. See [[concepts/order-book-and-revenue-visibility]].
- Reiterated guidance of 80% revenue growth with a 24% ±100 bps EBITDA margin, while stating confidence in **beating** it — a formulation he returned to when pressed on whether the guidance would be upgraded.

## Management Style as Observed

Reddy's answers are directional and conviction-led rather than numerically granular: he repeatedly deflected requests for precise capacity figures, market share percentages, and per-platform content values, preferring qualitative framing ("massive," "multifold," "going to grow more and more") and deferring quantification to the CFO or to future disclosures. He anchors the equity story in **capability built ahead of demand** and in execution discipline — timely delivery, working capital, prudent capital allocation, and improving margins — rather than in demand forecasting. See [[concepts/capital-management]].
