# Indian Overseas Bank

Indian Overseas Bank is an Indian public sector bank that appears in the consolidated financial statements of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] as one of its secured lenders. It is a bank, not a group company or related party.

## Role in the FY 2025-26 Financial Statements

The bank is disclosed in Note 15 (Borrowings) of the consolidated financial statements as a provider of secured non-current term loans to Apollo Micro Systems Limited. Three separate Indian Overseas Bank facilities are listed:

| Facility | Interest rate p.a. | Tenure | Outstanding as at 31 March 2026 (₹ lakhs) | Outstanding as at 31 March 2025 (₹ lakhs) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| General term loan | 9.05% | 84 instalments | 12.36 | 14.91 |
| Loan taken for R&D expenditure | 13.10% | 36 instalments | 15.65 | 157.49 |
| Loan taken for building | 10.25% | 108 instalments | 242.82 | 250.41 |

The R&D loan carried the highest interest rate (13.10%) among the bank term loans disclosed, though its outstanding balance fell sharply year-on-year.

## Security and Guarantees

The term loans are secured by hypothecation of the specific assets created out of the loans financed by the bank, along with equitable mortgage of specific properties of the Company, its promoters and third parties. They are further secured by the personal guarantees of two Directors of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] and others in their individual capacities. The loans are repayable as per the respective agreements.

The Company reported no default in repayment of loans or interest to Indian Overseas Bank, or to any lender, as at 31 March 2026 and 31 March 2025.

## Context

Indian Overseas Bank sits alongside the Group's other banking lenders, including [[entities/state_bank_of_india]], [[entities/hdfc_bank]], [[entities/bandhan_bank]] and [[entities/bajaj_finance_limited]]. Together these facilities form part of the Group's secured borrowing profile disclosed under [[concepts/consolidated-financial-statements]] and reflected in the movement of [[concepts/leases]] and other financing liabilities. The loans are among the assets and obligations tied to the Group's [[concepts/property-plant-and-equipment]].
