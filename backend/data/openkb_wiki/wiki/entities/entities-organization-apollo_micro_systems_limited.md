# Apollo Micro Systems Limited

[[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]] is an Indian defence and aerospace technology company founded in 1985 and headquartered in Hyderabad. It specializes in delivering mission-critical electronic and electromechanical solutions for missile systems, naval platforms, avionics, space systems, and homeland security. The company is scaling rapidly as a development-cum-production partner in major Indian defence programs and is advancing toward becoming a fully integrated Tier-1 defence OEM.

## Key Facts

- **Transformational Growth:** Reported a strong FY 2024-25 with consolidated revenue of ₹562.07 crore (up 51.24%), and sustained momentum in Q1 FY26 with revenue of ₹234 crore (up 46% YoY), EBITDA of ₹41 crore (up 83%, margin 31%), and PAT of ₹18 crore (more than doubled, margin 13%).
- **Maiden Export Order:** Secured its first export order worth $13.37 million (~₹113.81 crore) for an advanced avionics system for dual civil and military aircraft platforms, marking a strategic entry into global defence markets.
- **Strategic Acquisitions:** Advancing the acquisition of [[entities/idl_explosives]] through its subsidiary [[entities/apollo_defence_industries_private_limited]]; formalities are nearing completion, with ex-DRDO scientists supporting technology transfer and explosives manufacturing for missiles and MIGM.
- **Defence Programme Participation:** Involved as a subsystem partner in over 60 Defence Capital Procurement Programmes, including MIGM, QRSAM, Project Kusha, BrahMos, Pinaka, and torpedo programmes; contributes around 63% of electronics and electromechanical work for DRDO missile programmes, with a current order book of ~₹735 crore.
- **Financial Strength & Funding:** Credit rating upgraded by ACUITE to A-/A2+; received in-principle approval for preferential equity listing; targeting 45–50% revenue CAGR over FY26–27 and a 100–120 day reduction in working capital cycle from FY27.
- **Strategic Partnerships:** Collaborates with [[entities/bharat_dynamics_limited]], [[entities/drdo]], BEL, GRSE, and other public sector enterprises, aligning closely with national indigenization initiatives.
