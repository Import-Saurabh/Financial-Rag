# National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE)

## Overview
The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) is one of India's principal stock exchanges. In the context of [[entities/apollo_micro_systems_limited]], the NSE is one of the two exchanges on which the company's equity shares are listed, alongside [[entities/bse_limited]].

## Key Facts from the 2026 Annual Report (Part 5)

### Listing and regulatory interaction
- Apollo Micro Systems Limited's shares are listed on the NSE and [[entities/bse_limited]], and the company is subject to the [[concepts/corporate-governance-and-compliance]] framework of [[entities/securities_and_exchange_board_of_india]] (SEBI), including the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.
- Disclosures such as the credit rating action — an **ACUITE A-** long-term rating with a "Stable" outlook and a reaffirmed **ACUITE A2+** short-term rating from [[entities/acuite_rating_and_research_limited]] — were intimated to the stock exchanges under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.
- Under Regulation 40(9) and the SEBI (Depositories and Participants) Regulations, 2018, certificates reconciling shares held in NSDL and CDSL and in physical form against the total issued/paid-up capital are submitted to the BSE and NSE within 30 days of the end of each quarter by a Company Secretary-in-Practice.

### Penalty imposed on Apollo Micro Systems
- The NSE imposed a fine of **₹88,500** on the company for a compliance lapse under **Regulation 33 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015**: by oversight, the company submitted Limited Review Reports instead of the Statutory Auditor's Reports for both the Standalone and Consolidated Financial Results for the year ended 31 March 2025, filed along with the financial results on 23 May 2025.
- This was the only capital-markets related penalty disclosed for the last three years, alongside a separate MCA compounding fee for delayed filing of Form CRA-4. Apollo Micro Systems states it has otherwise complied with the requirements of the stock exchanges (NSE & BSE), SEBI and other statutory authorities on all capital-markets matters.
